# 214. Diskusi dan Debat Terstruktur

## Definisi & Target
Diskusi adalah pertukaran ide; debat adalah argumen pro/kontra terstruktur. Target: mampu berpartisipasi dalam diskusi dan debat akademik dengan argumen logis dan bahasa yang sopan.

## Analogi
Diskusi seperti ngobrol di seminar — saling mendengar dan menambah. Debat seperti pertandingan tinju intelektual — ada pemenang, aturan, dan juri.

## Poin-Poin Penting

- **Diskusi:**
  - *Agreeing:* I agree with you. That's a good point.
  - *Disagreeing politely:* I see your point, but... / I respectfully disagree.
  - *Adding:* Building on what you said... / Another aspect is...
  - *Clarifying:* What do you mean by...? / Could you elaborate?
- **Debat:**
  - *Opening statement:* Clearly state position + preview arguments.
  - *Rebuttal:* Respond to opponent's arguments + present counter-evidence.
  - *Closing:* Summarize strongest points + call to action.
- **Bahasa debat:** *The first speaker argued that... However, evidence suggests...*

## Langkah-Langkah

1. Pilih topik debatable (misal: "haruskah seragam sekolah dihapus?").
2. Tentukan posisi (pro atau kontra) dan kumpulkan argumen.
3. Latihan opening statement (1 menit).
4. Latihan rebuttal (30 detik).
5. Simulasi debat dengan teman atau rekam diri.

## Contoh

**Topik:** "Social media does more harm than good."
**Pro:** *"Social media increases anxiety and spreads misinformation. Studies show that teenagers who spend more than 3 hours on social media have higher rates of depression."*
**Kontra:** *"While social media has risks, it also connects people and provides access to information. Many movements for social justice started on social media."*

## Latihan

1. Pilih topik kontroversial. Tulis 3 argumen pro dan 3 kontra.
2. Latih opening statement 1 menit untuk posisi yang dipilih.
3. Latih disagreeing politely: "I understand your point, however..."
4. Apa perbedaan debat formal vs diskusi informal?
5. Simulasi debat 5 menit dengan teman.
