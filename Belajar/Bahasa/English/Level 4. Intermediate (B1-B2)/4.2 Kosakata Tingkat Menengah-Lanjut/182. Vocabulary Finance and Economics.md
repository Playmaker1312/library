# 182. Vocabulary Finance and Economics

## Definisi & Target
Kosakata esensial untuk memahami berita ekonomi, laporan keuangan, dan diskusi bisnis. Target: mampu membaca artikel ekonomi dan mendiskusikan topik keuangan.

## Analogi
Seperti belajar istilah "debit", "kredit", "saldo" sebelum buka rekening bank — ini adalah istilah dasar yang wajib tahu kalau mau ngobrol tentang uang dan ekonomi.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Kata | Makna | Contoh |
|------|-------|--------|
| **Revenue** | Pendapatan kotor | The company's revenue increased by 20% |
| **Profit** | Laba (pendapatan - biaya) | Net profit reached $1 million |
| **Expenditure** | Pengeluaran/belanja | Government expenditure rose sharply |
| **Inflation** | Inflasi (kenaikan harga) | Inflation is at an all-time high |
| **Recession** | Resesi (penurunan ekonomi) | The economy entered a recession |
| **Stock / Share** | Saham | Share prices fell dramatically |
| **Dividend** | Dividen (bagian laba) | The company paid a high dividend |
| **Interest rate** | Suku bunga | The central bank raised interest rates |
| **GDP (Gross Domestic Product)** | PDB | GDP grew by 3% this quarter |
| **Asset / Liability** | Aset / kewajiban | The company has more assets than liabilities |
| **Depreciation** | Depresiasi (penyusutan) | The currency experienced depreciation |
| **Subsidy** | Subsidi | The government provides fuel subsidies |

## Contoh

1. **Due to inflation, the purchasing power of consumers has decreased.** — Akibat inflasi, daya beli konsumen menurun.
2. **The company's revenue grew but profit margins shrank.** — Pendapatan perusahaan tumbuh tapi margin laba menyusut.
3. **The stock market crashed, causing panic among investors.** — Pasar saham jatuh, menyebabkan kepanikan di kalangan investor.
