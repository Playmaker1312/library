# 181. Collocations Lanjutan (Adverb+Adjective, Verb+Noun Tingkat Tinggi)

## Definisi & Target
Collocations adalah pasangan kata yang sering muncul bersama. Level lanjutan mencakup pasangan yang lebih kompleks dan idiomatis. Target: membuat bahasa terdengar natural seperti penutur asli.

## Analogi
Di Indonesia: "ancaman serius" (bukan "ancaman berat") atau "peluang besar" (bukan "peluang lebar"). Collocations seperti pasangan yang sudah "jodoh" secara linguistik.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Type | Collocation | Contoh |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Adverb + Adjective | **Highly unlikely** | It's highly unlikely that he'll come |
| Adverb + Adjective | **Bitterly disappointed** | She was bitterly disappointed |
| Adverb + Adjective | **Deeply concerned** | We are deeply concerned about this issue |
| Adverb + Adjective | **Widely regarded** | He is widely regarded as an expert |
| Adverb + Adjective | **Fully aware** | I am fully aware of the risks |
| Verb + Noun | **Draw a conclusion** | What conclusion can we draw? |
| Verb + Noun | **Make a commitment** | She made a commitment to improve |
| Verb + Noun | **Take offense** | Don't take offense at his comments |
| Verb + Noun | **Face consequences** | You must face the consequences |
| Verb + Noun | **Meet requirements** | Does this meet the requirements? |

## Contoh

1. **It is highly unlikely that the project will finish on time.** — Sangat tidak mungkin proyek selesai tepat waktu.
2. **She was bitterly disappointed by the results.** — Dia sangat kecewa dengan hasilnya.
3. **We need to draw a conclusion from this data.** — Kita perlu menarik kesimpulan dari data ini.
4. **He is widely regarded as the best in his field.** — Dia dianggap luas sebagai yang terbaik di bidangnya.

## Latihan

1. Cari collocation yang tepat: "He is ___ aware of the problem." (deeply/fully/highly)
2. Lengkapi: "She ___ offense at his joke." (took/made/had)
3. Buat kalimat dengan "make a commitment" tentang karir.
4. Bedakan "highly likely" vs "highly unlikely" dalam konteks.
5. Tulis 3 verb + noun collocations dengan "conclusion".
