# 178. Euphemisms

## Definisi & Target
Euphemism adalah ungkapan halus untuk menggantikan kata yang dianggap kasar, tabu, atau tidak nyaman. Target: memahami dan menggunakan euphemisms dengan tepat dalam konteks sosial.

## Analogi
Seperti di Indonesia bilang "meninggal dunia" bukan "mati", atau "tidak bisa melihat" bukan "buta". Euphemisms membuat bahasa lebih sopan dan tidak menyakitkan.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Euphemism | Arti Langsung | Konteks |
|-----------|---------------|---------|
| **Pass away** | Die | He passed away last year |
| **Between jobs** | Unemployed | He's currently between jobs |
| **Let someone go** | Fire | The company had to let 50 people go |
| **Plus-size** | Overweight | The store carries plus-size clothing |
| **Correctional facility** | Prison | He served time in a correctional facility |
| **Economical with the truth** | Lie | He was being economical with the truth |
| **Adult entertainment** | Pornography | Not appropriate for children |
| **Vertically challenged** | Short | He's a bit vertically challenged |
| **Powder your nose** | Go to toilet | Excuse me, I need to powder my nose |

## Contoh

1. **Unfortunately, our grandfather passed away last night.** — Sayangnya, kakek kami meninggal tadi malam.
2. **She's between jobs right now, but she's actively looking.** — Dia sedang menganggur, tapi aktif mencari kerja.
3. **The management decided to let him go due to restructuring.** — Manajemen memutuskan memberhentikannya karena restrukturisasi.
