# 171. Complex Passive Structures (is said to be, is believed to have)

## Definisi & Target
Complex passive menggunakan pola "It + passive verb + that..." atau "S + passive verb + to infinitive" untuk melaporkan pendapat, kepercayaan, atau klaim tanpa menyebut sumber. Target: menulis secara objektif dan formal.

## Analogi
Seperti berita yang bilang "Dilaporkan bahwa..." atau "Diduga..." tanpa bilang siapa yang lapor. Ini cara ilmiah/formal untuk bicara tanpa harus menyebut subjek.

## Pola & Struktur

| Struktur | Rumus | Contoh |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Personal | S + is/are + said/believed + to V | He is said to be very rich |
| Personal (past) | S + is/are + said/believed + to have V3 | She is believed to have left the country |
| Impersonal 1 | It + is + said/believed + that + clause | It is believed that the project will fail |
| Impersonal 2 | There + is/are + said/believed + to be | There are said to be problems |

## Contoh

1. **He is said to be the best surgeon in the country.** — Dia dikatakan sebagai ahli bedah terbaik di negeri ini.
2. **The painting is believed to have been stolen.** — Lukisan itu diyakini telah dicuri.
3. **It is thought that the company will go bankrupt.** — Diperkirakan perusahaan akan bangkrut.
4. **She is known to have worked for the government.** — Dia diketahui pernah bekerja untuk pemerintah.
5. **There is expected to be a rise in prices.** — Diperkirakan akan ada kenaikan harga.

## Latihan

1. Ubah "People say that he is a genius" ke complex passive (personal).
2. Ubah "They believe that she escaped" ke impersonal passive.
3. Buat kalimat dengan "is expected to" tentang cuaca.
4. Lengkapi: The suspect ___ (report) ___ (see) near the bank.
5. Bedakan "He is said to be rich" vs "He is said to have been rich."
