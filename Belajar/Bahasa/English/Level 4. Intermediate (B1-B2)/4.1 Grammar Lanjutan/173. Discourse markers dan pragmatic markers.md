# 173. Discourse Markers dan Pragmatic Markers

## Definisi & Target
Discourse markers menghubungkan ide dalam teks. Pragmatic markers mengekspresikan sikap pembicara. Target: menggunakan penanda wacana untuk tulisan dan percakapan yang lebih natural dan terstruktur.

## Analogi
Seperti rambu lalu lintas: "belok kiri", "hati-hati", "jalan lurus" — mereka memberi sinyal ke pembaca/pendengar ke mana arah pembicaraan selanjutnya.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Marker | Fungsi | Contoh |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **However / Nevertheless** | Kontras | The weather was bad. However, we continued |
| **Moreover / Furthermore** | Menambah argumen | He is smart. Moreover, he is diligent |
| **Therefore / Consequently** | Kesimpulan/akibat | She studied hard. Therefore, she passed |
| **Meanwhile** | Waktu bersamaan | I cooked dinner. Meanwhile, he set the table |
| **Actually / As a matter of fact** | Koreksi/penekanan | Actually, I don't agree |
| **Well / I mean / You know** | Pragmatic (ragu/penjelas) | Well, I'm not so sure |
| **Honestly / Frankly** | Sikap jujur | Honestly, that was terrible |
| **By the way** | Ganti topik | By the way, have you seen my keys? |

## Contoh

1. **The flight was delayed. Nevertheless, we arrived on time.** — Penerbangan tertunda. Namun, kami tiba tepat waktu.
2. **He's not just talented; furthermore, he works incredibly hard.** — Dia tidak hanya berbakat; lebih lagi, dia kerja sangat keras.
3. **Well, I suppose you have a point.** — Yah, saya kira kamu ada benarnya.
4. **Honestly, I had no idea it would be this difficult.** — Jujur, saya tidak tahu akan sesulit ini.
5. **The company lost money. Consequently, they had to lay off staff.** — Perusahaan rugi. Akibatnya, mereka harus PHK.

## Latihan

1. Hubungkan: "It was raining." + "We went for a walk." (pakai however)
2. Tambah argumen: "She is experienced." + "She has great references" (pakai moreover)
3. Gunakan "by the way" dalam percakapan ganti topik.
4. Tulis 3 kalimat dengan "therefore", "nevertheless", dan "frankly".
5. Bedakan penggunaan "actually" untuk koreksi vs penekanan.
