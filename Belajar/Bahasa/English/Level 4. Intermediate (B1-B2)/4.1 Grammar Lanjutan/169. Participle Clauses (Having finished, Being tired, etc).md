# 169. Participle Clauses (Having finished, Being tired, etc)

## Definisi & Target
Participle clause menggunakan present participle (V-ing) atau past participle (V3) sebagai klausa pengganti. Target: menulis kalimat kompleks secara ringkas.

## Analogi
Seperti caption Instagram yang pendek-pendek: "Finished work. Feeling tired. Going home." Itulah participle clause — memadatkan dua aksi jadi satu kalimat efisien.

## Pola & Struktur

| Type | Struktur | Contoh |
|------|----------|--------|
| Present (active) | V-ing + clause | Walking home, I saw an accident |
| Past (passive) | V3 + clause | Exhausted by the trip, she went straight to bed |
| Perfect (sebab sebelumnya) | Having + V3 + clause | Having finished dinner, we watched a movie |
| Being + adjective | Being + adj + clause | Being late, he apologized profusely |
| Passive perfect | Having been + V3 | Having been warned, they left early |

## Contoh

1. **Having studied all night, she passed the exam.** — Setelah belajar semalaman, dia lulus ujian.
2. **Walking through the park, I found a wallet.** — Sambil jalan di taman, saya nemu dompet.
3. **Born in a small village, he never forgot his roots.** — Lahir di desa kecil, dia tak pernah lupa asalnya.
4. **Not knowing what to do, I called my mother.** — Tidak tahu harus apa, saya telepon ibu.
5. **Having been rejected twice, he almost gave up.** — Setelah ditolak dua kali, dia hampir menyerah.

## Latihan

1. Gabung: "She finished her homework. Then she went out."
2. Ubah "Because he was tired, he went to bed early" pakai participle.
3. Buat participle clause dari "After she had been promoted, she moved to a new office."
4. Tulis kalimat dengan "Being..." dalam konteks terlambat.
5. Bedakan: "Driving home, I listened to music" vs "Driven home, I..."
