# 170. Subjunctive Mood (I suggest he go, If I were you)

## Definisi & Target
Subjunctive mood digunakan untuk situasi yang tidak nyata, harapan, saran, atau permintaan. Bentuknya berbeda dari indicative biasa. Target: menggunakan subjunctive dengan tepat dalam saran dan situasi hipotetis.

## Analogi
Seperti di film ketika seseorang bilang "Long live the king!" — itu subjunctive. Bukan "The king lives long" (kenyataan), tapi harapan. Di Indonesia: "Tuhan memberkati" — itu subjunctive.

## Pola & Struktur

| Penggunaan | Struktur | Contoh |
|------------|----------|--------|
| After suggest/recommend/insist | S + suggest + that + S + base verb | I suggest that he go now |
| After wish (present unreal) | S + wish + S + past simple | I wish I were taller |
| After wish (past regret) | S + wish + S + past perfect | I wish I had studied harder |
| As if/as though | S + V + as if + S + past | He acts as if he were the boss |
| Fixed expressions | May + S + V | May the force be with you |

## Contoh

1. **I insist that he be present at the meeting.** — Saya bersikeras dia hadir di rapat.
2. **If I were you, I would accept the offer.** — Kalau saya jadi kamu, saya akan terima tawaran itu.
3. **The doctor recommended that she rest for a week.** — Dokter menyarankan dia istirahat seminggu.
4. **I wish I knew the answer.** — Saya berharap tahu jawabannya.
5. **She talks as if she were an expert.** — Dia bicara seolah-olah dia ahli.

## Latihan

1. Benarkan: "I suggest that he goes to the doctor."
2. Buat kalimat wish untuk: "I didn't save money, now I regret it."
3. Lengkapi: "It is essential that every student ___ (submit) the form."
4. Buat kalimat dengan "as if" tentang seseorang yang berpura-paya kaya.
5. Terjemahkan: "Saya usul agar dia tidak pergi sendirian."
