# 164. Mixed Conditionals

## Definisi & Target
Mixed conditionals menggabungkan dua waktu berbeda dalam conditional sentence — biasanya **if clause** di satu waktu (past) dan **main clause** di waktu lain (present/future). Target: mampu menyampaikan situasi hipotetis yang menghubungkan masa lalu dengan sekarang.

## Analogi
Bayangkan kamu menekan tombol "undo" di game setelah kalah. "Kalau dulu aku gak lari ke lorong itu (past), sekarang aku gak akan mati (present)." Ini mixed conditional — sebab di masa lalu, akibat di masa kini.

## Pola & Struktur

| Type | If Clause | Main Clause | Contoh |
|------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Past → Present | If + past perfect (had + V3) | would + base verb | If I had studied, I would be a doctor now |
| Past → Future | If + past perfect (had + V3) | would + base verb | If you had told me, I would help you tomorrow |
| Present → Past | If + past simple | would have + V3 | If I were rich, I would have bought that house |

## Contoh

1. **If I had taken that job, I would be living in London now.** — Kalau dulu saya ambil pekerjaan itu, sekarang saya tinggal di London.
2. **If she weren't so busy, she would have come to the party.** — Kalau dia tidak sibuk, dia pasti sudah datang ke pesta.
3. **They would be champions now if they had trained harder.** — Mereka akan jadi juara sekarang kalau dulu latihan lebih keras.
4. **If he had saved money, he wouldn't be in debt today.** — Kalau dulu dia menabung, dia tidak akan berutang sekarang.
5. **We wouldn't be lost if you had brought a map.** — Kita tidak akan tersesat kalau kamu bawa peta.

## Latihan

1. Ubah "I didn't study. I don't understand the lesson." menjadi mixed conditional.
2. Lengkapi: If they ___ (invest) earlier, they ___ (enjoy) the profits now.
3. Buat kalimat mixed conditional dari: She ate too much. Now she feels sick.
4. Bedakan arti: "If I had money, I would buy it" vs "If I had had money, I would have bought it."
5. Terjemahkan: "Kalau saja aku mendengarkan sarannya, aku tidak akan ada di masalah ini sekarang."
