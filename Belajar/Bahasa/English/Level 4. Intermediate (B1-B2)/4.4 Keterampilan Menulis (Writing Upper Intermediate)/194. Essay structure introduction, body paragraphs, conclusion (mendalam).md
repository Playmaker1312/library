# 194. Essay Structure: Introduction, Body Paragraphs, Conclusion (Mendalam)

## Definisi & Target
Memahami struktur tripartit esai akademik secara mendalam. Target: menulis esai terstruktur dengan introduction yang kuat, body paragraphs yang koheren, dan conclusion yang impactful.

## Analogi
Esai seperti rumah: introduction adalah pintu masuk (menarik dan memberi gambaran), body paragraphs adalah ruangan (masing-masing dengan fungsi spesifik), dan conclusion adalah pintu keluar (mengesankan).

## Poin-Poin Penting

- **Introduction:** Hook → Background → Thesis statement.
  - Hook: kalimat pembuka menarik (question, fact, anecdote).
  - Background: konteks singkat topik.
  - Thesis statement: argumen utama penulis.
- **Body paragraphs:** Topic sentence → Supporting sentences → Concluding sentence.
  - Satu paragraf = satu ide utama.
  - PEEL: Point, Evidence, Explanation, Link.
- **Conclusion:** Restate thesis → Summarize key points → Final thought.
  - JANGAN masukkan ide baru di conclusion.

## Struktur Penulisan

**Introduction (10-15% dari total esai)**
1. Hook — menarik perhatian
2. Background information — konteks
3. Thesis statement — argumen utama

**Body (70-80%)**
- Paragraph 1: Point + Evidence + Explanation + Link
- Paragraph 2: Point + Evidence + Explanation + Link
- Paragraph 3: Counter-argument + Rebuttal (opsional)

**Conclusion (10-15%)**
- Restate thesis (beda kata)
- Summarize main points
- Final thought / call to action

## Contoh

**Introduction:**
*"Have you ever wondered why some students excel while others struggle? (Hook) The debate between traditional and modern teaching methods has been ongoing for decades. (Background) This essay argues that a blended approach, combining the best of both methods, produces the most effective learning outcomes." (Thesis)*

## Latihan

1. Tulis thesis statement untuk topik: "Social media's impact on society."
2. Buat hook (3 jenis: question, fact, anecdote) untuk topik "climate change."
3. Kembangkan satu body paragraph dengan struktur PEEL.
4. Tulis conclusion dari esai yang sudah kamu buat sebelumnya.
5. Analisis esai contoh: identifikasi hook, thesis, topic sentences.
