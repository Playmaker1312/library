# 197. Cause-Effect Essay

## Definisi & Target
Cause-effect essay menganalisis penyebab dan akibat dari suatu fenomena. Target: menulis esai analitis yang menghubungkan sebab dan akibat secara logis.

## Analogi
Seperti dokter yang mencari penyebab sakit (cause) dan memprediksi apa yang akan terjadi jika tidak diobati (effect). Atau seperti detektif yang merunut "mengapa" dan "apa akibatnya."

## Poin-Poin Penting

- **Fokus:** Bisa focus on causes (lebih banyak sebab) atau focus on effects (lebih banyak dampak).
- **Chain reaction:** Satu cause bisa menyebabkan multiple effects, atau sebaliknya.
- **Immediate vs long-term effects:** Bedakan dampak jangka pendek dan panjang.
- **Causal link:** Pastikan hubungan sebab-akibat logis, bukan korelasi semu.
- **Transition words:** *because, due to, as a result, consequently, therefore, leads to, results in.*

## Struktur Penulisan

1. **Introduction:** Hook + Phenomenon description + Thesis (apakah fokus pada causes atau effects).
2. **Body 1 — Cause 1:** Explain one cause + how it leads to the phenomenon.
3. **Body 2 — Cause 2 (atau Effect):** Another cause or main effect.
4. **Body 3 — Effect:** The consequences (jika fokus pada effects).
5. **Conclusion:** Summarize + significance + prediction.

## Contoh

**Thesis (focus on effects):** *"The rise of social media has led to increased anxiety, decreased face-to-face interaction, and the spread of misinformation."*

**Cause paragraph:**
*"One major cause of deforestation is the expansion of palm oil plantations. As global demand for palm oil increases, companies clear vast areas of rainforest to make way for plantations."*

**Effect paragraph:**
*"As a result of deforestation, countless species lose their habitats, pushing them toward extinction."*

## Latihan

1. Pilih satu fenomena (misal: "kecanduan HP pada remaja"). Tulis 3 causes dan 3 effects.
2. Tulis thesis statement cause-effect tentang "global warming."
3. Kembangkan satu cause paragraph: jelaskan bagaimana cause → effect.
4. Bedakan immediate vs long-term effects dalam satu topik.
5. Tulis esai cause-effect mini 3 paragraf tentang "inflation."
