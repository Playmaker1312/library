# 92. Menulis Deskripsi Orang, Tempat, Benda

## Definisi & Target
Menulis teks deskriptif tentang orang, tempat, atau benda. Target: menggunakan adjective dengan tepat untuk membuat deskripsi yang hidup.

## Analogi
Seperti melukis dengan kata-kata — kamu ingin pembaca bisa "melihat" apa yang kamu deskripsikan. Gunakan kata sifat yang tepat seperti cat warna-warni.

## Poin-Poin Penting

### Deskripsi Orang
| Aspek | Contoh Vocabulary |
|---|---|
| **Penampilan** | tall, short, slim, curly hair, glasses |
| **Kepribadian** | friendly, funny, kind, hardworking, shy |
| **Usia** | young, elderly, middle-aged |
| **Pakaian** | wearing a blue shirt, casual clothes |

### Deskripsi Tempat
| Aspek | Contoh Vocabulary |
|---|---|
| **Ukuran** | big, small, huge, cozy, spacious |
| **Suasana** | quiet, noisy, peaceful, crowded |
| **Kondisi** | clean, dirty, modern, old, well-maintained |
| **Lokasi** | near, far, in the city center, by the beach |

### Deskripsi Benda
| Aspek | Contoh Vocabulary |
|---|---|
| **Ukuran & Bentuk** | round, square, long, flat |
| **Warna** | red, blue, dark green, bright yellow |
| **Bahan** | wooden, plastic, metal, glass |
| **Fungsi** | used for, functions as |

### Aturan Urutan Adjective
**Opinion → Size → Age → Shape → Color → Origin → Material → Purpose**
- "A **beautiful (opinion) big (size) old (age) round (shape) wooden (material) table**."

## Struktur Penulisan
1. **Topic sentence:** memperkenalkan subjek
2. **Deskripsi umum:** kesan pertama, gambaran besar
3. **Detail spesifik:** ciri-ciri khusus
4. **Penutup:** kesimpulan atau opini

## Contoh
"**My mother** is a kind and hardworking woman. She has long black hair and a warm smile. She is not very tall, but she is strong. She always wears simple clothes. I love her because she is very caring and always supports me."

## Latihan
1. Deskripsikan temanmu (penampilan + kepribadian)
2. Deskripsikan kamar tidurmu (ukuran, suasana, warna)
3. Deskripsikan benda favoritmu (bentuk, warna, fungsi)
4. Gunakan 3 adjectives dalam satu kalimat deskripsi
5. Tulis paragraf (4-5 kalimat) mendeskripsikan rumah impian
