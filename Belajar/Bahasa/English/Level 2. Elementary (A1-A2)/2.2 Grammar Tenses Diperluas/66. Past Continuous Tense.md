# 66. Past Continuous Tense

## Definisi & Target
Past Continuous untuk aksi yang sedang berlangsung di masa lalu. Target: memahami dan menggunakan was/were + V-ing.

## Analogi
Seperti CCTV yang merekam momen tertentu di masa lalu — "saat itu saya sedang melakukan sesuatu." Bukan aksi yang selesai, tapi yang sedang berlangsung.

## Pola & Struktur

**Rumus:** Subject + was/were + verb-ing

| Subject | was/were | V-ing | Contoh |
|---|---|---|---|
| I/He/She/It | was | sleeping | I was sleeping. |
| You/We/They | were | eating | They were eating. |

**Negatif:** Subject + was/were + not + V-ing
**Tanya:** Was/Were + subject + V-ing?

### Penggunaan
1. **Aksi sedang berlangsung di masa lalu:** "At 8 PM, I **was watching** TV."
2. **Dua aksi bersamaan:** "While I **was cooking**, he **was reading**."
3. **Aksi berlangsung ketika aksi lain terjadi:** "I **was walking** when it **started** to rain."
4. **Menggambarkan latar belakang cerita:** "The sun **was shining**, birds **were singing**."

## Contoh
- "**I was sleeping** when you called." — Saya sedang tidur ketika kamu menelepon.
- "**What were you doing** at 9 PM?" — Apa yang sedang kamu lakukan jam 9 malam?
- "**While she was studying**, her brother **was playing** games." — Sementara dia belajar, adiknya bermain game.

## Latihan
1. Ubah: "I (watch) TV when she arrived."
2. Buat negatif: "They ___ (not/sleep) at midnight."
3. Tanya: "What ___ you ___ (do) at 7 AM?"
4. Lengkapi: "While I ___ (walk), I ___ (see) a rainbow."
5. Buat 2 kalimat Past Continuous tentang kegiatan kemarin malam.
