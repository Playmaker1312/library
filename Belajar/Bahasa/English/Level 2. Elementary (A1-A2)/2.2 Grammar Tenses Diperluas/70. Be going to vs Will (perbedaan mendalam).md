# 70. Be Going To vs Will (Perbedaan Mendalam)

## Definisi & Target
Membedakan "be going to" (rencana & bukti) dengan "will" (spontan & prediksi). Target: memilih ekspresi future yang tepat.

## Analogi
"Be going to" seperti sudah beli tiket konser — pasti jadi. "Will" seperti baru bilang "ayo nonton" — spontan, belum pasti.

## Pola & Struktur

| Aspek | Be Going To | Will |
|---|---|---|
| Rumus | Subject + am/is/are + going to + V1 | Subject + will + V1 |
| Rencana | Sudah direncanakan sebelum bicara | Diputuskan saat bicara |
| Bukti | Ada bukti sekarang | Prediksi umum |
| Contoh | "It's cloudy. It's going to rain." | "I think it will rain tomorrow." |

### Kapan pakai Be Going To?
1. **Rencana sudah dibuat:** "I **am going to** visit my grandma this weekend."
2. **Ada bukti sekarang:** "Look at the sky! It **is going to** rain."
3. **Niat:** "I **am going to** learn English seriously."

### Kapan pakai Will?
1. **Keputusan spontan:** "The phone is ringing. I **will** answer it."
2. **Janji:** "I **will** call you later."
3. **Prediksi tanpa bukti:** "I think she **will** pass the exam."
4. **Tawaran:** "I **will** help you with that."

## Contoh
- "**I'm going to** buy a new laptop." (sudah rencana, mungkin sudah cari-cari)
- "This bag is heavy. I **will** carry it for you." (spontan menawarkan bantuan)
- "She **is going to** have a baby." (bukti: dia hamil) vs "I think she **will** have a baby girl." (prediksi)

## Latihan
1. Pilih: "A: Why did you buy paint? B: I ___ (paint) my room."
2. Pilih: "I forgot my pen. Don't worry, I ___ (lend) you mine."
3. Lengkapi: "The clouds are dark. It ___ (rain)."
4. Buat janji menggunakan will dan rencana menggunakan going to.
5. Dialog: rencanakan liburan (going to) dan respons spontan (will).
