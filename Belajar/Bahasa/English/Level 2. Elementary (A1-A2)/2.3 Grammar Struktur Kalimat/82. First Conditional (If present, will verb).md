# 82. First Conditional (If + present, will + verb)

## Definisi & Target
First Conditional untuk situasi yang mungkin terjadi di masa depan jika kondisi terpenuhi. Target: membuat kalimat pengkondisian tipe 1.

## Analogi
Seperti "Kalau hujan, saya akan pakai payung" — kondisi nyata yang mungkin terjadi. If-nya realistis, hasilnya logis.

## Pola & Struktur

**Rumus:** If + present simple, will + base verb

| If clause (kondisi) | Main clause (hasil) |
|---|---|
| If it rains, | I will stay home. |
| If you study, | you will pass. |
| If she comes, | we will be happy. |

### Urutan Kalimat
1. **If clause di awal:** "**If** you study hard, **you will pass** the exam." (pakai koma)
2. **If clause di akhir:** "**You will pass** the exam **if** you study hard." (tanpa koma)

### Variasi
- **If + present, can/may/might:** "If you finish early, you **can** go home."
- **If + present, imperative:** "If you see him, **tell** me."
- **Unless = if not:** "**Unless** you study, you will fail." (If you don't study...)

### Jangan Tertukar: Zero vs First Conditional
| Zero Conditional | First Conditional |
|---|---|
| Fakta umum | Situasi spesifik masa depan |
| "If you heat ice, it melts." | "If you heat the ice, it **will** melt." |
| Always true | Possible future |

## Contoh
- "**If** I have time, I **will visit** you." — Jika saya punya waktu, saya akan mengunjungimu.
- "**If** it's sunny tomorrow, we **will go** to the beach." — Jika besok cerah, kami akan pergi ke pantai.
- "She **will be** late **if** she doesn't hurry." — Dia akan terlambat jika tidak buru-buru.

## Latihan
1. Lengkapi: "If it rains, I ___ (stay) home."
2. Lengkapi: "If you ___ (study), you will pass."
3. Gabungkan: "You may get lost. Take a map." (gunakan if)
4. Ubah: "If you don't hurry" menggunakan "unless".
5. Buat 3 kalimat First Conditional tentang rencana besok.
