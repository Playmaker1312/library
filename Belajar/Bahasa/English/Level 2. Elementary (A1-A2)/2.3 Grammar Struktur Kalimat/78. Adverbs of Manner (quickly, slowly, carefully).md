# 78. Adverbs of Manner (quickly, slowly, carefully)

## Definisi & Target
Adverbs of manner menjelaskan *bagaimana* suatu aksi dilakukan. Target: membentuk dan menempatkan adverb of manner dengan benar.

## Analogi
Adverb seperti bumbu masakan — memberi rasa pada kata kerja. "Dia berlari" vs "Dia berlari **cepat**" — kata "cepat" menjelaskan caranya.

## Pola & Struktur

### Pembentukan
| Aturan | Adjective | Adverb |
|---|---|---|
| + -ly | quick → | quickly |
| Akhir -y → -ily | easy → | easily |
| Akhir -le → -ly | gentle → | gently |
| Akhir -ic → -ically | automatic → | automatically |
| Irregular | good → | well |
| Irregular | fast → | fast |
| Irregular | hard → | hard |
| Irregular | early → | early |

### Posisi dalam Kalimat
1. **Setelah verb:** "She sings **beautifully**."
2. **Sebelum verb (formal):** "He **quietly** left the room."
3. **Di awal kalimat (penekanan):** "**Carefully**, she opened the door."

### Jangan Tertukar: Adjective vs Adverb
- Adjective menerangkan noun: "She is a **quick** runner." (pelari cepat)
- Adverb menerangkan verb: "She runs **quickly**." (berlari dengan cepat)

## Contoh
- "Please speak **quietly**." — Tolong bicara dengan pelan.
- "He drives **carefully**." — Dia mengemudi dengan hati-hati.
- "She plays the piano **beautifully**." — Dia bermain piano dengan indah.
- "I did **well** on the test." — Saya melakukannya dengan baik di ujian.

## Latihan
1. Ubah adjective ke adverb: slow → ___, happy → ___, good → ___
2. Isi: "She sings ___ (beautiful)."
3. Perbaiki: "He drives careful."
4. Bedakan: "He is a hard worker" vs "He works hard."
5. Buat 3 kalimat menggunakan adverb of manner.
