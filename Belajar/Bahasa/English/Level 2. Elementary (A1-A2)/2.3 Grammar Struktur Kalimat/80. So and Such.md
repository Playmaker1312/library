# 80. So and Such

## Definisi & Target
"So" dan "Such" untuk memberikan penekanan atau menunjukkan tingkat ekstrem. Target: menggunakan so + adjective dan such + noun dengan benar.

## Analogi
"So" seperti "sangat" — "Dia **sangat** cantik" (so beautiful). "Such" seperti "sungguh" — "Dia **sungguh** orang yang baik" (such a nice person).

## Pola & Struktur

### So + Adjective/Adverb
**Rumus:** so + adjective/adverb (+ that clause)

- "The movie was **so** boring **(that)** I fell asleep."
- "She speaks **so** quickly."
- "I'm **so** tired."

### Such + (a/an) + (adjective) + Noun
**Rumus:** such + (a/an) + adjective + noun (+ that clause)

- "It was **such** a boring movie **(that)** I fell asleep."
- "She is **such** a nice person."
- "They are **such** kind people."

### Perbandingan

| So (tanpa noun) | Such (dengan noun) |
|---|---|
| "The food was **so** good." | "It was **such** good food." |
| "He is **so** smart." | "He is **such** a smart boy." |
| "The weather is **so** bad." | "It is **such** bad weather." |

### Pola dengan "that" (menunjukkan akibat)
- "It was **so** cold **that** we stayed inside."
- "It was **such** a cold day **that** we stayed inside."

## Contoh
- "She is **so** kind." — Dia sangat baik.
- "She is **such** a kind person." — Dia sungguh orang yang baik.
- "This book is **so** interesting **that** I can't stop reading." — Buku ini sangat menarik sehingga saya tidak bisa berhenti membaca.

## Latihan
1. Ubah ke such: "The car is so expensive."
2. Ubah ke so: "It is such a beautiful day."
3. Lengkapi: "He is ___ a good teacher."
4. Lengkapi: "The music was ___ loud that my ears hurt."
5. Buat 2 kalimat so dan 2 kalimat such.
