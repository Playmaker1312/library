# 341. English for Academic Research and Publishing

## Definisi & Target
Mempelajari academic writing, manuscript preparation, dan publishing process untuk jurnal internasional. Target: mampu menulis paper, response to reviewers, dan conference presentation.

## Analogi
Academic publishing seperti "proses kurasi museum" — butuh metodologi ketat, review teliti, dan revisi berkali-kali sebelum layak dipamerkan.

## Daftar Istilah & Frasa

| Istilah | Arti | Contoh |
|---------|------|--------|
| Abstract | Ringkasan penelitian | "The abstract should include background, methods, results, conclusion." |
| IMRaD | Introduction, Methods, Results, and Discussion — struktur paper | "Most scientific papers follow the IMRaD structure." |
| Peer review | Tinjauan sejawat | "The manuscript is currently under peer review." |
| Impact factor | Metrik pengaruh jurnal | "Nature has an impact factor of over 40." |
| DOI | Digital Object Identifier — ID permanen artikel | "The article is available at doi:10.1038/s41586-024-00000-0." |
| Plagiarism | Plagiarisme | "The paper was rejected due to plagiarism in the literature review." |
| Corresponding author | Penulis korespondensi | "The corresponding author handles all communication with the journal." |
| Revision (major/minor) | Revisi besar/kecil | "We received a major revision request from the reviewers." |
| Proof | Bukti cetak sebelum publikasi | "Please review the proof for any errors." |
| Retraction | Pencabutan artikel | "The paper was retracted due to data fabrication." |

**Frasa Akademik:**
- "This study aims to investigate..." (Penelitian ini bertujuan menyelidiki...)
- "The results indicate that..." (Hasil menunjukkan bahwa...)
- "These findings are consistent with previous research" (Temuan ini konsisten dengan penelitian sebelumnya)
- "Further research is needed to..." (Penelitian lebih lanjut diperlukan untuk...)

## Contoh
**Abstract:**
"This study investigates the impact of gamification on vocabulary acquisition among Indonesian EFL learners. Forty participants were randomly assigned to experimental and control groups. Results indicate that the gamified group significantly outperformed the control group (p < 0.05) in post-test scores. These findings suggest that gamification is an effective strategy for enhancing vocabulary learning."

## Latihan
1. Tulis 1 abstract 200 kata untuk penelitian fiktif bidangmu.
2. Baca 1 paper di Google Scholar, identifikasi struktur IMRaD.
3. Tulis 1 response to reviewer comment yang menolak metodologi.
4. Presentasi conference 3 menit tentang penelitianmu (record).
5. Pelajari 10 academic transition phrases: moreover, nevertheless, consequently, etc.
