# 339. English for Journalism and Media

## Definisi & Target
Mempelajari gaya penulisan jurnalistik, reporting, dan produksi konten media dalam Bahasa Inggris. Target: mampu menulis berita, feature, dan script broadcast.

## Analogi
Journalism English seperti "resep masakan" — ada struktur baku (inverted pyramid), tapi bumbu (gaya bahasa) yang bikin beda.

## Daftar Istilah & Frasa

| Istilah | Arti | Contoh |
|---------|------|--------|
| Inverted pyramid | Struktur berita — info terpenting di awal | "Breaking news uses the inverted pyramid: lead, body, tail." |
| Lead (lede) | Paragraf pembuka — menjawab 5W+1H | "The lead should grab the reader's attention immediately." |
| Byline | Nama penulis artikel | "The byline reads: By Sarah Jones, Staff Reporter." |
| Dateline | Kota + tanggal artikel ditulis | "JAKARTA, June 1, 2026 —" |
| Quote attribution | Sumber kutipan | "The mayor stated, 'We will rebuild.'" |
| Off the record | Informasi tidak boleh dipublikasikan | "The source spoke off the record about the investigation." |
| Embargo | Dilarang publikasi sebelum waktu tertentu | "The press release is under embargo until 5 PM." |
| Editorial | Artikel opini resmi media | "The editorial criticizes the new policy." |
| Angle | Sudut pandang liputan | "The story's angle focuses on the impact on small businesses." |
| Scoop / Exclusive | Berita eksklusif | "The journalist broke the scoop on corruption allegations." |

**Frasa Kunci:**
- "According to sources..." (Menurut sumber...)
- "When reached for comment..." (Saat dihubungi untuk komentar...)
- "This is a developing story" (Berita masih berkembang)

## Contoh
**News Lead (Inverted Pyramid):**
"A 6.2 magnitude earthquake struck the coast of Sumatra at 2:47 AM local time today, killing at least 45 people and displacing thousands, according to the National Disaster Management Agency."

## Latihan
1. Tulis 1 lead paragraph berita (5W+1H) tentang kejadian di sekitarmu.
2. Baca 1 artikel BBC News, identifikasi struktur inverted pyramid.
3. Rewrite 1 press release menjadi berita 200 kata.
4. Role-play: Wawancara narasumber (you are the journalist).
5. Tulis 1 editorial pendek 200 kata: "Should social media be regulated?"
