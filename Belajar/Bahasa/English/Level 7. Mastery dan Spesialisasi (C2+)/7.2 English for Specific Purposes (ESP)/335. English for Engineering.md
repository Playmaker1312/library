# 335. English for Engineering

## Definisi & Target
Mempelajari technical vocabulary dan komunikasi profesional dalam bidang engineering (sipil, mesin, elektro, dll). Target: mampu membaca technical specs, menulis laporan, dan presentasi proyek.

## Analogi
English for Engineering seperti "blueprint" — presisi dan kejelasan lebih penting daripada gaya bahasa.

## Daftar Istilah & Frasa

| Istilah | Arti | Contoh Kalimat |
|---------|------|----------------|
| Load | Beban | "The bridge is designed to withstand a load of 50 tons." |
| Tolerance | Toleransi (deviasi diizinkan) | "The tolerance for this component is ±0.01 mm." |
| Fatigue | Kelelahan material | "Stress analysis showed signs of metal fatigue." |
| Prototype | Purwarupa | "The prototype was tested under simulated conditions." |
| Compliance | Kesesuaian (terhadap standar) | "The design complies with ISO 9001 standards." |
| Specification (spec) | Spesifikasi | "The specs require a tensile strength of 400 MPa." |
| Finite element analysis | Analisis elemen hingga (FEA) | "FEA was conducted to evaluate stress distribution." |
| Failure mode | Mode kegagalan | "The failure mode was identified as brittle fracture." |

**Frasa Kunci:**
- "According to the calculations..." (Sesuai perhitungan...)
- "The system must be designed to withstand..." (Sistem harus dirancang untuk menahan...)
- "Further testing is required to validate..." (Pengujian lebih lanjut diperlukan untuk memvalidasi...)

## Contoh
**Technical Report:**
"The beam deflection was measured at 2.3 mm under full load, which falls within the acceptable tolerance of ±3.0 mm. However, stress concentration was observed at the weld joint, requiring reinforcement."

## Latihan
1. Baca 1 datasheet komponen elektronik/mesin, catat 10 spesifikasi kunci.
2. Tulis 1 paragraph describing a simple engineering process (e.g., water filtration).
3. Presentasi 2 menit tentang proyek engineering fiktif — rekam dan evaluasi.
4. Gambarkan flowchart proses manufaktur, jelaskan langkah-langkah dalam English.
5. Cari 1 patent description (Google Patents), baca dan rangkum claim utama.
