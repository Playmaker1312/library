# 342. English for Teaching (TEFL TESOL Methodology Pengenalan)

## Definisi & Target
Pengenalan metodologi pengajaran Bahasa Inggris sebagai bahasa asing (TEFL/TESOL). Target: memahami prinsip lesson planning, classroom management, dan language assessment.

## Analogi
Teaching English seperti "jadi chef" — tidak cukup bisa masak (bisa English), harus tahu teknik menyajikan dan resep yang pas untuk murid.

## Daftar Istilah & Frasa

| Istilah | Arti | Contoh |
|---------|------|--------|
| TEFL | Teaching English as a Foreign Language | "TEFL certification is required to teach English abroad." |
| TESOL | Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages | "She earned an MA in TESOL from the University of London." |
| CLT | Communicative Language Teaching — fokus pada komunikasi nyata | "CLT emphasizes interaction as both the means and goal of learning." |
| PPP | Presentation, Practice, Production — model lesson | "The teacher introduced new vocabulary (P1), students drilled (P2), then created dialogues (P3)." |
| TBLT | Task-Based Language Teaching — belajar melalui tugas | "Students planned a trip itinerary to practice travel vocabulary." |
| CEFR | Common European Framework of Reference — A1 to C2 | "This course is designed for B1 learners." |
| Scaffolding | Dukungan bertahap sampai murid mandiri | "The teacher used sentence starters as scaffolding." |
| Elicit | Memancing respons dari murid | "Instead of telling, the teacher elicited the answer through questions." |
| Correction techniques | Teknik koreksi | "Delayed correction is better during fluency activities." |
| Warmers / Coolers | Aktivitas pembuka/penutup | "A quick warmer activity sets the tone for the lesson." |

**Frasa Classroom:**
- "Work in pairs" — Bekerja berpasangan
- "Let's go over the answers" — Mari bahas jawaban
- "Can you give me an example?" — Bisakah memberi contoh?
- "Good effort, but try this..." — Usaha bagus, tapi coba ini...

## Contoh
**Lesson Plan Fragment (PPP):**
- **Target:** Comparative adjectives (bigger, more expensive)
- **Presentation:** Show pictures of two cities, model sentences
- **Practice:** Fill-in-the-blank worksheet + pair drill
- **Production:** Students compare two products in a role-play (buying a phone)

## Latihan
1. Tulis lesson plan 40 menit untuk level B1: topic "Ordering food in a restaurant".
2. Microteaching: record 5 menit mengajar grammar point (present perfect).
3. Cari 3 warmer activities for adults, tulis step-by-step instructions.
4. Analisis 1 materi ajar (buku teks English), evaluasi menggunakan CLT principles.
5. Role-play: Teacher corrects a student's error — use 3 different correction techniques.
