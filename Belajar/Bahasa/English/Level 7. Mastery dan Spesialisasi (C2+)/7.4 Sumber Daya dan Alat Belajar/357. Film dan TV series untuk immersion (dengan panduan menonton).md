# 357. Film dan TV Series untuk Immersion (Dengan Panduan Menonton)

## Definisi & Target
Menggunakan film dan serial sebagai tools immersive listening. Target: 2–3 episode per minggu dengan metode active watching.

## Daftar & Deskripsi

### Rekomendasi Berdasarkan Level

| Film / Series | Aksen | Alasan untuk Advanced Learners |
|-------------|-------|-------------------------------|
| **The Crown** (Netflix) | British (formal, RP) | Dialog formal, vocabulary politik/kerajaan, slow-paced |
| **Sherlock** (BBC) | British (fast, modern) | Fast-paced dialogue, vocabulary deduction, British slang |
| **The West Wing** | American (fast, formal) | Witty dialogue, political vocabulary, rhetorical devices |
| **Succession** (HBO) | American (business) | Business jargon, sarcasm, power dynamics language |
| **The Office (US)** | American (casual) | Colloquial English, workplace humor, idioms sehari-hari |
| **Black Mirror** | British/American | Tech vocabulary, ethical discussion, cultural references |
| **Killers of the Flower Moon** (Scorsese) | American (diverse aksen) | Historical narrative, formal + colloquial mix |
| **Fleabag** (BBC) | British (modern) | Natural dialogue, humor, emotional range |

## Tips Penggunaan

### Panduan Menonton
1. **First watch:** English audio + English subtitles. Read and listen simultaneously.
2. **Second watch (scene):** No subtitles — pilih 1 adegan kunci, coba pahami 100%.
3. **Shadowing:** Ulangi dialog aktor — mimic intonation, stress, rhythm.
4. **Pause & predict:** Pause sebelum punchline, tebak apa yang akan dikatakan.
5. **Vocabulary log:** Catat 5–10 expressions per episode. Contoh: "I'm not going to mince words" (tidak akan berbasa-basi).

### Metode "5 Scene" untuk Advanced Learners
- Pilih 5 adegan pendek (3–5 menit per episode).
- Tonton 3x: (1) subtitle on, (2) subtitle off, (3) shadowing.
- Transkrip 1 adegan secara manual.

## Contoh
**The West Wing — "Walk and Talk" Scene:**
- **Dialog:** "We need to get the budget proposal to the Hill by Friday. What's our timeline?"
- **Vocab:** "the Hill" = Capitol Hill (Congress), "timeline" = jadwal
- **Frasa:** "Get something to the Hill" — mengajukan sesuatu ke Kongres

## Latihan
1. Pilih 1 episode The Crown, watch 20 menit dengan English subtitles, catat 10 new words.
2. Pilih 1 scene pendek, tonton tanpa subtitle, tulis transkrip manual.
3. Shadowing: tiru 5 kalimat dari aktor, rekam, bandingkan intonation.
4. Tulis paragraph review film 200 kata — opinimu + 3 vocabulary baru.
5. Buat list "slang/idiom yang muncul" dari 1 episode The Office US.
