# 360. AI Tools untuk Language Learning (ChatGPT, Language Tutors)

## Definisi & Target
Memanfaatkan AI sebagai personal language tutor virtual. Target: integrasi AI ke daily practice untuk speaking, writing, dan grammar feedback.

## Analogi
AI language tutor seperti "personal trainer yang sabar" — available 24/7, tidak pernah bosan, bisa disesuaikan dengan kebutuhanmu.

## Daftar & Deskripsi

| Tool | Fitur | Keunggulan |
|------|-------|-----------|
| **ChatGPT (GPT-4 / Claude)** | Conversational AI | Simulasi percakapan, grammar explanation, writing feedback, role-play |
| **Perplexity AI** | Search + answer berbasis sumber | Research vocabulary usage dengan source real |
| **DeepL Write** | AI writing assistant | Lebih natural dari Grammarly untuk bilingual EN-ID |
| **Talkpal / Praktika** | AI speaking tutor | Voice-based conversation, pronunciation feedback |
| **OpenAI Whisper** | Speech-to-text akurat | Transcribe speaking practice, cek pengucapan |

## Tips Penggunaan

### Prompt ChatGPT untuk Language Learning
- **Role-play:** "Let's practice a job interview. You are the interviewer asking about my experience in project management."
- **Error correction:** "Correct this paragraph and explain each change: [your text]"
- **Vocabulary:** "Give me 5 synonyms for 'important' with example sentences and usage context."
- **Grammar explanation:** "Explain the difference between 'I have been working' and 'I had been working' with examples."
- **Debate practice:** "Argue against my position on [topic]. I believe remote work is better than office work."

### Metode "AI-First Learning"
1. **Speaking:** Bicara dengan AI voice mode (ChatGPT app) — 10 menit daily.
2. **Writing:** Tulis journal entry, minta AI feedback on vocabulary + grammar.
3. **Reading:** Minta AI summarise artikel, jelaskan vocabulary sulit.
4. **Listening:** AI generate listening exercise dengan topik spesifik.

## Contoh
**ChatGPT Prompt untuk Writing Feedback:**
Prompt: "I wrote this paragraph for my blog. Please correct any grammar errors, suggest better vocabulary choices, and explain why: 'Nowadays, many people is using social media for get news. This is causing some problems because the news sometimes not true.'"

## Latihan
1. ChatGPT role-play: simulate ordering food at a restaurant (10 exchanges).
2. Minta ChatGPT correct 1 paragraph writingmu — tanya "why" untuk tiap koreksi.
3. Use voice mode (ChatGPT app) — diskusi 5 menit tentang topik favorit.
4. Minta ChatGPT generate 5 soal TOEFL/IELTS writing, jawab, minta score.
5. Coba DeepL Write: tulis 1 paragraf in Indonesian, translate + polish English version.
