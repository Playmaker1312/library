# 325. Cambridge Exams CAE (C1 Advanced)

## Definisi & Target
CAE (C1 Advanced) membuktikan kemampuan English for high-level academic/professional. Target: Grade A, B, atau C (setara IELTS 6.5–8.0).

## Analogi
CAE seperti "SIM internasional" — bisa nyetir di jalan tol akademik, ngerti rambu-rambu kompleks, dan improvisasi situasi sulit.

## Struktur & Strategi

### Reading & Use of English (1 jam 30 menit, 8 parts)
- **Part 1–4:** Mirip FCE tapi lebih kompleks (multiple-choice cloze, open cloze, word formation, key word transformations).
- **Part 5–6:** Multiple choice (long text) + cross-text multiple matching.
- **Part 7–8:** Gapped text + multiple matching.
- **Strategi Fokus:** "Key word transformations" (Part 4) — gunakan structures seperti inversions, cleft sentences, passive voice.

### Writing (1 jam 30 menit, 2 tasks)
- **Task 1:** Essay (220–260 words) — analysis + evaluation.
- **Task 2:** Pilihan — proposal, report, review, letter.
- **Strategi:** Proposal dan report sering keluar di CAE — pelajari format keduanya.

### Listening (40 menit, 4 parts)
- **Part 1:** Three short extracts (multiple choice).
- **Part 2:** Sentence completion (monologue).
- **Part 3:** Multiple choice (interview/discussion).
- **Part 4:** Multiple matching (longer monologues).
- **Strategi:** Part 4 — identifikasi attitude dan opinion tiap speaker (not just facts).

### Speaking (15 menit, 4 parts)
- Sama format FCE tetapi topik lebih abstrak dan kompleks.
- **Part 3:** Collaborative task dengan data visual (charts/diagrams).
- **Part 4:** Justify opinions, speculate, evaluate.

## Contoh
**Key Word Transformation (Part 4):**
- Prompt: "I'm sure it was John who took the money." (MUST)
- Jawaban: "It MUST have BEEN John who took the money."
- Latihan modals of deduction + cleft sentences.

## Latihan
1. Word formation: base "PRECISE" → "precisely", "imprecise", "precision" dalam kalimat.
2. Transform 5 kalimat menggunakan inversion: "Not until...", "Rarely...", "Seldom...".
3. Tulis proposal 250 kata: "Propose a change to improve your workplace."
4. Listening — dengar lecture akademik 3 menit, identifikasi 3 opini + 3 fakta.
5. Speaking — rekam jawaban Part 4: "To what extent should governments regulate technology?"
