# 328. PTE Academic

## Definisi & Target
PTE Academic adalah tes English 100% berbasis komputer, sering dipilih karena hasil cepat (48 jam). Target: 65+ (setara IELTS 7.0).

## Analogi
PTE Academic seperti "test drive digital" — semua dinilai oleh AI, jadi strateginya beda dengan ujian manusia.

## Struktur & Strategi

### Speaking & Writing (77–93 menit, bersama)
- **Personal Introduction:** Tidak dinilai tapi dikirim ke institusi.
- **Read Aloud:** Baca teks di layar — nilai pronunciation + fluency.
- **Repeat Sentence:** Dengar, ulangi persis — jangan ubah kata.
- **Describe Image:** Bar chart, line graph, map, proses — deskripsi 40 detik.
- **Re-tell Lecture:** Dengar kuliah 90 detik, rangkum 40 detik.
- **Summarize Written Text:** 1 kalimat (5–75 words).
- **Essay:** 200–300 words, 20 menit.

**Strategi khusus PTE:** AI menilai keyword dan pronunciation. Bicara jelas, jangan berhenti di tengah kalimat (fluency score penting).

### Reading (32–41 menit)
- Multiple choice, re-order paragraphs, fill in the blanks (reading & writing).
- **Strategi:** Fill in the blanks — cari collocations dan grammar clues.

### Listening (45–57 menit)
- Summarize spoken text, multiple choice, fill in the blanks, highlight incorrect words, write from dictation.
- **Strategi Write from Dictation:** Ketik persis kata yang didengar — satu kata salah, skor berkurang.

## Contoh
**Describe Image:**
Image: Bar chart "Monthly Sales 2024"
Response: "This bar chart illustrates monthly sales in 2024. Overall, sales peaked in December and reached their lowest point in January. The most significant increase occurred between September and October."

## Latihan
1. Cari gambar grafik Google, rekam deskripsi 40 detik — transkrip, evaluasi keyword.
2. Repeat Sentence — dengar audio pendek, ulangi, cek accuracy (gunakan PTE practice apps).
3. Write from Dictation — cari 10 kalimat di YouTube PTE channel, ketik persis.
4. Summarize Written Text — baca paragraph 50 kata, ringkas jadi 1 kalimat.
5. Essay 250 kata: "Technology has changed the way people interact. Discuss."
