# 331. GMAT Verbal Section

## Definisi & Target
GMAT Verbal mengukur kemampuan berpikir kritis dan komunikasi tertulis — esensial untuk MBA. Target: 35–51 (skala 0–60).

## Analogi
GMAT Verbal seperti "sidang pengadilan bisnis" — harus bisa bedain argumen kuat-lemah, deteksi asumsi tersembunyi, dan tulis dengan presisi.

## Struktur & Strategi

### Reading Comprehension (13–14 soal)
- 4 passages (200–350 words), topik: business, science, humanities, social science.
- **Question types:** Main idea, detail, inference, purpose, tone.
- **Strategi:** Identifikasi structure passage — theory vs critique vs evidence.

### Critical Reasoning (12–14 soal)
- **Core skill:** Analyze arguments — identify premise, conclusion, assumption.
- **Question types:** Strengthen, weaken, assumption, inference, paradox, boldface, flaw.
- **Strategi:** Setiap soal CR: temukan conclusion dulu. Tanpa conclusion, kamu tidak bisa jawab.

### Sentence Correction (12–13 soal)
- Pilih kalimat yang grammatically correct dan paling jelas.
- **Tes:** Subject-verb agreement, parallelism, modifiers, pronouns, verb tense, idioms.
- **Strategi:** Eliminasi 3–4 jawaban dulu berdasarkan grammar errors, baru pilih yang paling concise.

### Integrated Reasoning (opsional di GMAT Focus Edition)
- **Catatan:** GMAT Focus Edition (2024+) menghilangkan Sentence Correction dan mengintegrasikan Data Insights. Cek versi ujian yang kamu ambil.

## Contoh
**Critical Reasoning (Weaken):**
"People who exercise regularly have lower blood pressure. Therefore, regular exercise reduces blood pressure."
**Process:** Premise = correlation, Conclusion = causation. Weaken dengan: "People with lower BP tend to exercise more naturally."

## Latihan
1. Baca 1 passage business (Harvard Business Review), jawab 3 soal main idea + inference.
2. Kerjakan 5 soal Critical Reasoning — identifikasi premise + conclusion tiap soal.
3. Sentence Correction: "The committee, consisting of experts from various fields, have decided..." — apa error? (Answer: S-V agreement — "committee has decided").
4. Analisis 1 argument dari editorial berita, cari logical flaw.
5. Review jawaban salah di GMAT Official Guide, catat pola error.
