# 243. Academic Writing: Methodology Description

## Definisi & Target
Menulis deskripsi metodologi penelitian yang jelas, reproducible, dan sesuai standar akademik. Target: mampu mendeskripsikan desain penelitian, partisipan, instrumen, dan prosedur.

## Analogi
Seperti resep masakan — metodologi harus cukup detail sehingga orang lain bisa mengulanginya dengan hasil yang sama.

## Poin-Poin Penting
- Gunakan past tense (metode sudah dilakukan)
- Bersifat objektif, tanpa opini
- Sertakan justifikasi: mengapa memilih metode ini
- Identifikasi variabel, populasi, sampel
- Jelaskan instrumen dan prosedur analisis

## Struktur & Strategi
```
1. Research design: "A quantitative correlational design was employed..."
2. Participants: "A total of 100 participants were recruited..."
3. Instruments: "Data were collected using a survey adapted from..."
4. Procedure: "Participants completed the questionnaire in 20 minutes..."
5. Data analysis: "Data were analyzed using SPSS version 26..."
```

## Contoh
"A **mixed-methods design** was employed to examine the impact of remote work on productivity. **Quantitative data** were collected through an online survey (n=250), while **qualitative data** were gathered via semi-structured interviews (n=15). Participants were recruited through professional networks..."

## Latihan
1. Tulis deskripsi partisipan untuk studi tentang kebiasaan membaca.
2. Jelaskan instrumen penelitian yang Anda pilih dan mengapa.
3. Bedakan deskripsi metode kualitatif dan kuantitatif.
4. Gunakan passive voice dalam deskripsi prosedur.
5. Tulis 1 paragraf metodologi lengkap untuk topik pilihan.
