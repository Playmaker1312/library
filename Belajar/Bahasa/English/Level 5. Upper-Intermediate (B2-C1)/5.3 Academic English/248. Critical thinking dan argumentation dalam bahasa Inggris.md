# 248. Critical Thinking dan Argumentation dalam Bahasa Inggris

## Definisi & Target
Mengembangkan kemampuan berpikir kritis dan argumentasi dalam bahasa Inggris. Target: mampu mengevaluasi argumen, mengidentifikasi logical fallacies, dan membangun argumen yang koheren.

## Analogi
Seperti hakim di pengadilan — mendengar semua sisi, mengevaluasi bukti, lalu memutuskan berdasarkan logika, bukan emosi.

## Poin-Poin Penting
- Claim: pernyataan yang perlu dibuktikan
- Evidence: data, fakta, penelitian
- Warrant: alasan mengapa evidence mendukung claim
- Counterargument: argumen lawan
- Rebuttal: sanggahan terhadap counterargument

## Struktur & Strategi
Model Toulmin:
```
1. Claim: "Remote work increases productivity."
2. Data: "Studies show 15% increase."
3. Warrant: "Fewer distractions at home."
4. Counterargument: "But collaboration suffers."
5. Rebuttal: "Regular virtual meetings address this."
```

Logical fallacies umum: ad hominem, straw man, false dilemma, slippery slope, hasty generalization.

## Contoh
"**While** critics argue that social media connects people (counterargument), **the evidence suggests** that excessive use correlates with loneliness (claim). **This is because** online interactions often replace, rather than supplement, face-to-face relationships (warrant)."

## Latihan
1. Identifikasi logical fallacy: "You don't like this policy? You must hate progress."
2. Bangun argumen Toulmin tentang AI di pendidikan.
3. Tulis counterargument dan rebuttal tentang kebijakan work-from-home.
4. Bedakan valid argument dan fallacy.
5. Evaluasi argumen: "All politicians are corrupt because some were caught."
