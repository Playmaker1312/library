# 240. Lexical Bundles dan Formulaic Sequences

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai lexical bundles — kombinasi kata yang sering muncul bersama dalam konteks tertentu. Target: menggunakan frasa formulaik yang alami dan idiomatik.

## Analogi
Seperti chord dalam musik — kombinasi nada yang sering dipakai bersama. Lexical bundles adalah "chord" dalam bahasa.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Lexical Bundle | Konteks | Contoh |
|---------------|---------|--------|
| In the context of | Akademik | "In the context of climate change..." |
| On the other hand | Argumentasi | "On the other hand, critics argue..." |
| As far as... is concerned | Opinion | "As far as the budget is concerned..." |
| It is worth noting that | Emphasis | "It is worth noting that the results vary." |
| There is a growing body of evidence | Research | "There is a growing body of evidence linking diet and health." |
| Taking into consideration | Evaluasi | "Taking into consideration all factors..." |
| In light of the fact that | Reasoning | "In light of the fact that resources are limited..." |

## Contoh
1. "**In light of the recent developments**, we need to revise our strategy."
2. "**There is a growing body of evidence** supporting the effectiveness of early intervention."
3. "**As far as the timeline is concerned**, we are on schedule."

## Latihan
1. Buat kalimat dengan "in the context of" tentang pendidikan.
2. Gunakan "on the other hand" untuk menunjukkan kontras.
3. Lengkapi: "___ is worth noting that ___."
4. Bedakan "in terms of" vs "in light of".
5. Tulis 3 kalimat menggunakan lexical bundles berbeda.
