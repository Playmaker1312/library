# 233. Figurative Language: Metaphor, Simile, Personification, Hyperbole

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai bahasa figuratif untuk memperkaya tulisan dan pidato. Target: membedakan dan menggunakan metaphor, simile, personification, dan hyperbole secara efektif.

## Analogi
Seperti bumbu dapur — masing-masing memberi rasa berbeda pada hidangan bahasa Anda.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Figure | Definisi | Contoh |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Metaphor | Perbandingan langsung tanpa like/as | "Time is a thief." |
| Simile | Perbandingan dengan like/as | "Her voice was like velvet." |
| Personification | Benda mati diberi sifat manusia | "The wind whispered through the trees." |
| Hyperbole | Melebih-lebihkan | "I've told you a million times." |

Penggunaan dalam konteks profesional:
- Metaphor: "The company is a well-oiled machine."
- Simile: "The market reacted like a startled deer."
- Personification: "This software has a mind of its own."
- Hyperbole: "This is the best solution in the entire world."

## Contoh
1. "The economy is **a roller coaster** this year." (metaphor)
2. "The new policy spread through the organization **like wildfire**." (simile)
3. "The algorithm **refuses** to cooperate today." (personification)

## Latihan
1. Buat metaphor untuk mendeskripsikan startup.
2. Buat simile untuk menggambarkan pertumbuhan ekonomi.
3. Identifikasi: "The sun smiled down on us." — jenis?
4. Buat hyperbole tentang deadline yang ketat.
5. Ubah simile menjadi metaphor: "He fought like a lion."
