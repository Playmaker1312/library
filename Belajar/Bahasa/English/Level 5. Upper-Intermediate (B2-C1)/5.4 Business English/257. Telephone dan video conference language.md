# 257. Telephone dan Video Conference Language

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai bahasa untuk komunikasi telepon dan video conference dalam konteks bisnis. Target: mampu melakukan panggilan bisnis dan video meeting secara profesional.

## Analogi
Seperti bermain radio — tanpa bahasa tubuh, Anda hanya mengandalkan suara dan kata-kata. Video conference setengahnya bergantung pada visual.

## Poin-Poin Penting
- Telephone: ucapan jelas, konfirmasi, recap
- Video: kamera, lighting, mute discipline
- Handling technical issues gracefully
- Active listening: "I see", "Right", "Uh-huh"
- Struktur panggilan: opening → purpose → body → closing

## Struktur & Strategi
**Telephone:**
1. "Hello, this is [name] from [company]."
2. "I'm calling regarding/about..."
3. "Could you repeat that, please?"
4. "Let me confirm what I've heard..."
5. "Thank you. I'll follow up via email."

**Video conference:**
- "Can everyone see my screen?"
- "You seem to be on mute."
- "Could we go back to the previous slide?"
- "I'll put that in the chat."

## Contoh
"**Hello, this is Andi from TechCorp. I'm calling regarding** the invoice we sent last week. **I just wanted to confirm** that you received it. **Could you check on your end**? ... **Great, I'll send a copy via email as well.** "

## Latihan
1. Tulis opening script untuk panggilan bisnis ke klien baru.
2. Praktik meminta seseorang mengulangi di telepon.
3. Buat frasa untuk mengatasi technical glitch di Zoom.
4. Role-play: konfirmasi detail pesanan via telepon.
5. Tulis follow-up email setelah video conference.
