# 227. Advanced Linking Devices dan Discourse Organization

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai cohesive devices tingkat lanjut untuk mengorganisir wacana secara logis dan profesional. Target: menulis paragraf yang koheren dengan transisi yang mulus.

## Analogi
Seperti rambu lalu lintas — memberi tahu pembaca kemana arah argumen selanjutnya.

## Pola & Struktur

| Fungsi | Linking Device |
|--------|---------------|
| Menambahkan argumen | Furthermore, Moreover, In addition, Additionally |
| Kontras | Nevertheless, Nonetheless, Conversely, On the contrary |
| Sebab-akibat | Consequently, As a result, Accordingly, Hence |
| Konsesi | Granted, Admittedly, Even so, Despite this |
| Menyimpulkan | In conclusion, To summarize, Ultimately, On the whole |
| Mengurutkan | Subsequently, Meanwhile, Eventually, Previously |

## Contoh
1. "The initial results were promising. Nevertheless, further testing revealed significant flaws."
2. "The company faced financial difficulties. Consequently, restructuring was inevitable."
3. "Granted, the method has limitations. Even so, it remains the most practical solution."

## Latihan
1. Hubungkan dua kalimat dengan "nevertheless".
2. Pilih linking device yang tepat untuk menambahkan argumen.
3. Tulis paragraf 3 kalimat dengan "furthermore" dan "consequently".
4. Bedakan penggunaan "on the contrary" vs "in contrast".
5. Ganti "but" dengan linking device yang lebih formal.
