# 226. Complex Noun Phrases

## Definisi & Target
Membangun noun phrases yang panjang dan kompleks dengan pre-modifiers (adjective, noun adjunct) dan post-modifiers (prepositional phrase, relative clause). Target: menulis kalimat akademik dengan noun phrase yang padat informasi.

## Analogi
Seperti membuat burger bertingkat — semakin banyak lapisan (modifier), semakin kaya informasinya.

## Pola & Struktur

```
[DETERMINER] + [ADJECTIVES] + [NOUN ADJUNCT] + HEAD NOUN + [POST-MODIFIER]
```

Contoh struktur:
- "The rapidly growing technology industry in Southeast Asia"
- "A highly controversial government policy regarding data privacy"
- "The recently published research paper on artificial intelligence"

## Contoh
1. "The increasingly competitive global job market requires continuous upskilling."
2. "A meticulously designed three-phase experimental study was conducted."
3. "The long-term economic implications of climate change policy remain unclear."

## Latihan
1. Perluas "the report" menjadi complex noun phrase (min 5 kata).
2. Bedakan makna: "English teacher" vs "English literature teacher".
3. Tambahkan post-modifier ke "the methodology".
4. Buat noun phrase dengan 3 pre-modifiers + head + 1 post-modifier.
5. Identifikasi head noun: "The rapidly changing political landscape in developing nations."
