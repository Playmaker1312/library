# 228. Grammatical Metaphor

## Definisi & Target
Memahami grammatical metaphor — fenomena di mana makna gramatikal diekspresikan dengan struktur yang berbeda dari bentuk "congruent" (biasa). Target: menggunakan grammatical metaphor untuk tulisan akademik yang lebih canggih.

## Analogi
Seperti mengatakan "Cahaya membanjiri ruangan" — kita menggunakan metafora leksikal. Grammatical metaphor adalah versi gramatikalnya: mengubah proses menjadi benda.

## Pola & Struktur

| Congruent (biasa) | Metaphorical (grammatical metaphor) |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Verb → Noun | "We analyzed" → "The analysis" |
| Conjunction → Verb | "Because" → "Caused" |
| Clause → Noun phrase | "The population grew" → "Population growth" |
| Adjective → Noun | "It is accurate" → "The accuracy" |

## Contoh
1. "The discovery of penicillin revolutionized medicine." (Bukan "Penicillin was discovered and this revolutionized medicine.")
2. "A significant increase in temperature was observed." (Bukan "The temperature increased significantly.")
3. "The failure of the policy resulted from inadequate planning."

## Latihan
1. Ubah "Because the data was insufficient, we postponed the study" menjadi grammatical metaphor.
2. Identifikasi grammatical metaphor dalam: "The implementation of the new system caused delays."
3. Ubah congruent ke metaphorical: "They investigated the cause."
4. Tulis ulang "We concluded that growth is slowing" dengan nominalization.
5. Bedakan dampak stilistik antara congruent dan metaphorical.
