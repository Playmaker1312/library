# 222. Advanced Modal Verbs (Nuansa Halus: Shall, Dare, Need)

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai modal verbs yang jarang dipakai (shall, dare, need) serta perbedaan nuansa halus antara must, have to, should, ought to, dan could. Target: menggunakan modal untuk makna sosial dan logis yang presisi.

## Analogi
Seperti tombol di dashboard mobil — ada yang jarang disentuh (hazard light) tapi penting dalam situasi tertentu.

## Pola & Struktur

| Modal | Makna | Contoh |
|-------|-------|--------|
| Shall (suggestion/offer) | Tawaran formal | "Shall we proceed?" |
| Dare (challenge) | Keberanian | "How dare you speak to me like that?" |
| Need (necessity) | Kebutuhan | "You need not worry." |
| Must not vs Don't have to | Larangan vs tidak perlu | "You must not enter." vs "You don't have to enter." |

## Contoh
1. "Shall I open the window?" — Tawaran bantuan yang sangat formal.
2. "I dare say he is correct." — Saya berani mengatakan dia benar (opinion softener).
3. "You needn't have brought anything." — Tidak perlu (sudah terjadi, tapi sebenarnya tidak perlu).
4. "He couldn't have said that — it doesn't make sense." — Ketidakpercayaan logis.

## Latihan
1. Bedakan "You must not go" dan "You don't have to go".
2. Buat kalimat dengan "shall" untuk suggestion.
3. Buat kalimat negative dengan "need" (needn't).
4. Terjemahkan "Beraninya kau!" menggunakan dare.
5. Pilih modal yang tepat: "You ___ (should/must/have to) see a doctor immediately."
