# 225. Advanced Conditionals (Implicated Conditionals, Kondisional Tanpa If)

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai conditional sentences tingkat lanjut termasuk implied conditionals (tanpa if) dan mixed conditionals. Target: mengekspresikan kondisi dengan struktur yang lebih bervariasi dan alami.

## Analogi
Seperti bermain catur — tidak selalu harus membuka dengan langkah yang sama. Ada banyak cara untuk menyampaikan "jika-maka" tanpa kata "jika".

## Pola & Struktur

| Struktur | Makna | Contoh |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Inverted conditional | Formal, tanpa if | "Had I known, I would have come." |
| Should you... | Saran/kemungkinan | "Should you need assistance, call me." |
| Were it not for | Kontrafaktual | "Were it not for the rain, we would go." |
| Unless/Provided/As long as | Kondisional alternatif | "You may go provided you finish." |
| Implied (but for, otherwise) | Tersirat | "But for your help, I would fail." |

## Contoh
1. "Had she arrived earlier, she would have caught the train." (Inverted third conditional)
2. "Were I in your position, I would accept the offer." (Inverted second conditional)
3. "But for the scholarship, I couldn't afford tuition." (Implied conditional)

## Latihan
1. Ubah "If I had known, I would have helped" menjadi inverted.
2. Buat kalimat dengan "Were it not for" tentang cuaca.
3. Tulis conditional tanpa if menggunakan "otherwise".
4. Lengkapi: "Should you ___ (have) any questions, ___."
5. Bedakan makna: "Unless you try" vs "If you don't try".
