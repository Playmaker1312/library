# 47. Menanyakan dan memberi arah (Asking and giving directions)

## Definisi & Target
Kemampuan bertanya dan menunjukkan arah. Target: mampu menanyakan lokasi dan memberi petunjuk arah sederhana.

## Analogi
Seperti menggunakan GPS — kamu perlu tahu cara meminta dan memberi petunjuk jalan.

## Poin-Poin Penting
- Bertanya: "Excuse me, where is...?" / "How do I get to...?"
- Memberi arah: "Go straight." / "Turn left/right."
- Jarak: "It is 100 meters from here."
- Tengaran: "It is next to the bank."

## Langkah-Langkah

**Menanyakan Arah:**
1. "Excuse me, **where is the [tempat]**?"
2. "**How do I get to** [tempat]?"
3. "**Is it far from here**?"
4. "**Thank you**!"

**Memberi Arah:**
1. "**Go straight** for about 100 meters."
2. "**Turn left** at the traffic light."
3. "**Turn right** after the supermarket."
4. "It is **on your left/right**."
5. "You can't miss it."

**Kosakata Arah:**
- Turn left = belok kiri
- Turn right = belok kanan
- Go straight = lurus
- Traffic light = lampu merah
- Intersection / crossroads = simpang
- Corner = pojok / tikungan

## Contoh
**You**: "Excuse me, where is the nearest hospital?"
**Local**: "Go straight for 200 meters, then turn left. The hospital is on your right."
**You**: "Thank you!"
**Local**: "You're welcome!"

## Latihan
1. Tulis cara bertanya: "Di mana stasiun kereta?"
2. Beri arah dari kelas ke kantin dalam Bahasa Inggris.
3. Apa arti: "Go straight, turn right, it's on your left."
4. Praktikkan dengan teman: satu tanya, satu jawab.
