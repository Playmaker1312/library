# 46. Percakapan saat berbelanja

## Definisi & Target
Kemampuan berbelanja dalam Bahasa Inggris. Target: mampu menanyakan harga, ukuran, warna, dan membeli barang.

## Analogi
Seperti di pasar atau mall — kamu bertanya, memilih, lalu membayar.

## Poin-Poin Penting
- Tanya harga: "How much is this?"
- Tanya ukuran: "Do you have this in size M?"
- Tanya warna: "Do you have it in red?"
- Memutuskan: "I'll take it." / "I'll buy it."

## Langkah-Langkah
1. **Melihat Barang**: "Excuse me, how much is this?"
2. **Tanya Ukuran/Warna**: "Do you have this in [size/color]?"
3. **Mencoba**: "Can I try this on?" (untuk baju)
4. **Memutuskan**: "I'll take it." / "I'll buy it."
5. **Membayar**: "Here is the money." / "Can I pay by card?"
6. **Penutup**: "Thank you!" / "Have a nice day!"

**Frasa Berguna:**
- "It is too expensive." — Terlalu mahal.
- "Is there a discount?" — Ada diskon?
- "I am just looking." — Saya lihat-lihat saja.
- "Do you have a smaller size?" — Ada ukuran lebih kecil?

## Contoh
**You**: "Excuse me, how much is this T-shirt?"
**Seller**: "It is Rp150,000."
**You**: "Do you have it in blue?"
**Seller**: "Yes, here it is."
**You**: "Great, I'll take it. Here is the money."
**Seller**: "Thank you!"

## Latihan
1. Buat dialog belanja (4-6 baris) antara pembeli dan penjual.
2. Tulis cara menanyakan harga dalam Bahasa Inggris.
3. Bagaimana bilang "Saya lihat-lihat saja"?
4. Praktikkan: minta ukuran yang lebih kecil.
