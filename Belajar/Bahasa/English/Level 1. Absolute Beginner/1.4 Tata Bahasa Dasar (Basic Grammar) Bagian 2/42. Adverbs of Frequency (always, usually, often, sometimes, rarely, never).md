# 42. Adverbs of Frequency (always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never)

## Definisi & Target
**Adverbs of frequency** memberi tahu seberapa sering suatu aksi terjadi. Target: menggunakan adverb of frequency dengan tepat dalam kalimat.

## Analogi
Seperti skala dari 0 sampai 100% — *never* = 0%, *always* = 100%.

## Pola & Struktur

**Tingkat Frekuensi:**
| Adverb | Arti | Persentase |
|--------|------|-----------|
| **Always** | Selalu | 100% |
| **Usually** | Biasanya | 90% |
| **Often** | Sering | 70% |
| **Sometimes** | Kadang-kadang | 50% |
| **Rarely** | Jarang | 20% |
| **Never** | Tidak pernah | 0% |

**Posisi dalam Kalimat:**
1. **Sebelum** verb utama: "I **always** eat breakfast."
2. **Setelah** to be: "She **is always** happy."
3. Setelah modal verbs: "You can **always** call me."

**Pertanyaan:** "How often do you exercise?" — "I **sometimes** exercise."

## Contoh
- "I **always** brush my teeth before bed."
- "She **usually** wakes up at 6 AM."
- "We **sometimes** eat out on weekends."
- "He **never** smokes."
- "They **rarely** watch TV."

## Latihan
1. Isi adverb: "I ___ (selalu) drink water."
2. Isi: "She ___ (tidak pernah) eats meat."
3. Susun ulang: "go / I / often / to the park" → "I often go to the park."
4. Jawab: "How often do you study English?" (gunakan adverb)
5. Benerin: "I drink always coffee." (salah — always drink)
