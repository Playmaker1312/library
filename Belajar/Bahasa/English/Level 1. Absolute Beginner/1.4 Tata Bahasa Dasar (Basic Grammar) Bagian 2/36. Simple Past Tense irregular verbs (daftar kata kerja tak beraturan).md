# 36. Simple Past Tense — irregular verbs (daftar kata kerja tak beraturan)

## Definisi & Target
**Irregular verbs** tidak mengikuti aturan -ed. Bentuk past-nya berubah sendiri. Target: menghafal irregular verbs yang paling umum.

## Analogi
Seperti kata "anak" jadi "anak-anak" — bukan "anak-anakan". Ada pola sendiri yang harus dihafal.

## Pola & Struktur

**Daftar Irregular Verbs Paling Umum**

| Verb 1 (Present) | Verb 2 (Past) | Arti |
|-----------------|---------------|------|
| go | **went** | pergi |
| eat | **ate** | makan |
| drink | **drank** | minum |
| sleep | **slept** | tidur |
| have | **had** | punya |
| do | **did** | melakukan |
| say | **said** | mengatakan |
| see | **saw** | melihat |
| come | **came** | datang |
| take | **took** | mengambil |
| give | **gave** | memberi |
| make | **made** | membuat |
| buy | **bought** | membeli |
| think | **thought** | berpikir |
| meet | **met** | bertemu |
| read | **read** (diucapkan "red") | membaca |
| write | **wrote** | menulis |
| speak | **spoke** | berbicara |
| sit | **sat** | duduk |
| stand | **stood** | berdiri |
| run | **ran** | berlari |
| swim | **swam** | berenang |
| sing | **sang** | bernyanyi |
| teach | **taught** | mengajar |
| bring | **brought** | membawa |

**Negatif & Tanya** tetap pakai **didn't** + Verb 1:
- "I **didn't go**." (bukan *I didn't went*)
- "**Did** you **eat**?" (bukan *Did you ate*)

## Contoh
- "Yesterday, I **went** to the market."
- "She **ate** an apple for breakfast."
- "We **saw** a movie last night."
- "They **bought** a new car."

## Latihan
1. Tulis past tense dari: *go, eat, have, see, buy, write, speak*.
2. Isi: "Yesterday, I ___ (go) to school."
3. Isi: "She ___ (eat) breakfast at 7."
4. Benerin: "I didn't went to the party." (salah — didn't go)
5. Buat 3 kalimat menggunakan irregular verbs di past tense.
