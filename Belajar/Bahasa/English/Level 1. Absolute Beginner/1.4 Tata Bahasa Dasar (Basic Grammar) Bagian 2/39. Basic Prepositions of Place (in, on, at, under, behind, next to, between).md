# 39. Basic Prepositions of Place (in, on, at, under, behind, next to, between)

## Definisi & Target
**Prepositions of place** menunjukkan posisi sesuatu. Target: menggunakan *in, on, at, under, behind, next to, between* dengan tepat.

## Analogi
Seperti petunjuk posisi — "di dalam", "di atas", "di bawah." Membantu menjelaskan di mana benda berada.

## Pola & Struktur

| Preposition | Fungsi | Contoh |
|------------|--------|--------|
| **In** | Di dalam (ruang, area, kota, negara) | **In** the room, **in** Jakarta |
| **On** | Di atas permukaan (menempel) | **On** the table, **on** the wall |
| **At** | Di titik spesifik (alamat, lokasi) | **At** the door, **at** school |
| **Under** | Di bawah | **Under** the bed |
| **Behind** | Di belakang | **Behind** the door |
| **Next to** | Di samping | **Next to** the bank |
| **Between** | Di antara (dua benda) | **Between** the chairs |

**Catatan Penting:**
- *In* untuk ruang tertutup / area luas: *in the box, in the city*
- *On* untuk permukaan: *on the floor, on the page*
- *At* untuk titik spesifik: *at the bus stop, at the corner*

## Contoh
- "The book is **on** the table."
- "She is **in** the kitchen."
- "He sits **next to** me."
- "The cat is **under** the chair."
- "The bank is **between** the post office and the market."

## Latihan
1. Isi *in, on, at*: "The pen is ___ the desk."
2. Isi: "She lives ___ Jakarta."
3. Isi: "He is ___ the bus stop."
4. Deskripsikan posisi 3 benda di ruanganmu.
5. Bedakan *in* dan *on* — beri contoh masing-masing.
