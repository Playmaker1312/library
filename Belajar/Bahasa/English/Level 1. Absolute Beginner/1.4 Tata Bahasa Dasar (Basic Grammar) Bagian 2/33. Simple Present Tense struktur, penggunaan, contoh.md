# 33. Simple Present Tense — struktur, penggunaan, contoh

## Definisi & Target
**Simple Present Tense** digunakan untuk kebiasaan, fakta umum, dan jadwal tetap. Target: membentuk kalimat positif, negatif, dan tanya dalam simple present.

## Analogi
Seperti rutinitas harian — "Saya bangun jam 5 setiap pagi." Itu terjadi berulang, bukan hanya sekali.

## Pola & Struktur

**Positif (+)**
| Subjek | Verb | Contoh |
|--------|------|--------|
| I/You/We/They | Verb 1 | I **eat** breakfast. |
| He/She/It | Verb 1 + s/es | She **eats** breakfast. |

**Aturan -s/-es:**
- Umum: +s (*eat → eats*)
- -ch, -sh, -s, -x, -o: +es (*go → goes, wash → washes*)
- Konsonan + y: -y + ies (*study → studies*)

**Negatif (-)**
- I/You/We/They: **don't** + Verb 1
- He/She/It: **doesn't** + Verb 1 (verb kembali ke bentuk dasar)
  - "He **doesn't eat** meat."

**Tanya (?)**
- **Do** + I/You/We/They + Verb 1?
- **Does** + He/She/It + Verb 1?

## Contoh
- "I **drink** coffee every morning." (kebiasaan)
- "The sun **rises** in the east." (fakta)
- "She **doesn't like** spicy food." (negatif)
- "**Do** you **speak** English?" (tanya)

## Latihan
1. Isi verb yang benar: "He ___ (go) to school every day."
2. Ubah ke negatif: "I like coffee." → "I don't like coffee."
3. Ubah ke tanya: "She studies English." → "Does she study English?"
4. Benerin: "He go to school." (salah — goes)
5. Buat 3 kalimat tentang rutinitas harianmu.
