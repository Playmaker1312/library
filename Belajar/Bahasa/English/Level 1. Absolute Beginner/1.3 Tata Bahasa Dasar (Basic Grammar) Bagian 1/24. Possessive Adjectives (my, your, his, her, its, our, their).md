# 24. Possessive Adjectives (my, your, his, her, its, our, their)

## Definisi & Target
**Possessive adjectives** menunjukkan kepemilikan. Target: menyatakan milik dengan tepat menggunakan kata ganti kepunyaan.

## Analogi
Seperti label "milik" — memberi tahu siapa pemilik suatu benda. "Ini bukuku" = "This is **my** book."

## Pola & Struktur

| Pronoun | Possessive Adj | Arti | Contoh |
|---------|---------------|------|--------|
| I | **my** | milik saya | **my** book |
| you | **your** | milik kamu | **your** pen |
| he | **his** | milik dia (lk) | **his** car |
| she | **her** | milik dia (pr) | **her** bag |
| it | **its** | milik benda/hewan | **its** tail |
| we | **our** | milik kami/kita | **our** house |
| they | **their** | milik mereka | **their** school |

**Aturan Penting:**
- Possessive adjective SELALU diikuti noun (benda)
- Tidak ada bentuk jamak: *my books* (bukan *my's books*)
- **Its** tanpa apostrof — berbeda dengan **it's** (it is)

## Contoh
- "This is **my** pencil."
- "**Her** name is Sari."
- "They love **their** parents."
- "The cat washes **its** face."

## Latihan
1. Isi: "I have a pen. It is ___ pen." (my)
2. Terjemahkan: "Rumah mereka besar."
3. Bedakan *its* dan *it's* dalam kalimat.
4. Pilih: "(His/Her) ___ name is Budi." (laki-laki)
5. Benerin: "This is she book." (salah)
