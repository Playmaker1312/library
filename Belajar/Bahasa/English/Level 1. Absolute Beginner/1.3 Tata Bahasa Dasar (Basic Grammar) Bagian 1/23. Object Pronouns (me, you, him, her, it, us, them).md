# 23. Object Pronouns (me, you, him, her, it, us, them)

## Definisi & Target
**Object pronouns** adalah kata ganti yang berfungsi sebagai objek (penerima aksi). Target: membedakan subject dan object pronouns.

## Analogi
Subject = yang memberi. Object = yang menerima. "**I** (subject) kick **him** (object)."

## Pola & Struktur

| Subject | Object | Contoh |
|---------|--------|--------|
| I | **me** | She sees **me**. |
| you | **you** | I call **you**. |
| he | **him** | We like **him**. |
| she | **her** | He helps **her**. |
| it | **it** | I buy **it**. |
| we | **us** | They meet **us**. |
| they | **them** | She loves **them**. |

**Aturan Penting:**
- Object pronoun selalu setelah verb atau preposition
- **You** sama bentuknya untuk subject dan object
- **It** juga sama bentuknya
- Contoh setelah preposition: "Give it to **me**."

## Contoh
- "Please call **me** tomorrow." (object — saya yang dipanggil)
- "I saw **her** at the market." (object — dia yang dilihat)
- "The teacher helps **us**." (object — kami yang dibantu)

## Latihan
1. Ganti objek dengan pronoun: "I see Budi" → "I see **him**."
2. Ganti: "She helps my sister and me" → "She helps **us**."
3. Pilih: "Please give the book to (I/me) ___."
4. Bedakan: "I call she" (salah) → "I call **her**" (benar).
5. Terjemahkan: "Dia (laki-laki) mencintai dia (perempuan)."
