# 161. Diskusi dan debat sederhana

## Definisi & Target
Berpartisipasi dalam diskusi dan debat sederhana dalam bahasa Inggris — menyampaikan pendapat, menanggapi argumen, dan mempertahankan posisi.

## Analogi
Seperti **obrolan seru** di warung kopi — bedanya ada topik dan struktur. Kamu harus dengar pendapat orang lain lalu merespons dengan sopan.

## Poin-Poin Penting

### Frasa untuk Diskusi
| Fungsi | Frasa |
|--------|-------|
| **Memulai** | "Shall we start with...?" |
| **Pendapat** | "In my opinion... / I think... / As far as I'm concerned..." |
| **Setuju** | "I agree with you. / That's a good point. / You're right." |
| **Tidak setuju (sopan)** | "I see your point, but... / I'm not sure I agree. / With respect, I think..." |
| **Menambahkan** | "Building on what you said... / Another point is..." |
| **Meminta klarifikasi** | "What do you mean by that? / Could you elaborate?" |

### Struktur Argumen Sederhana
```
1. State your opinion (I believe that...)
2. Give a reason (because...)
3. Give an example (for example...)
4. Conclude (that's why I think...)
```

## Langkah-Langkah
1. Dengar pendapat orang lain dulu  
2. Gunakan frasa sopan — jangan langsung "You're wrong!"
3. Beri alasan, bukan cuma opini
4. Minta pendapat orang lain: "What do you think?"
5. Ringkas diskusi: "So, we agree that..."

## Contoh Debat Mini
**Topik:** "Should students have homework?"
**Pro:** "I believe homework is important because it reinforces what we learn in class. For example, math practice at home helps students remember formulas."
**Kontra:** "I see your point, but I think students need time to relax. Too much homework can cause stress."

## Latihan
1. Pilih topik debat: "Social media is bad for teenagers." Siapkan 2 argumen.
2. Beri tanggapan setuju sopan: "I agree because..."
3. Beri tanggapan tidak setuju sopan: "I see your point, but..."
4. Praktik debat 2 menit dengan teman tentang topik sederhana.
5. Gunakan frasa minta klarifikasi: "What do you mean by...?"
