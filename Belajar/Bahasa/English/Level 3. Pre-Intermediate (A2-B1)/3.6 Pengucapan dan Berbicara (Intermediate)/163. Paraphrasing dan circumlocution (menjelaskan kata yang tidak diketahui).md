# 163. Paraphrasing dan circumlocution (menjelaskan kata yang tidak diketahui)

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai teknik **paraphrasing** (mengatakan ulang dengan kata berbeda) dan **circumlocution** (menjelaskan kata yang tidak diketahui dengan kata lain). Skill penting saat "blank" di tengah percakapan.

## Analogi
Seperti **Tebak kata** — saat lupa kata "refrigerator," kamu bisa bilang "the thing in the kitchen where you keep food cold." Itulah circumlocution.

## Poin-Poin Penting

### Teknik Paraphrasing
| Cara | Contoh |
|------|--------|
| **Synonyms** | big → large, difficult → challenging |
| **Change word form** | She sings beautifully → Her singing is beautiful |
| **Change sentence structure** | Active → Passive: "He wrote the book" → "The book was written by him" |
| **Use general → specific** | fruit → apple, vehicle → car |

### Teknik Circumlocution
| Situasi | Frasa |
|---------|-------|
| Lupa kata benda | "It's a thing that you use to..." |
| Lupa kata kerja | "It's when you..." |
| Lupa adjective | "It's kind of like..." |
| Lupa orang/profesi | "Someone who..." |
| Lupa tempat | "A place where..." |

### Useful Phrases
| Fungsi | Frasa |
|--------|-------|
| Paraphrasing | "In other words... / That is to say... / What I mean is..." |
| Circumlocution | "It's like... / You know, the thing that... / I can't remember the word, but it's..." |
| Checking | "Do you know what I mean? / Does that make sense?" |

## Langkah-Langkah
1. Jika lupa kata, jangan panik — gunakan kata lain yang mirip
2. Deskripsikan fungsi atau penampilan benda
3. Gunakan gesture untuk membantu
4. Minta bantuan: "What's the word for...?"
5. Parafrase kalimat sendiri jika lawan bicara bingung

## Contoh
**Lupa kata "umbrella":** "You know, the thing you hold over your head when it rains? It keeps you dry."
**Paraphrase:** "The weather was terrible" → "The weather was really bad and unpleasant."

## Latihan
1. Jelaskan "toothbrush" tanpa menyebut kata itu — gunakan circumlocution.
2. Parafrase: "She is very intelligent" (3 cara berbeda).
3. Lupa kata "microwave" — bagaimana menjelaskannya?
4. Parafrase kalimat: "I need to buy groceries" dengan kata lain.
5. Praktik: teman menyebut kata, kamu harus menjelaskannya tanpa kata itu.
