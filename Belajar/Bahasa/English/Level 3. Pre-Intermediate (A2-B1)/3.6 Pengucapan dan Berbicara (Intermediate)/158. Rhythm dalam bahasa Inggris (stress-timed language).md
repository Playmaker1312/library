# 158. Rhythm dalam bahasa Inggris (stress-timed language)

## Definisi & Target
Memahami bahwa bahasa Inggris adalah **stress-timed language** — ritme ditentukan oleh jumlah **stressed syllables**, bukan jumlah suku kata.

## Analogi
Seperti **drum** — hanya ketukan (stressed words) yang penting. Kata-kata tidak penting (function words) dilewatkan cepat seperti nada hening di antara ketukan.

**Bandingkan:** Bahasa Indonesia adalah syllable-timed (setiap suku kata sama panjang). Inggris: "I **WENT** to the **STORE** to **BUY** some **MILK**" — hanya 4 ketukan, sisanya cepat.

## Poin-Poin Penting

### Aturan Rhythm
- **Content words** (kata isi) = stressed: nouns, main verbs, adjectives, adverbs
- **Function words** (kata fungsi) = unstressed: prepositions, articles, pronouns, auxiliaries
- Waktu antarkedua stress relatif sama

### Contoh Rhythm Pattern
| Kalimat | Jumlah suku kata | Jumlah ketukan | Waktu |
|---------|-----------------|----------------|-------|
| Dogs eat meat. | 3 | 3 | sama |
| The dogs have eaten the meat. | 7 | 3 | sama! |
| I will have been waiting. | 6 | 2 | 2 ketukan |

### Latihan Ketukan (tap your hand)
```
  TAP    TAP       TAP    TAP
  I     WENT    to the  STORE
  (tap) (tap)   - -     (tap)
```

## Langkah-Langkah
1. Tepuk meja setiap stressed syllable
2. Function words diucapkan cepat di antara tepukan
3. Latihan: ucapkan "bread and butter" dalam 1 ketukan untuk "and"
4. Dengarkan musik Inggris — perhatikan ritme stres

## Latihan
1. Tandai stressed words: "I want to buy a new car."
2. Berapa jumlah ketukan dalam: "She has been studying English for two years."
3. Mengapa "The dog ate the bone" dan "Dogs eat bones" punya durasi mirip?
4. Ucapkan kalimat sambil tepuk meja di stressed words.
5. Bandingkan "I saw him" (3 kata, 2 stress) vs "I have seen him" (4 kata, 1 stress).
