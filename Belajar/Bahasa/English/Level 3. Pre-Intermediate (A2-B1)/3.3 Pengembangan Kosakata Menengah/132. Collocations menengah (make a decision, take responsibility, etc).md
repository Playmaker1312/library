# 132. Collocations menengah (make a decision, take responsibility, etc)

## Definisi & Target
Memahami collocations — pasangan kata yang selalu muncul bersama dalam bahasa Inggris alami. Penting untuk terdengar natural bukan seperti terjemahan langsung.

## Analogi
Seperti **mie dengan kuah** — orang Indonesia bilang "makan mie" bukan "memakan mie." Demikian juga "I miss you" bukan "I feel the absence of you." Collocation adalah pasangan yang terbukti alami.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

### Verb + Noun Collocations
| Collocation | Arti |
|-------------|------|
| **Make a decision** | Mengambil keputusan |
| **Take responsibility** | Bertanggung jawab |
| **Do business** | Berbisnis |
| **Have a conversation** | Bercakap-cakap |
| **Give a presentation** | Memberi presentasi |
| **Pay attention** | Memerhatikan |
| **Keep a promise** | Menepati janji |
| **Break the law** | Melanggar hukum |

### Adjective + Noun
| Collocation | Arti |
|-------------|------|
| **Heavy rain** | Hujan deras |
| **Strong coffee** | Kopi kental |
| **Fast food** | Makanan cepat saji |
| **High temperature** | Suhu tinggi |

### Misc.
| Collocation | Arti |
|-------------|------|
| **Make a mistake** | Membuat kesalahan |
| **Do homework** | Mengerjakan PR |
| **Take a break** | Istirahat |
| **Have a good time** | Bersenang-senang |

## Latihan
1. Pilih: "I need to ______ (make/do) a decision soon."
2. Pilih: "Please ______ (pay/give) attention to the instructions."
3. Lengkapi: "I always ______ a mistake when I'm in a hurry."
4. Pilih collocation tepat: "It's ______ (strong/heavy) rain outside."
5. Koreksi kalimat ini: "I did a mistake on the test."
