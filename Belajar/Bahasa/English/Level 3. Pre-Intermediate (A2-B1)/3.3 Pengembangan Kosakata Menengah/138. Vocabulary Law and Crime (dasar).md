# 138. Vocabulary Law and Crime (dasar)

## Definisi & Target
Memperkaya kosakata bahasa Inggris dasar tentang hukum dan kejahatan.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Vocabulary | Arti | Contoh |
|------------|------|--------|
| **law** | hukum | Everyone must follow the **law**. |
| **crime** | kejahatan | Stealing is a **crime**. |
| **criminal** | penjahat | The **criminal** was arrested. |
| **victim** | korban | The **victim** called the police. |
| **witness** | saksi | A **witness** saw the accident. |
| **evidence** | bukti | The police found **evidence**. |
| **investigation** | penyelidikan | The **investigation** is ongoing. |
| **arrest** | menangkap | The police **arrested** the thief. |
| **trial** | pengadilan | The **trial** will start next month. |
| **judge** | hakim | The **judge** made a decision. |
| **lawyer** | pengacara | She hired a **lawyer**. |
| **guilty** | bersalah | The jury found him **guilty**. |
| **innocent** | tidak bersalah | She was proved **innocent**. |
| **sentence** | hukuman | He received a 5-year **sentence**. |
| **fine** | denda | He had to pay a **fine**. |
| **robbery** | perampokan | There was a bank **robbery** yesterday. |
| **burglary** | pencurian (di rumah) | **Burglary** is common in this area. |

## Latihan
1. Lengkapi: "The ______ (saksi) saw the robbery."
2. Apa beda "guilty" dan "innocent"?
3. Pilih: "He was ______ (arrested/sentenced) by the police."
4. Lengkapi: "The ______ (hakim) gave the criminal a 10-year sentence."
5. Beda "robbery" dan "burglary"?
