# 133. Confusing words (affect effect, advice advise, borrow lend, etc)

## Definisi & Target
Membedakan pasangan kata yang sering tertukar oleh pembelajar bahasa Inggris karena mirip ejaan, bunyi, atau makna.

## Analogi
Seperti **"apotek" vs "apotik"** — sering salah tapi sebenarnya beda. Dalam bahasa Inggris, "affect" dan "effect" sering tertukar bahkan oleh penutur asli.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Kata | Arti | Contoh |
|------|------|--------|
| **affect** (v) | memengaruhi | The weather **affects** my mood. |
| **effect** (n) | efek, hasil | The medicine had a good **effect**. |
| **advice** (n) | nasihat | Thank you for your **advice**. |
| **advise** (v) | menasihati | I **advise** you to study harder. |
| **borrow** (v) | meminjam (dari) | Can I **borrow** your pen? |
| **lend** (v) | meminjamkan (ke) | Can you **lend** me your pen? |
| **stationery** (n) | alat tulis | I need to buy some **stationery**. |
| **stationary** (adj) | diam, tidak bergerak | The car remained **stationary**. |
| **principal** (n) | kepala sekolah | The **principal** gave a speech. |
| **principle** (n) | prinsip | He has strong **principles**. |
| **desert** (n/ˈdezərt/) | gurun | Camels live in the **desert**. |
| **dessert** (n/dɪˈzɜːrt/) | makanan penutup | Ice cream is my favorite **dessert**. |

## Latihan
1. Pilih: "How does this ______ (affect/effect) me?"
2. Pilih: "She gave me some good ______ (advice/advise)."
3. Lengkapi: "Can I ______ (borrow/lend) your car? I'll return it tomorrow."
4. Lengkapi: "We bought some ______ (stationery/stationary) for school."
5. Pilih: "The ______ (principle/principal) of the school is very strict."
