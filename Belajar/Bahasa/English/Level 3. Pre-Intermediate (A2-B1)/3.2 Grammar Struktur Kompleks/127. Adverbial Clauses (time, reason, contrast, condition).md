# 127. Adverbial Clauses (time, reason, contrast, condition)

## Definisi & Target
Menggunakan klausa yang berfungsi sebagai **adverbia** — menerangkan kapan, mengapa, kontras, atau kondisi suatu aksi.

## Analogi
Seperti **bumbu masakan** — kalimat utama adalah bahan pokok, adverbial clause adalah bumbu yang memberi rasa: kapan (time), mengapa (reason), meskipun (contrast), atau jika (condition).

## Pola & Struktur

| Jenis | Conjunction | Contoh |
|-------|------------|--------|
| **Time** | when, while, before, after, until, as soon as | **When** I arrived, she left. |
| **Reason** | because, since, as | **Because** it was raining, we stayed home. |
| **Contrast** | although, even though, whereas, while | **Although** he is rich, he is unhappy. |
| **Condition** | if, unless, provided that | **If** you study, you will pass. |

**Aturan:** Jika adverbial clause di awal kalimat, gunakan koma.

## Contoh
| English | Indonesia |
|---------|-----------|
| I will call you **when I get home**. | Saya akan meneleponmu saat saya sampai rumah. |
| **Because she was tired**, she went to bed early. | Karena dia lelah, dia tidur lebih awal. |
| **Although he tried hard**, he failed. | Meskipun dia berusaha keras, dia gagal. |
| **Unless you hurry**, you'll miss the bus. | Kecuali kamu buru-buru, kamu akan ketinggalan bus. |
| She likes coffee **whereas I prefer tea**. | Dia suka kopi sedangkan saya lebih suka teh. |

## Latihan
1. Gabungkan dengan "because": "He passed the exam. He studied hard."
2. Gabungkan dengan "although": "She is rich. She is not happy."
3. Lengkapi: "I will wait here ______ you come back."
4. Buat kalimat dengan "unless" tentang syarat.
5. Buat kalimat dengan "while" (contrast) tentang dua hal yang berbeda.
