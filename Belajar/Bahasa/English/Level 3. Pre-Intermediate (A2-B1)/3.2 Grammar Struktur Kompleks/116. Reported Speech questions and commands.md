# 116. Reported Speech questions and commands

## Definisi & Target
Mengubah pertanyaan (questions) dan perintah (commands) langsung menjadi tidak langsung.

## Analogi
- **Direct question** = "Tanya langsung: 'Jam berapa sekarang?'"
- **Indirect question** = "Dia bertanya jam berapa" (tanpa tanda tanya, urutan kata berubah)

## Pola & Struktur

### Questions (Pertanyaan)
| Jenis | Direct | Indirect |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **Yes/No** | "Do you like coffee?" | He asked **if/whether** I liked coffee. |
| **WH-** | "Where do you live?" | She asked **where** I lived. |

**Perubahan penting:** Subjek + verb (bukan question word order). Hilangkan do/does/did.

### Commands (Perintah/permintaan)
| Direct | Indirect |
|--------|----------|
| "Sit down!" | He told me **to** sit down. |
| "Don't be late!" | She told me **not to** be late. |

## Contoh
| Direct | Indirect |
|--------|----------|
| "Are you hungry?" | He asked if I was hungry. |
| "What time does the train leave?" | She asked what time the train left. |
| "Don't touch that!" | He told me not to touch that. |
| "Please help me." | She asked me to help her. |
| "Have you ever been abroad?" | They asked whether I had ever been abroad. |

## Latihan
1. Ubah: "Where is the nearest bank?" she asked.
2. Ubah: "Do you speak English?" he asked.
3. Ubah: "Close the window!" she told me.
4. Ubah: "Don't smoke here!" the guard said.
5. Ubah: "Why are you crying?" she asked the child.
