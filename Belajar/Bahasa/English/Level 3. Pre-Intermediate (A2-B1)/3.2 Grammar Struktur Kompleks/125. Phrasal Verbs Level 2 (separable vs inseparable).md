# 125. Phrasal Verbs Level 2 (separable vs inseparable)

## Definisi & Target
Memahami perbedaan phrasal verbs yang **dapat dipisah** (separable) dan **tidak dapat dipisah** (inseparable) oleh objek.

## Analogi
- **Separable** = **Boneka bongkar pasang** — bisa dipisah dan digabung lagi. "Turn off the light" atau "Turn the light off" — sama saja.
- **Inseparable** = **Lem super** — tidak bisa dipisah. "Look after the baby" — tidak bisa "look the baby after."

## Pola & Struktur

### Separable (dapat dipisah)
| Struktur | Contoh |
|----------|--------|
| Verb + particle + noun | Turn off the TV. |
| Verb + noun + particle | Turn the TV off. |
| Verb + pronoun + particle | Turn **it** off. (wajib di tengah!) |

### Inseparable (tidak dapat dipisah)
| Struktur | Contoh |
|----------|--------|
| Verb + particle + noun | Look after your sister. |
| Verb + particle + pronoun | Look after **her**. (tidak bisa dipisah) |

### Daftar Penting
| Phrasal Verb | Separable/Inseparable | Arti |
|-------------|----------------------|------|
| turn off/on | Separable | mematikan/menyalakan |
| pick up | Separable | menjemput |
| put on/take off | Separable | memakai/melepas |
| look after | Inseparable | menjaga |
| look for | Inseparable | mencari |
| get along with | Inseparable | akur dengan |
| run into | Inseparable | tidak sengaja bertemu |

## Contoh
| English | Indonesia |
|---------|-----------|
| Please pick up the kids. / Pick the kids up. / Pick them up. | Tolong jemput anak-anak. |
| I'm looking for my keys. (NOT looking my keys for) | Saya mencari kunci saya. |
| She takes after her mother. (NOT takes her mother after) | Dia mirip ibunya. |
| He turned the proposal down. / He turned it down. | Dia menolak proposal itu. |

## Latihan
1. Mana yang benar? "Look it up" atau "Look up it"?
2. Pisahkan: "Turn off the engine." (2 cara)
3. Mengapa "I looked after him" benar tetapi "I looked him after" salah?
4. Lengkapi: "She ______ (put off) the meeting." (2 cara jika separable)
5. Identifikasi separable atau inseparable: call off, get over, break down, bring up.
