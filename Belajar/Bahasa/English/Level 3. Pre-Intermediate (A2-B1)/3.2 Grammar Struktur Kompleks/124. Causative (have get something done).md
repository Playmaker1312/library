# 124. Causative (have/get something done)

## Definisi & Target
Menyatakan bahwa kita **menyuruh orang lain** melakukan sesuatu untuk kita, bukan melakukannya sendiri.

## Analogi
Seperti pergi ke **bengkel** — "Saya **membuat motor saya diperbaiki**" artinya saya tidak memperbaikinya sendiri, tapi menyuruh montir. Causative = "menyuruh/ membuat sesuatu dikerjakan oleh orang lain."

## Pola & Struktur

| Pola | Rumus | Contoh |
|------|-------|--------|
| **have + something + done** | have + objek + V3 | I have my hair cut. |
| **get + something + done** | get + objek + V3 (lebih informal) | I get my car washed. |
| **have + someone + do** | have + person + V1 | I had the mechanic fix it. |
| **get + someone + to do** | get + person + to V1 | I got him to help me. |

## Contoh
| English | Indonesia |
|---------|-----------|
| I have my hair cut every month. | Saya potong rambut (disuruh orang) setiap bulan. |
| She had her house painted last week. | Dia mengecat rumahnya (disuruh tukang) minggu lalu. |
| I got my phone repaired. | Saya memperbaiki HP saya (di service center). |
| He had his assistant book the tickets. | Dia menyuruh asistennya memesan tiket. |

**Bandingkan:** "I cut my hair" (saya potong sendiri) vs "I have my hair cut" (saya potong rambut di salon).

## Latihan
1. Ubah: "I cut my hair at the salon." (gunakan causative have)
2. Ubah ke causative: "The mechanic fixed my car." (gunakan get)
3. Buat kalimat: "She / have / her nails / do."
4. Lengkapi: "We ______ (have) our photo ______ (take) at the studio."
5. Bedakan: "I had him clean the room" vs "I had the room cleaned."
