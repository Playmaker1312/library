# 149. Transition words dan linking expressions

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai kata penghubung (transition words) untuk membuat tulisan lebih lancar, logis, dan profesional.

## Analogi
Seperti **rambu lalu lintas** — "however" memberi tahu pembaca bahwa arah tulisan akan berbelok (kontras), "furthermore" berarti akan lurus terus (tambahan).

## Struktur Penulisan

| Fungsi | Transition Words | Contoh Penggunaan |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Addition** | moreover, furthermore, in addition, besides | "She is smart. **Moreover**, she is hardworking." |
| **Contrast** | however, on the other hand, in contrast, whereas | "I like coffee. **However**, my wife prefers tea." |
| **Cause/Effect** | therefore, consequently, as a result, thus | "He studied hard. **Therefore**, he passed." |
| **Sequence** | first, next, then, finally, meanwhile | "**First**, mix the ingredients. **Next**, bake for 30 minutes." |
| **Example** | for example, for instance, such as | "I enjoy outdoor activities, **such as** hiking." |
| **Conclusion** | in conclusion, to summarize, overall | "**In conclusion**, regular exercise improves health." |

## Contoh
| English | Indonesia |
|---------|-----------|
| The hotel was expensive. **However**, the service was excellent. | Hotelnya mahal. Namun, pelayanannya sangat baik. |
| **Furthermore**, the study shows that exercise improves memory. | Selain itu, penelitian menunjukkan olahraga meningkatkan memori. |
| **As a result** of the storm, flights were cancelled. | Akibat badai, penerbangan dibatalkan. |
| Some people enjoy city life, **whereas** others prefer the countryside. | Beberapa orang suka hidup di kota, sedangkan yang lain suka desa. |

## Latihan
1. Lengkapi: "I wanted to go out. ______, it started raining."
2. Gabungkan dengan "furthermore": "He is kind. He is generous."
3. Pilih transition word yang tepat: "She didn't study. ______ (However/Therefore), she failed."
4. Tulis 3 kalimat berurutan dengan: First... Next... Finally...
5. Bedakan: "However" vs "Moreover" — kapan pakai yang mana?
