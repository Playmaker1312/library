# 152. Menulis surat atau email formal

## Definisi & Target
Menulis surat atau email formal dalam bahasa Inggris untuk situasi resmi seperti lamaran kerja, pengaduan, atau korespondensi bisnis.

## Analogi
Seperti **pakai jas** — formal, rapi, sopan. Ada aturan yang harus diikuti. Tidak bisa pakai bahasa santai seperti ngobrol dengan teman.

## Poin-Poin Penting
- **Subject line** jelas dan singkat
- **Salam pembuka**: Dear Mr./Ms./Dr. [LastName]
- **Nada sopan**: I would like to, I am writing to, Could you please
- **Tidak pakai kontraksi**: I am (bukan I'm), I would (bukan I'd)
- **Tidak pakai slang**: banyak (bukan a lot), help (bukan give a hand)
- **Penutup**: Sincerely, Yours faithfully, Best regards

## Struktur Penulisan
```
Subject: [jelas dan singkat]

Dear Mr./Ms. [LastName],

[Paragraf 1: Perkenalan dan tujuan menulis]
I am writing to...

[Paragraf 2: Detail / penjelasan]
I would like to inform you that...

[Paragraf 3: Tindakan / permintaan]
I would appreciate it if you could...

Sincerely,
[Nama Lengkap]
```

## Contoh
**Subject:** Application for Marketing Intern Position
**Isi:** "Dear Ms. Johnson, I am writing to apply for the Marketing Intern position advertised on LinkedIn. I recently graduated from the University of Indonesia with a degree in Marketing. I have attached my CV and portfolio for your review. I would be grateful for the opportunity to discuss my qualifications further. Thank you for your consideration. Sincerely, Andi Pratama."

## Latihan
1. Tulis subject line untuk: (a) lamaran kerja, (b) pengaduan produk.
2. Tulis kalimat pembuka formal: "I want to ask about..." → ubah ke formal.
3. Tulis email formal pendek: apply for English course.
4. Ubah ke formal: "Thanks for your help! Let me know if you need anything else."
5. Tulis penutup formal untuk surat pengaduan.
