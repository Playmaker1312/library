# 154. Menulis narrative (cerita)

## Definisi & Target
Menulis teks naratif — cerita pendek dalam bahasa Inggris dengan alur, tokoh, dan latar yang jelas.

## Analogi
Seperti **mengobrol** tentang pengalaman seru — "Tau nggak, kemarin saya..." — naratif mengajak pembaca merasakan pengalaman melalui kata-kata.

## Poin-Poin Penting
- **Tenses utama**: Past Simple (aksi utama), Past Continuous (latar), Past Perfect (sebelum aksi utama)
- **Struktur**: Orientation → Complication → Resolution → Reorientation
- **Show, don't tell**: "He was angry" → "His face turned red and his fists clenched."
- **Dialogue** membuat cerita hidup
- **Time words**: first, then, after that, suddenly, finally, in the end

## Struktur Penulisan
```
Orientation (perkenalan)
  └── Who, when, where, what

Complication (masalah)
  └── Konflik atau tantangan yang muncul

Resolution (penyelesaian)
  └── Bagaimana masalah diselesaikan

Reorientation (penutup)
  └── Pelajaran / kesimpulan (opsional)
```

## Contoh
**Orientation:** "Last summer, I went to Yogyakarta with my family."
**Complication:** "On our way to Borobudur, our car broke down in the middle of nowhere."
**Resolution:** "Luckily, a kind local farmer helped us call a mechanic."
**Reorientation:** "It was a stressful moment, but it taught me that there are always kind people around."

## Latihan
1. Tulis orientation (2-3 kalimat) tentang pengalaman liburan.
2. Tulis complication (2-3 kalimat) — apa masalah yang terjadi?
3. Tulis resolution (2-3 kalimat) — bagaimana masalah selesai?
4. Ubah "telling" ke "showing": "She was scared."
5. Tulis cerita pendek lengkap (5-8 kalimat) tentang pengalaman lucu.
