# 109. Past Perfect Tense

## Definisi & Target
Menyatakan aksi yang selesai **sebelum** aksi lain di masa lalu (the past in the past). Membantu memperjelas urutan kejadian.

## Analogi
Seperti **memakai baju hujan setelah kehujanan** — urutannya terbalik. Past Perfect untuk kejadian yang "lebih dulu" dari kejadian masa lalu lainnya.

## Pola & Struktur
| Pola | Rumus |
|------|-------|
| (+) | **S + had + V3 (past participle)** |
| (-) | **S + had + not + V3** |
| (?) | **Had + S + V3?** |

## Contoh
| English | Indonesia |
|---------|-----------|
| When I arrived, the train had already left. | Saat saya tiba, keretanya sudah pergi. |
| She had finished her homework before dinner. | Dia sudah menyelesaikan PR-nya sebelum makan malam. |
| They hadn't eaten anything before the exam. | Mereka belum makan apa pun sebelum ujian. |
| Had you ever seen snow before you went to Japan? | Apakah kamu pernah melihat salju sebelum ke Jepang? |

## Latihan
1. Gabungkan: "I woke up. The sun already rose." (gunakan Past Perfect)
2. Isi: "She ______ (never/see) a whale before she visited the aquarium."
3. Ubah ke negatif: "He had locked the door before leaving."
4. Buat kalimat: "After I ______ (finish) work, I went home."
5. Jelaskan perbedaan: "When she arrived, he left" vs "When she arrived, he had left."
