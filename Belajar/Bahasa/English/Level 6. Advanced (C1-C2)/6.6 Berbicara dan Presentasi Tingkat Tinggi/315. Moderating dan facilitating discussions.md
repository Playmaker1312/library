# 315. Moderating dan Facilitating Discussions

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai keterampilan moderasi dan fasilitasi diskusi — memimpin percakapan kelompok agar produktif, inklusif, dan mencapai tujuan. Target: mampu memoderasi diskusi dalam berbagai konteks (panel, workshop, meeting).

## Analogi
Seperti wasit sepak bola — moderator tidak bermain, tapi memastikan permainan berjalan adil, lancar, dan sesuai aturan.

## Poin-Poin Penting
1. **Peran moderator**: 
   - Set the stage: introduce topic, agenda, ground rules
   - Manage time: keep on schedule
   - Ensure inclusion: draw out quiet participants, manage dominant ones
   - Guide discussion: ask questions, summarize, redirect
   - Handle conflict: de-escalate, reframe, park if needed
2. **Key techniques**:
   - *Active listening*: paraphrase, reflect, clarify
   - *Questioning*: open-ended (*What do you think?*), probing (*Can you elaborate?*), redirecting
   - *Summarizing*: *"So far we've heard three perspectives..."*
   - *Parking lot*: *"That's interesting, let's put it on the list for later"*
3. **Virtual facilitation**: mute management, chat integration, breakout rooms

## Langkah-Langkah
1. **Before**: understand topic, agenda, participant backgrounds
2. **Opening**: welcome, introduce, set norms (*"One speaker at a time"*)
3. **During**: listen actively, manage flow, conclude sections
4. **Closing**: summarize key points, action items, thank participants

## Contoh
- **Opening**: *"Welcome everyone. Today we're discussing remote work policy. Our goal is to identify 3 key recommendations by the end of this hour. Please respect the speaker and keep comments to 2 minutes."*
- **Intervention**: *"Thank you, Andi. Let's hear from someone who hasn't spoken yet. Sari, what's your perspective?"*
- **Managing conflict**: *"I see there's disagreement here. Let's reframe: what evidence do we have for each position?"*
- **Summarizing**: *"To recap, we've agreed on point A and B, but need more discussion on point C."*

## Latihan
1. Moderasi diskusi 15 menit dengan 4 orang — catat teknik yang kamu gunakan.
2. Praktikkan active listening: paraphrase apa yang baru saja dikatakan seseorang.
3. Tangani participant dominan: latih cara sopan mengalihkan bicara.
4. Buat agenda untuk diskusi panel 60 menit dengan 3 panelis.
5. Fasilitasi breakout group di meeting virtual.
