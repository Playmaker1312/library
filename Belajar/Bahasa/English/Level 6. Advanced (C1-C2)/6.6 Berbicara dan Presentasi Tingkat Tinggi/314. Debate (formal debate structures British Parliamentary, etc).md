# 314. Debate (Formal Debate Structures: British Parliamentary, etc)

## Definisi & Target
Mempelajari struktur debat formal — terutama British Parliamentary (BP) format. Target: mampu berpartisipasi dalam debat formal dengan argumentasi yang terstruktur dan rebuttal yang efektif.

## Analogi
Seperti pertandingan catur — debat bukan tentang berteriak paling keras, tapi tentang strategi, antisipasi, dan langkah yang diperhitungkan.

## Poin-Poin Penting
1. **British Parliamentary (BP) format**:
   - 4 teams of 2: Opening Government, Opening Opposition, Closing Government, Closing Opposition
   - 7-minute speeches
   - Points of Information (POI): boleh interupsi sopan
2. **Other formats**: 
   - *Lincoln-Douglas*: 1v1, value-based
   - *Policy debate*: 2v2, policy-focused, evidence-heavy
   - *Asian parliamentary*: 3v3, similar to BP
3. **Speech structure**: 
   - *Matter*: argument, evidence, logic
   - *Manner*: delivery, style, persuasion
   - *Method*: structure, time management
4. **Rebuttal**: 
   - *Attack*: "Their argument fails because..."
   - *Defense*: "Our argument still stands because..."
   - *Compare*: "Our case is stronger because X outweighs Y"

## Langkah-Langkah
1. **Case building**: define motion, establish criteria, build arguments
2. **Argument structure**: Claim → Warrant → Impact
3. **Rebuttal**: Listen → Identify flaw → Explain → Compare
4. **POI**: offer (stand up, say "Point of Information") or accept ("On that point")

## Contoh
- **Motion**: *"This House would ban single-use plastics"*
- **Proposition case**: environmental impact → feasibility → alternatives exist
- **Rebuttal**: *"While the opposition argues that banning plastics hurts businesses, studies show that the long-term cost of plastic pollution far outweighs short-term economic losses."*
- **Claim-Warrant-Impact**: *"Banning plastics reduces ocean pollution (claim). Studies show 80% of ocean plastic comes from single-use items (warrant). This directly protects marine ecosystems and human health (impact)."*

## Latihan
1. Buat argument Claim-Warrant-Impact untuk: *"Social media does more harm than good."*
2. Latih memberikan POI dalam sesi latihan 5 menit.
3. Tulis 3 rebuttals untuk argumen lawan.
4. Debat 2v2 tentang motion sederhana dengan teman.
5. Analisis video debat BP — identifikasi strategi tim pemenang.
