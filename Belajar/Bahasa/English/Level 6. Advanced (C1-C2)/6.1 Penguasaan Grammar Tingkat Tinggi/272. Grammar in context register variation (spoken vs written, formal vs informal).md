# 272. Grammar in Context: Register Variation (Spoken vs Written, Formal vs Informal)

## Definisi & Target
Memahami bagaimana pilihan grammar berubah berdasarkan konteks: lisan vs tulisan, formal vs informal, dan berbagai register lainnya. Target: mampu menyesuaikan grammar secara otomatis sesuai situasi komunikasi.

## Analogi
Seperti pakaian — kamu tidak pakai jas ke pantai, dan tidak pakai celana pendek ke pernikahan. Grammar juga punya "dress code" sendiri.

## Pola & Struktur
1. **Spoken grammar**: fragments, ellipsis, fillers — *"Coming?" "Yeah."*
2. **Written grammar**: kalimat lengkap, subordinasi kompleks
3. **Formal**: passive voice, nominalization, complex sentences
4. **Informal**: contractions, phrasal verbs, slang
5. **Academic**: hedging (*it may be suggested that*), impersonal constructions
6. **Legal**: archaic terms, long sentences, doublets (*null and void*)
7. **Digital**: abbreviations, lack of punctuation, emoji as grammar

## Contoh
- **Formal written**: *It is imperative that the committee convene at the earliest possible convenience.*
- **Informal spoken**: *We gotta meet up soon, yeah?*
- **Academic**: *It could be hypothesized that the observed phenomenon correlates with...*
- **Texting**: *u there? lol*

## Latihan
1. Ubah kalimat formal ini ke spoken informal: *I would appreciate it if you could provide the documents.*
2. Ubah kalimat spoken ini ke academic written: *It's obvious that the experiment didn't work.*
3. Identifikasi register dari 3 teks berbeda (news, chat,论文).
4. Tulis kalimat yang sama dalam 3 register berbeda.
5. Analisis perbedaan grammar antara transcript pidato dan versi tertulisnya.
