# 274. Error Analysis dan Common Advanced-Level Mistakes

## Definisi & Target
Mengidentifikasi dan memperbaiki kesalahan grammar yang paling sering dilakukan oleh pembelajar tingkat lanjut (C1-C2). Target: mampu mengoreksi diri sendiri dan memahami mengapa kesalahan tertentu terjadi.

## Analogi
Seperti dokter yang mendiagnosis penyakit — kamu harus tahu gejala (error) dan penyebabnya (misunderstanding of grammar rule) sebelum memberi obat (correction).

## Common Advanced Mistakes
1. **Artikel dengan uncountable nouns**: *an advice* → *some advice / a piece of advice*
2. **Preposition after certain verbs**: *discuss about* → *discuss* (no preposition)
3. **Tense in reported speech**: *He said he is coming* → *He said he was coming*
4. **Conditional type mixing**: *If I would have known* → *If I had known*
5. **Relative pronouns**: *The person which called* → *The person who called*
6. **Subjunctive mood**: *I suggest that he goes* → *I suggest that he go*
7. **Dangling modifiers**: *Walking home, the rain started* → *Walking home, I felt the rain*

## Contoh
- ❌ *I am looking forward to meet you.* → ✅ *I am looking forward to meeting you.* (to di sini preposition, bukan infinitive)
- ❌ *The data shows that...* → ✅ *The data show that...* (data plural)
- ❌ *Neither of them are ready.* → ✅ *Neither of them is ready.* (neither singular)

## Latihan
1. Koreksi: *She suggested me to go.*
2. Identifikasi error: *Having eaten lunch, the meeting began.*
3. Perbaiki: *If I would be you, I would take the job.*
4. Pilih yang benar: *The committee has/have decided.*
5. Analisis 5 kalimat error dari tulisan sendiri minggu ini.
