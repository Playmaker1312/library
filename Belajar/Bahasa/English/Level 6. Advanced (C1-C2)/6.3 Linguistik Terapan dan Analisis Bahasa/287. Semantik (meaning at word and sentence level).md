# 287. Semantik (Meaning at Word and Sentence Level)

## Definisi & Target
Mempelajari makna pada tingkat kata (lexical semantics) dan kalimat (compositional semantics). Target: mampu menganalisis relasi makna, ambiguitas, dan bagaimana makna dibangun dari komponen-komponennya.

## Analogi
Seperti bermain dengan prisma — kata-kata seperti cahaya putih, bisa diuraikan menjadi spektrum makna yang berbeda tergantung sudut pandang.

## Poin-Poin Penting
1. **Lexical relations**: synonymy (*big/large*), antonymy (*hot/cold*), hyponymy (*dog/animal*), meronymy (*finger/hand*)
2. **Polysemy vs Homonymy**: *bank* (tempat uang) vs *bank* (tepian sungai) — homonymy; *head* (bagian tubuh, pemimpin, kepala paku) — polysemy
3. **Sense vs Reference**: *The morning star = The evening star* — same reference, different sense (Venus)
4. **Compositional semantics**: makna kalimat = makna kata + aturan kombinasinya
5. **Semantic roles**: Agent, Patient, Theme, Experiencer, Instrument, Location
6. **Truth-conditional semantics**: *"Snow is white"* is true iff snow is white
7. **Presupposition vs Entailment**: *"The king of France is bald"* presupposes ada king of France

## Konsep Utama
- **Ambiguity**: lexical (*I saw a bat*) vs structural (*I saw the man with a telescope*)
- **Prototype theory**: kucing lebih prototypical "pet" daripada iguana
- **Semantic features**: [+HUMAN], [+MALE], [+ADULT] → *woman* = [+HUMAN, +FEMALE, +ADULT]

## Latihan
1. Bedakan polysemy dan homonymy untuk kata *run* dan *light*.
2. Analisis semantic roles dalam: *The boy broke the window with a rock.*
3. Identifikasi ambiguitas: *"Visiting relatives can be boring."*
4. Bedakan presupposition vs entailment: *"He stopped smoking."*
5. Cari hyponyms untuk kata *furniture* dan *vehicle*.
