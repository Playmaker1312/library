# 296. Membaca Fiksi Klasik (Classic Novels: Austen, Dickens, Bronte, etc)

## Definisi & Target
Mengembangkan kemampuan membaca novel klasik Inggris — memahami gaya, tema, dan konteks sosio-historisnya. Target: mampu membaca dan menikmati novel klasik dengan pemahaman mendalam.

## Analogi
Seperti mengunjungi museum hidup — novel klasik adalah jendela ke masa lalu yang masih berbicara tentang isu universal yang relevan hari ini.

## Poin-Poin Penting
1. **Jane Austen** (1775–1817): *Pride and Prejudice, Emma* — ironi, social commentary, marriage plot, free indirect discourse
2. **Charles Dickens** (1812–1870): *Great Expectations, A Tale of Two Cities* — social criticism, vivid characters, serialized publication, melodrama
3. **Bronte sisters**: Charlotte (*Jane Eyre* — gothic, feminism), Emily (*Wuthering Heights* — passion, revenge), Anne (*The Tenant of Wildfell Hall*)
4. **George Eliot** (1819–1880): *Middlemarch* — psychological realism, provincial life
5. **Thomas Hardy** (1840–1928): *Tess of the d'Urbervilles* — fatalism, rural life, criticism of Victorian morality

## Analisis
- *Pride and Prejudice* opening: *"It is a truth universally acknowledged..."* — ironi langsung tentang pernikahan
- *Great Expectations*: Pip, Miss Havisham, Estella — tema class mobility, guilt, redemption
- *Jane Eyre*: first person narrative, *"I am no bird; and no net ensnares me"* — feminist voice
- *Wuthering Heights*: frame narrative, unreliable narrators, gothic elements

## Latihan
1. Baca chapter 1 *Pride and Prejudice* — analisis ironi Austen.
2. Bandingkan gaya naratif first person (*Jane Eyre*) vs third person omniscient (*Middlemarch*).
3. Analisis satu karakter Dickens — apa yang membuatnya "Dickensian"?
4. Baca 50 halaman novel klasik dan catat vocabulary yang tidak familiar.
5. Refleksi: tema apa dari novel klasik yang masih relevan hari ini?
