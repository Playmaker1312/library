# 293. Pengenalan Sejarah Sastra Inggris (Overview Old English to Modern English)

## Definisi & Target
Memahami periodisasi sastra Inggris dari Old English hingga kontemporer, termasuk konteks historis dan karakteristik setiap periode. Target: mampu menempatkan karya sastra dalam konteks sejarahnya.

## Analogi
Seperti mempelajari peta waktu bahasa — setiap era adalah benua berbeda dengan bahasa, gaya, dan nilai yang unik.

## Poin-Poin Penting
1. **Old English (450–1066)**: *Beowulf* — epik heroik, bahasa Germanic, alliterative verse
2. **Middle English (1066–1500)**: Chaucer — *The Canterbury Tales*, pengaruh Norman French
3. **Renaissance (1500–1660)**: Shakespeare, Marlowe — drama, sonnets, humanism, blank verse
4. **Restoration & 18th Century (1660–1798)**: Pope, Swift, Defoe — satire, neoclassicism, rise of novel
5. **Romantic Period (1798–1837)**: Wordsworth, Coleridge, Blake, Keats — emotion, nature, imagination
6. **Victorian Period (1837–1901)**: Dickens, Bronte, Tennyson — social criticism, realism, moral concern
7. **Modern Period (1901–1945)**: Joyce, Woolf, Eliot — stream of consciousness, fragmentation, experimentation
8. **Contemporary (1945–present)**: diverse voices, postcolonial, digital literature

## Analisis
- **Old English**: *Hwæt! We Gar-Dena in gear-dagum, þeod-cyninga, þrym gefrunon...* — tidak bisa dibaca tanpa belajar khusus
- **Chaucer**: *Whan that Aprill with his shoures soote / The droghte of March hath perced to the roote* — masih bisa dikenali sebagai English
- **Shakespeare**: *To be, or not to be, that is the question* — Early Modern English, masih dimengerti sekarang
