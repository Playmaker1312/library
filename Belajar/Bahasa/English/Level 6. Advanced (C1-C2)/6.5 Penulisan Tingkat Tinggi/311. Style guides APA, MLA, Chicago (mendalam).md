# 311. Style Guides: APA, MLA, Chicago (Mendalam)

## Definisi & Target
Memahami dan mampu menerapkan tiga style guide utama dalam penulisan akademik dan profesional. Target: mampu memformat tulisan sesuai gaya yang diminta secara mandiri.

## Analogi
Seperti seragam — style guide menentukan "pakaian" yang harus dikenakan tulisanmu, tergantung "sekolah" (disiplin ilmu) yang kamu masuki.

## Poin-Poin Penting
1. **APA (American Psychological Association)**:
   - Bidang: social sciences, psychology, education
   - In-text: (Author, Year) — *Smith (2020) states...*
   - References: Author, A. (Year). *Title*. Publisher.
   - Headings: level 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 dengan format berbeda
2. **MLA (Modern Language Association)**:
   - Bidang: humanities, literature, language
   - In-text: (Author Page) — *Smith 23*
   - Works Cited: Author, First Name. "Title." *Journal*, vol, no, year, pages.
   - No title page: student papers
3. **Chicago Manual of Style**:
   - Bidang: history, art, publishing
   - Two systems: Notes-Bibliography (footnotes) dan Author-Date
   - Footnotes/endnotes + bibliography
   - *Chicago 17th edition*: current standard
4. **Key differences**: 
   - APA: year matters most (social sciences)
   - MLA: page numbers matter most (humanities)
   - Chicago: flexibility, comprehensive for publishing

## Struktur Penulisan
- **APA example**: *Brown, J. (2019). *The psychology of learning*. Oxford University Press.*
- **MLA example**: *Brown, James. *The Psychology of Learning*. Oxford UP, 2019.*
- **Chicago (notes)**: *1. James Brown, *The Psychology of Learning* (New York: Oxford University Press, 2019), 45.*
- **In-text APA**: *(Brown, 2019, p. 45)*
- **In-text MLA**: *(Brown 45)*
