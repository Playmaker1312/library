# 306. Creative Writing: Personal Essay dan Memoir

## Definisi & Target
Mengembangkan kemampuan menulis personal essay dan memoir — non-fiksi naratif yang menggabungkan pengalaman pribadi dengan refleksi universal. Target: mampu menulis cerita nyata yang bermakna dan engaging.

## Analogi
Seperti melihat foto album sambil bercerita — personal essay bukan sekadar menceritakan apa yang terjadi, tetapi juga apa artinya.

## Poin-Poin Penting
1. **Personal essay**: 
   - Reflective: pengalaman → makna (*"What did I learn?"*)
   - Circular structure: begins and ends in same place, but changed
   - Voice: authentic, conversational yet crafted
   - *"I"* is central but not self-indulgent
2. **Memoir**: 
   - Slice of life, bukan seluruh hidup (beda dengan autobiography)
   - Scene + reflection: show the story, then reflect on it
   - Honesty: even if unflattering
3. **Techniques**: 
   - *Scene*: show specific moments with dialogue, detail
   - *Summary*: compress time when needed
   - *Reflection*: the *now* looking back at *then*

## Struktur Penulisan
- **Hook**: start with a moment, not a explanation
- **Narrative arc**: scene → complication → climax → resolution → reflection
- **Ending**: epiphany, question, or circular return to beginning
- **Tone**: honest, vulnerable, with distance — not melodramatic

## Contoh
- **Opening**: *"My mother gave me two things that year: a typewriter and the news that she was dying."* — langsung hook
- **Reflection**: *"I thought I was learning to write. In truth, I was learning to grieve before the loss had even happened."*
- **Scene vs summary**: Scene: *"She set the kettle on the stove, her hands shaking slightly."* Summary: *"We talked for hours that winter."*

## Latihan
1. Tulis opening paragraph personal essay tentang momen penting.
2. Bedakan scene dan summary — tulis satu paragraf masing-masing tentang kejadian yang sama.
3. Tulis refleksi: apa yang kamu pelajari dari pengalaman 5 tahun lalu?
4. Analisis personal essay dari penulis terkenal (Didion, Sedaris, dll).
5. Tulis personal essay 500 kata tentang "a place that shaped you."
