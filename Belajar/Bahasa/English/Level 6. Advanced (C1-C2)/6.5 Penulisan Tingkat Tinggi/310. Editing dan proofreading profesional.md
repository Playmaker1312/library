# 310. Editing dan Proofreading Profesional

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai keterampilan editing dan proofreading — dua tahap penting dalam proses penulisan profesional. Target: mampu mengedit konten untuk clarity, consistency, dan correctness.

## Analogi
Editing seperti arsitek yang merenovasi rumah — mengubah struktur ruangan (isi, urutan, logika). Proofreading seperti tukang cat yang merapikan detail — noda kecil, garis lurus (typo, grammar, formatting).

## Poin-Poin Penting
1. **Levels of editing**:
   - *Structural editing*: organization, flow, content gaps
   - *Stylistic editing*: sentence structure, word choice, tone
   - *Copy editing*: grammar, spelling, punctuation, consistency
   - *Proofreading*: final check before publication
2. **Common errors checklist**:
   - Subject-verb agreement
   - Pronoun consistency
   - Tense consistency
   - Modifier placement
   - Punctuation (Oxford comma, semicolon)
   - Spelling (homophones: *their/there/they're*)
3. **Track changes**: menggunakan fitur review di Word/Google Docs
4. **Style sheets**: catat keputusan gaya untuk konsistensi (*-ize vs -ise*, angka vs kata)
5. **Reading techniques**: read aloud, read backward (untuk proofread), print out

## Struktur Penulisan
- **Process**: 
  1. First pass: big picture (structure, argument)
  2. Second pass: paragraph and sentence level
  3. Third pass: word level (grammar, spelling)
  4. Final pass: formatting, consistency

## Contoh
- **Before editing**: *"The study's findings are significant, however further research is needed."*
- **After editing**: *"The study's findings are significant; however, further research is needed."* (comma splice → semicolon)
- **Consistency**: *"She color-coded the files (American spelling)... He was colour-blind (British spelling)"* → pilih satu dan konsisten

## Latihan
1. Edit satu paragraf: perbaiki grammar, clarity, dan flow.
2. Cari dan koreksi 5 homophone errors dalam teks sampel.
3. Buat style sheet untuk dokumen 10 halaman.
4. Bedakan copy editing vs proofreading dengan contoh.
5. Proofread halaman ini: baca backward word by word.
