# 113. Review lengkap 12 dari 16 Tenses Bahasa Inggris (tabel dan perbandingan)

## Definisi & Target
Mereview dan membandingkan 12 tenses utama bahasa Inggris dalam satu tabel komprehensif. Fokus pada 4 tenses dasar di setiap waktu (Present, Past, Future) dengan 4 aspek (Simple, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous).

## Analogi
Seperti **peta waktu** — setiap tenses adalah alat untuk menunjukkan kapan dan bagaimana suatu aksi terjadi. Simple = faktual, Continuous = sedang berlangsung, Perfect = sudah selesai, Perfect Continuous = sudah & masih berlangsung.

## Pola & Struktur

### Tabel 12 Tenses

| Waktu | Simple | Continuous | Perfect | Perfect Continuous |
|-------|--------|------------|---------|-------------------|
| **Present** | S + V1(s/es)
I eat | S + am/is/are + V-ing
I am eating | S + have/has + V3
I have eaten | S + have/has + been + V-ing
I have been eating |
| **Past** | S + V2
I ate | S + was/were + V-ing
I was eating | S + had + V3
I had eaten | S + had + been + V-ing
I had been eating |
| **Future** | S + will + V1
I will eat | S + will + be + V-ing
I will be eating | S + will + have + V3
I will have eaten | S + will + have + been + V-ing
I will have been eating |

### Kapan Menggunakan?

| Tense | Fungsi Utama |
|-------|-------------|
| **Present Simple** | Fakta, kebiasaan, jadwal tetap |
| **Present Continuous** | Sedang terjadi sekarang, rencana dekat |
| **Present Perfect** | Hasil/sudah selesai, pengalaman hidup |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | Proses dari dulu sampai sekarang |
| **Past Simple** | Selesai di masa lalu (waktu spesifik) |
| **Past Continuous** | Sedang terjadi di masa lalu |
| **Past Perfect** | Sebelum aksi lain di masa lalu |
| **Past Perfect Continuous** | Durasi sebelum aksi lain di masa lalu |
| **Future Simple** | Prediksi, janji, keputusan spontan |
| **Future Continuous** | Akan sedang terjadi di masa depan |
| **Future Perfect** | Akan sudah selesai di masa depan |
| **Future Perfect Continuous** | Durasi sampai titik di masa depan |

## Latihan
1. Identifikasi tense: "She has been working here since 2020."
2. Ubah kalimat ini ke semua 12 tenses: "She / write / a letter."
3. Pilih tense yang tepat: "I ______ (wait) for you since 2 PM!"
4. Jelaskan perbedaan: "I lived in Jakarta" vs "I have lived in Jakarta" vs "I was living in Jakarta."
5. Buat 3 kalimat berbeda untuk 3 waktu (present, past, future) dengan aspek Continuous.
