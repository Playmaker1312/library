# 108. Perbedaan Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous

## Definisi & Target
Memahami kapan menggunakan Present Perfect (已完成) vs Present Perfect Continuous (已完成/持续中) dan perbedaan nuansa makna keduanya.

## Analogi
- **Present Perfect** = **Foto hasil jadi** — "Saya sudah melukis gambar ini" (hasilnya sudah selesai)
- **Present Perfect Continuous** = **Video proses melukis** — "Saya sedang melukis gambar ini sejak pagi" (prosesnya masih berlangsung)

## Pola & Struktur

| Aspek | Present Perfect | Present Perfect Continuous |
|-------|-----------------|---------------------------|
| Fokus | **Hasil** (result) | **Aktivitas/Proses** (activity) |
| Rumus | have/has + V3 | have/has + been + V-ing |
| Durasi | Biasanya tanpa durasi eksplisit | Sering dengan for/since |
| Pertanyaan "How many?" | Sering digunakan | Tidak digunakan |
| Pertanyaan "How long?" | Bisa digunakan | Lebih alami |

## Contoh
| Present Perfect | Present Perfect Continuous |
|-----------------|---------------------------|
| I have written five emails. (fokus: jumlah selesai) | I have been writing emails all morning. (fokus: aktivitas) |
| She has read that book. (fokus: selesai baca) | She has been reading that book for weeks. (fokus: durasi) |
| They have built a house. (hasil: rumah jadi) | They have been building a house. (proses: masih bangun) |

## Latihan
1. Pilih yang tepat: "I'm tired because I ______ (work / have worked / have been working) all day."
2. Pilih: "She ______ (has written / has been writing) three chapters this week."
3. Buat kalimat Present Perfect Continuous dari: "He started learning Japanese in January. He still learns it."
4. Mengapa "I have been knowing her" itu salah? Jelaskan.
5. Lengkapi dengan kedua tenses: "My hands are dirty because I ______ (repair) the car. I ______ (repair) three cars this week."
