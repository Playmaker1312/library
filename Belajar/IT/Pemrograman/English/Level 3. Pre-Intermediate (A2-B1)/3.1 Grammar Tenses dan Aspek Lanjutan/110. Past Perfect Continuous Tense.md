# 110. Past Perfect Continuous Tense

## Definisi & Target
Menyatakan aksi yang sedang berlangsung selama beberapa waktu **sebelum** aksi lain di masa lalu. Menekankan durasi dari suatu aktivitas yang sudah terjadi sebelum titik tertentu di masa lalu.

## Analogi
Seperti **mengecas HP sejak jam 12, lalu listrik padam jam 2** — aksi "mengecas" sudah berlangsung 2 jam sebelum listrik padam. Prosesnya yang penting, bukan hasilnya.

## Pola & Struktur
| Pola | Rumus |
|------|-------|
| (+) | **S + had + been + V-ing** |
| (-) | **S + had + not + been + V-ing** |
| (?) | **Had + S + been + V-ing?** |

## Contoh
| English | Indonesia |
|---------|-----------|
| They had been traveling for 10 hours before they arrived. | Mereka sudah bepergian selama 10 jam sebelum tiba. |
| She was exhausted because she had been working all night. | Dia kelelahan karena sudah bekerja semalaman. |
| Had you been waiting long before the bus came? | Apakah kamu sudah menunggu lama sebelum bus datang? |
| The ground was wet. It had been raining all day. | Tanahnya basah. Hujan sudah turun sepanjang hari. |

## Latihan
1. Isi: "He failed the test because he ______ (not/study) enough."
2. Gabungkan: "She was out of breath. She had been running." (jelaskan hubungannya)
3. Buat kalimat: "How long / you / study English / before you moved to London?"
4. Lengkapi: "They ______ (argue) for an hour when I walked in."
5. Bedakan maksud: "She had painted the room" vs "She had been painting the room."
