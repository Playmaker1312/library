# 156. Connected speech assimilation, elision, intrusion

## Definisi & Target
Memahami bagaimana bunyi berubah dalam ucapan cepat alami — **assimilation** (bunyi berubah), **elision** (bunyi hilang), **intrusion** (bunyi tambahan).

## Analogi
Seperti **ngomong "nggak"** di Indonesia — "tidak" cepat jadi "nggak." Bunyi berubah, hilang, atau bertambah. Di Inggris juga sama: "want to" jadi "wanna."

## Poin-Poin Penting

### Assimilation (Bunyi Berubah)
| Tulis | Ucap | Contoh |
|-------|------|--------|
| good boy | goo**b** boy | /ɡʊb bɔɪ/ |
| ten pens | te**m** pens | /tem penz/ |
| this year | thi**sh** year | /ðɪʃ jɪə/ |
| don't you | don**ch** you | /doʊntʃu/ |

### Elision (Bunyi Hilang)
| Tulis | Ucap | Contoh |
|-------|------|--------|
| next week | nes_ week | /neks wiːk/ |
| first time | firs_ time | /fɜːs taɪm/ |
| asked | as_ t | /ɑːst/ (kadang /æst/) |
| sandwich | san_wich | /sænwɪtʃ/ |

### Intrusion (Bunyi Tambahan)
| Tulis | Ucap | Contoh |
|-------|------|--------|
| law and order | law**r** and order | tambahan /r/ |
| I agree | I **y** agree | tambahan /j/ |
| go on | go **w** on | tambahan /w/ |

## Langkah-Langkah
1. Dengarkan native speaker — perhatikan beda tulis dan ucap
2. Rekam diri sendiri membaca kalimat
3. Praktikkan linking: "an apple" → a**n**apple (disambung)
4. Coba elision: "I don't know" → I dunno

## Latihan
1. Bagaimana bunyi "don't you" dalam ucapan cepat?
2. Latihan elision: ucapkan "next stop" dengan cepat — bunyi apa yang hilang?
3. Dengarkan lagu Inggris — cari contoh connected speech.
4. Praktikkan: "What do you want to do?" → apa yang terjadi dengan bunyinya?
5. Rekam dan bandingkan: "I want to go" diucapkan normal vs cepat.
