# 162. Menyampaikan pendapat, setuju dan tidak setuju (expressing opinions, agreeing and disagreeing)

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai ekspresi untuk menyampaikan pendapat, menyetujui, dan tidak menyetujui secara sopan dan efektif dalam bahasa Inggris.

## Analogi
Seperti **salaman** — cara kamu menyampaikan pendapat menentukan apakah orang mau dengar. Setuju/tidak setuju yang sopan membuka diskusi, bukan menutupnya.

## Poin-Poin Penting

### Expressing Opinions (Menyampaikan Pendapat)
| Tingkat Keyakinan | Frasa |
|------------------|-------|
| **Strong** | I'm convinced that... / I strongly believe that... |
| **Neutral** | In my opinion... / I think... / As far as I'm concerned... |
| **Weak** | I tend to think that... / It seems to me that... |

### Agreeing (Menyetujui)
| Tingkat | Frasa |
|---------|-------|
| **Strong** | I completely agree. / Absolutely! / Exactly! |
| **Neutral** | I agree with you. / That's a good point. / You're right. |
| **Partial** | I agree up to a point, but... / That may be true, but... |

### Disagreeing (Tidak Menyetujui)
| SOPAN | KASAR (hindari!) |
|-------|------------------|
| I see what you mean, but... | You're wrong! |
| I'm not sure I agree. | That's not true! |
| I see your point, but I think... | No way! |
| With respect, I'd say... | I disagree completely! |

## Langkah-Langkah
1. Dengar dulu sebelum merespons
2. Akui pendapat orang lain sebelum menyangkal
3. Gunakan "I see your point, but..." lebih sopan dari "I disagree"
4. Jaga intonasi — nada bicara penting

## Contoh
**A:** "I think English grammar is the hardest part of learning."
**B (agree):** "I completely agree. The tenses are really confusing."
**B (disagree politely):** "I see your point, but I think pronunciation is actually harder."

## Latihan
1. Berikan pendapat: "What do you think about online learning?"
2. Setuju sopan: "I think reading is the best way to learn vocabulary."
3. Tidak setuju sopan: "Social media is a waste of time."
4. Praktikkan: "I agree up to a point, but..." — selesaikan kalimat.
5. Role play: teman A setuju, teman B tidak setuju tentang topik "working from home."
