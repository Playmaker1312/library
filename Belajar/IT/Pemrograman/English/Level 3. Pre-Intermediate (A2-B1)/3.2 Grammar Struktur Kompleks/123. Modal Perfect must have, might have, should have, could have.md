# 123. Modal Perfect must have, might have, should have, could have

## Definisi & Target
Menggunakan modal + have + V3 untuk membuat **spekulasi, penyesalan, atau kritik** tentang masa lalu.

## Analogi
Seperti **detektif** yang menyimpulkan kejadian:
- **must have** = "Pasti... (95% yakin)"
- **might have** = "Mungkin... (50% yakin)"
- **could have** = "Bisa saja... (kemungkinan)"
- **should have** = "Seharusnya... (penyesalan/kritik)"

## Pola & Struktur

| Modal Perfect | Fungsi | Contoh |
|---------------|--------|--------|
| **must have + V3** | Kesimpulan logis masa lalu | She must have forgotten. |
| **might/may/could have + V3** | Kemungkinan masa lalu | He might have left early. |
| **should have + V3** | Kritik/penyesalan | I should have studied. |
| **could have + V3** | Kemungkinan yang tidak terjadi | We could have won. |
| **can't have + V3** | Ketidakmungkinan | He can't have done it. |

## Contoh
| English | Indonesia |
|---------|-----------|
| The ground is wet. It must have rained. | Tanah basah. Pasti sudah hujan. |
| She's late. She might have missed the bus. | Dia terlambat. Mungkin dia ketinggalan bus. |
| I should have told you earlier. | Saya seharusnya bilang lebih awal. |
| You could have hurt yourself! | Kamu bisa saja melukai dirimu sendiri! |
| He can't have finished already. | Tidak mungkin dia sudah selesai. |

## Latihan
1. Simpulkan: "Her phone is off. She ______ (must/have) forgotten to charge it."
2. Ekspresikan penyesalan: "I didn't call my mom. I ______ (should/call) her."
3. Spekulasi: "The window is broken. Somebody ______ (might/break) it."
4. Bedakan: "He should have called" vs "He could have called."
5. Buat kalimat dengan "can't have" tentang sesuatu yang tidak mungkin terjadi.
