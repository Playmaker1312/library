# 122. Modal Verbs Level 2 must have to, should ought to, might could (probability)

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai modal verbs tingkat lanjutan: **kewajiban** (must/have to), **saran kuat** (should/ought to), dan **probabilitas** (might/could).

## Analogi
- **must/have to** = Aturan sekolah "Harus pakai seragam" (tidak bisa ditawar)
- **should/ought to** = Saran dokter "Sebaiknya minum obat" (anjuran, bukan keharusan)
- **might/could (probability)** = Ramalan cuaca "Mungkin hujan" (kemungkinan, bukan kepastian)

## Pola & Struktur

| Modal | Fungsi | Kekuatan |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **must** | Kewajiban dari pembicara | 100% |
| **have to** | Kewajiban dari aturan eksternal | 100% |
| **must not** | Larangan | 0% diizinkan |
| **don't have to** | Tidak perlu (optional) | Boleh/tidak |
| **should / ought to** | Saran, rekomendasi | ~80% |
| **might / could** | Kemungkinan | ~50% |
| **can't (probability)** | Ketidakmungkinan | ~0% |

## Contoh
| English | Indonesia |
|---------|-----------|
| You must wear a helmet here. | Kamu harus pakai helm di sini. |
| I have to finish this report by Friday. | Saya harus menyelesaikan laporan ini Jumat. |
| You should see a doctor. | Sebaiknya kamu ke dokter. |
| We ought to help the poor. | Kita seharusnya membantu orang miskin. |
| It might rain later. | Mungkin nanti hujan. |
| She could be at home right now. | Dia mungkin ada di rumah sekarang. |
| That can't be true! | Itu tidak mungkin benar! |

## Latihan
1. Pilih must/have to: "You ______ stop at a red light."
2. Pilih: "I ______ (don't have to / must not) work tomorrow. It's a holiday."
3. Ubah probabilitas: "Maybe she is sick" (gunakan might).
4. Bedakan: "You must not smoke here" vs "You don't have to smoke here."
5. Buat kalimat dengan ought to tentang saran kesehatan.
