# 120. Relative Clauses defining (who, which, that, where, whose)

## Definisi & Target
Menggunakan relative clause **defining** (essential) untuk memberikan informasi **penting** tentang noun. Tanpa clause ini, kalimat kehilangan makna.

## Analogi
Seperti **KTP** — "Orang **yang memakai baju merah** itu adalah dosen saya." Frasa "yang memakai baju merah" penting untuk membedakan orang tersebut dari orang lain.

## Pola & Struktur

| Kata | Untuk | Contoh |
|------|-------|--------|
| **who** | Orang (subjek) | The woman who lives next door is a doctor. |
| **which** | Benda/hewan | I lost the book which you gave me. |
| **that** | Orang/benda (informal) | The car that I bought is blue. |
| **where** | Tempat | This is the restaurant where we first met. |
| **whose** | Kepemilikan | The student whose phone rang was embarrassed. |

**Defining clause** TIDAK pakai koma.

## Contoh
| English | Indonesia |
|---------|-----------|
| The man who called you is my brother. | Pria yang meneleponmu adalah kakakku. |
| I need a job which pays well. | Saya butuh pekerjaan yang gajinya bagus. |
| The house that I live in is small. | Rumah yang saya tinggali kecil. |
| That's the school where I studied. | Itu sekolah tempat saya belajar. |
| I know someone whose father is a pilot. | Saya kenal seseorang yang ayahnya pilot. |

## Latihan
1. Gabungkan: "I have a friend. She speaks five languages." (gunakan who)
2. Gabungkan: "This is the house. I grew up there." (gunakan where)
3. Lengkapi: "The man ______ car was stolen called the police."
4. Pilih who/which/that: "The movie ______ we watched was boring."
5. Buat kalimat dengan "whose" tentang kepemilikan.
