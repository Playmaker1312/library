# 115. Reported Speech (Direct vs Indirect Speech) statements

## Definisi & Target
Mengubah ucapan langsung (Direct Speech) menjadi ucapan tidak langsung (Indirect/Reported Speech) untuk pernyataan (statements).

## Analogi
- **Direct Speech** = **Replay voice note** — persis seperti yang diucapkan
- **Indirect Speech** = **Menjelaskan isi voice note** dengan kata sendiri

## Pola & Struktur

### Perubahan Tenses (Backshift)
| Direct | Indirect |
|--------|----------|
| Present Simple "I eat" | Past Simple "He said he ate" |
| Present Continuous "I am eating" | Past Continuous "He said he was eating" |
| Past Simple "I ate" | Past Perfect "He said he had eaten" |
| Present Perfect "I have eaten" | Past Perfect "He said he had eaten" |
| Past Perfect "I had eaten" | Past Perfect (tetap) "He said he had eaten" |
| will "I will eat" | would "He said he would eat" |
| can "I can" | could "He said he could" |
| must "I must" | had to "He said he had to" |

### Kata Penunjuk
| Direct | Indirect |
|--------|----------|
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| here | there |
| this | that |
| tomorrow | the next day |
| yesterday | the day before |

## Contoh
| Direct | Indirect |
|--------|----------|
| "I love this song." | She said (that) she loved that song. |
| "We are leaving now." | They said they were leaving then. |
| "I have finished the report." | He said he had finished the report. |
| "I will call you tomorrow." | She said she would call me the next day. |

## Latihan
1. Ubah ke indirect: "I am tired," she said.
2. Ubah: "We have never been to Bali," they said.
3. Ubah: "I can speak three languages," John said.
4. Ubah: "I will help you tomorrow," he said.
5. Ubah: "I bought this car yesterday," she said.
