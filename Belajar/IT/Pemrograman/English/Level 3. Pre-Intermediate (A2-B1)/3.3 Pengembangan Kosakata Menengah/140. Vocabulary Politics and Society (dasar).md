# 140. Vocabulary Politics and Society (dasar)

## Definisi & Target
Memperkaya kosakata bahasa Inggris dasar tentang politik dan masyarakat.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Vocabulary | Arti | Contoh |
|------------|------|--------|
| **government** | pemerintah | The **government** announced a new policy. |
| **democracy** | demokrasi | Indonesia is a **democracy**. |
| **election** | pemilihan umum | The **election** will be held next year. |
| **vote** | memilih | Did you **vote** in the last election? |
| **candidate** | kandidat | There are three **candidates** for president. |
| **political party** | partai politik | Which **political party** do you support? |
| **policy** | kebijakan | The new **policy** affects everyone. |
| **law** | undang-undang | The parliament passed a new **law**. |
| **citizen** | warga negara | Every **citizen** has rights and duties. |
| **rights** | hak | Human **rights** must be protected. |
| **society** | masyarakat | We live in a diverse **society**. |
| **community** | komunitas | The local **community** helped the victims. |
| **equality** | kesetaraan | We believe in **equality** for all. |
| **poverty** | kemiskinan | The government is fighting **poverty**. |
| **education** | pendidikan | **Education** is important for development. |
| **healthcare** | layanan kesehatan | Everyone deserves good **healthcare**. |

## Latihan
1. Lengkapi: "In a ______ (demokrasi), people vote for their leaders."
2. Apa perbedaan "policy" dan "law"?
3. Lengkapi: "Every ______ (warga negara) has the right to vote."
4. Jelaskan "equality" dalam bahasa Inggris sederhana.
5. Buat kalimat dengan "society" tentang lingkungan tempat tinggalmu.
