# 135. Vocabulary Business and Work

## Definisi & Target
Memperkaya kosakata bahasa Inggris di bidang bisnis dan pekerjaan untuk komunikasi profesional.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Vocabulary | Arti | Contoh |
|------------|------|--------|
| **colleague** | rekan kerja | My **colleague** helped me with the report. |
| **deadline** | batas waktu | The **deadline** is Friday. |
| **meeting** | rapat | We have a **meeting** at 10 AM. |
| **agenda** | agenda rapat | What's on the **agenda** today? |
| **salary** | gaji (bulanan) | He got a **salary** increase. |
| **wage** | upah (harian/mingguan) | The minimum **wage** went up. |
| **bonus** | bonus | She received a year-end **bonus**. |
| **employee** | karyawan | The company has 500 **employees**. |
| **employer** | majikan/perusahaan | My **employer** offers good benefits. |
| **resign** | mengundurkan diri | He decided to **resign**. |
| **fire** | memecat | The company **fired** five workers. |
| **hire** | mempekerjakan | We need to **hire** more staff. |
| **promotion** | promosi jabatan | She got a **promotion** to manager. |
| **negotiate** | bernegosiasi | We need to **negotiate** the contract. |
| **income** | penghasilan | Her annual **income** is $50,000. |
| **invoice** | faktur/ tagihan | Please send us an **invoice**. |

## Latihan
1. Lengkapi: "The ______ (batas waktu) for this project is next Monday."
2. Pilih: "My ______ (college/colleague) and I are working on a presentation."
3. Apa beda "salary" dan "wage"?
4. Lengkapi: "She got a ______ (promosi) after working for two years."
5. Buat kalimat dengan "negotiate" tentang gaji.
