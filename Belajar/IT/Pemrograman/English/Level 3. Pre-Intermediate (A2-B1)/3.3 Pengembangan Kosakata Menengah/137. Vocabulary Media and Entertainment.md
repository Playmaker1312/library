# 137. Vocabulary Media and Entertainment

## Definisi & Target
Memperkaya kosakata bahasa Inggris tentang media, hiburan, dan industri kreatif.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Vocabulary | Arti | Contoh |
|------------|------|--------|
| **broadcast** | siaran | The news **broadcast** starts at 7 PM. |
| **channel** | saluran TV | What **channel** is the movie on? |
| **episode** | episode | I watched the first **episode** last night. |
| **series** | serial | This **series** has 10 seasons. |
| **streaming** | streaming (menonton online) | I use Netflix for **streaming**. |
| **subscription** | langganan | I have a **subscription** to that platform. |
| **reality show** | acara realitas | I don't like **reality shows**. |
| **talk show** | acara bincang-bincang | She appeared on a **talk show**. |
| **audience** | penonton/audiens | The **audience** loved the performance. |
| **release** | merilis | The movie **release** date is next month. |
| **box office** | pendapatan film | The film was a **box office** hit. |
| **soundtrack** | lagu tema | I love the **soundtrack** of this film. |
| **plot** | alur cerita | The **plot** was very interesting. |
| **genre** | genre | What's your favorite music **genre**? |
| **celebrity** | selebriti | Many **celebrities** attended the event. |

## Latihan
1. Apa arti "streaming"?
2. Lengkapi: "I have a Netflix ______ (langganan)."
3. Pilih: "The movie ______ (released/relieved) last week."
4. Jelaskan beda "series" dan "episode."
5. Buat kalimat dengan "plot" tentang film favoritmu.
