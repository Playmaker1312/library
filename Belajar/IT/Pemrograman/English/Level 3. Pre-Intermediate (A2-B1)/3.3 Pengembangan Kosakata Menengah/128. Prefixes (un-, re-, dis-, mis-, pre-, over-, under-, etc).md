# 128. Prefixes (un-, re-, dis-, mis-, pre-, over-, under-, etc)

## Definisi & Target
Memahami awalan (prefixes) bahasa Inggris untuk memperluas kosakata dengan cepat. Satu prefix bisa mengubah arti puluhan kata.

## Analogi
Seperti **bahan tambahan makanan** — "cooked" (matang) + "un-" = "uncooked" (mentah). Prefix adalah "bumbu" yang mengubah arti dasar kata.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Prefix | Makna | Contoh |
|--------|-------|--------|
| **un-** | tidak, kebalikan | happy → **un**happy, lock → **un**lock |
| **re-** | lagi, kembali | do → **re**do, build → **re**build |
| **dis-** | tidak, kebalikan | agree → **dis**agree, appear → **dis**appear |
| **mis-** | salah | understand → **mis**understand, spell → **mis**spell |
| **pre-** | sebelum | view → **pre**view, pay → **pre**pay |
| **over-** | terlalu banyak | sleep → **over**sleep, estimate → **over**estimate |
| **under-** | terlalu sedikit, di bawah | estimate → **under**estimate, ground → **under**ground |
| **in-/im-/il-/ir-** | tidak (varian) | possible → **im**possible, legal → **il**legal |
| **non-** | bukan | stop → **non**-stop, fiction → **non**-fiction |
| **inter-** | antar, antara | national → **inter**national, view → **inter**view |

## Latihan
1. Tambahkan prefix yang tepat: "I ______agree with your opinion."
2. Tambahkan prefix: "Please ______write your essay. It's not good."
3. Lawan kata: "possible" → "______possible"
4. Lengkapi: "Don't ______understand me. That's not what I meant."
5. Pilih: "I ______estimated the cost. It was cheaper than I thought." (over/under)
