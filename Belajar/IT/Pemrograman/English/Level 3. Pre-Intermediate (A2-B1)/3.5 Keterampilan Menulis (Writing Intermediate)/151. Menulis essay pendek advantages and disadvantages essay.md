# 151. Menulis essay pendek advantages and disadvantages essay

## Definisi & Target
Menulis essay yang membahas **kelebihan (advantages)** dan **kekurangan (disadvantages)** dari suatu topik secara seimbang.

## Analogi
Seperti **membeli HP baru** — kita lihat plusnya (kamera bagus) dan minusnya (mahal) sebelum memutuskan. Essay ini menganalisis dua sisi dari suatu isu.

## Poin-Poin Penting
- **Tidak memihak** — bahas pro dan kontra secara adil
- **Perbandingan** — tunjukkan hubungan antara advantages dan disadvantages
- **Balanced conclusion** — bisa menyimpulkan mana yang lebih dominan
- **Transition words** untuk perbandingan (on the one hand, on the other hand, while, whereas)

## Struktur Penulisan
```
Introduction
  ├── Paraphrase topik
  └── Thesis statement (menyebut kedua sisi)

Body
  ├── Paragraf Advantages (On the one hand...)
  │   ├── Advantage 1 + detail
  │   └── Advantage 2 + detail
  └── Paragraf Disadvantages (On the other hand...)
      ├── Disadvantage 1 + detail
      └── Disadvantage 2 + detail

Conclusion
  ├── Ringkasan kedua sisi
  └── Opini pribadi / rekomendasi (opsional)
```

## Contoh
**Topik:** Advantages and Disadvantages of Working from Home
**Advantages:** flexible schedule, no commuting, more family time
**Disadvantages:** hard to separate work and personal life, less social interaction, distraction at home
**Conclusion:** While working from home offers flexibility, the lack of social interaction can be challenging for some people.

## Latihan
1. Pilih topik: "The internet" atau "Studying abroad." Cari 2 advantages dan 2 disadvantages.
2. Tulis introduction untuk topik tersebut.
3. Tulis body paragraph advantages (3-4 kalimat) dengan transition words.
4. Tulis body paragraph disadvantages (3-4 kalimat).
5. Tulis conclusion yang merangkum kedua sisi.
