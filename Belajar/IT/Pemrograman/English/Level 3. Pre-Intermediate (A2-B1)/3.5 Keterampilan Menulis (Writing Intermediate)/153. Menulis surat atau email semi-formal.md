# 153. Menulis surat atau email semi-formal

## Definisi & Target
Menulis surat atau email **semi-formal** — untuk situasi seperti email ke dosen, atasan yang dikenal, rekan kerja, atau klien yang sudah akrab.

## Analogi
Seperti **pakai batik** — rapi dan sopan, tapi tidak sekaku jas. Antara formal dan kasual. Kamu bisa lebih santai tapi tetap menghormati.

## Poin-Poin Penting

| Aspek | Formal | Semi-Formal | Kasual |
|-------|--------|-------------|--------|
| Salam | Dear Mr. Smith | Dear John / Hi John | Hey! |
| Nada | Sangat sopan | Sopan tapi santai | Santai |
| Kontraksi | Tidak | Boleh | Boleh |
| Penutup | Sincerely | Best regards / Warmly | Cheers / Take care |

## Struktur Penulisan
```
Subject: [jelas]

Dear [Nama depan],

[Paragraf 1: Sapaan dan tujuan]
I hope this email finds you well. I'm writing to...

[Paragraf 2: Penjelasan]
Just wanted to let you know that...

[Paragraf 3: Penutup]
Thanks a lot for your help. Let me know if you need anything.

Best regards,
[Nama]
```

## Contoh
**Topik:** Minta perpanjangan deadline ke dosen
**Isi:** "Dear Prof. Dewi, I hope you're doing well. I'm writing to ask if it would be possible to get an extension on the final paper. I've been dealing with some health issues and I'm a bit behind schedule. I completely understand if it's not possible, but I would really appreciate it. Thank you for your understanding. Best regards, Rina."

## Latihan
1. Tulis email semi-formal ke manajer: minta izin cuti 2 hari.
2. Beda salam: kapan pakai "Dear John" vs "Dear Mr. Smith"?
3. Ubah kalimat formal ke semi-formal: "I would like to request your assistance regarding..."
4. Tulis email ke dosen yang dikenal menanyakan nilai ujian.
5. Beda penutup: "Sincerely" vs "Best regards" vs "Cheers" — mana untuk semi-formal?
