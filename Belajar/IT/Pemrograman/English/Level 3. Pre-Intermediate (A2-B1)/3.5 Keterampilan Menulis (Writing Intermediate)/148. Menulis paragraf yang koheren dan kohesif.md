# 148. Menulis paragraf yang koheren dan kohesif

## Definisi & Target
Menulis paragraf yang **koheren** (ide mengalir logis) dan **kohesif** (kalimat terhubung dengan baik).

## Analogi
- **Koherensi** = **Jalan tol** — dari A ke B lancar, tidak belok-belok tanpa tujuan
- **Kohesi** = **Lem** yang merekatkan setiap kalimat — pakai linking words, pronouns, repetition

## Poin-Poin Penting

### Koherensi (Logical Flow)
- Satu paragraf = **satu ide utama** (topic sentence)
- Kalimat berikutnya mengembangkan ide tersebut
- Urutan logis: general → specific, problem → solution, cause → effect

### Kohesi (Language Connection)
| Alat Kohesi | Contoh |
|-------------|--------|
| **Transition words** | however, therefore, furthermore, in addition |
| **Pronouns** | it, they, this, these (mengacu ke sebelumnya) |
| **Synonyms** | gunakan kata yang berbeda untuk konsep sama |
| **Repetition** | ulang kata kunci untuk penekanan |

## Struktur Penulisan
```
Topic Sentence (ide utama)
├── Supporting Sentence 1 (detail, contoh)
├── Supporting Sentence 2 (detail, contoh)
└── Concluding Sentence (kesimpulan/transisi)
```

## Contoh
**Tanpa kohesi (kacau):** "I like reading. Books are good. Reading helps you learn. I read every day."

**Dengan kohesi:** "I really enjoy reading because it helps me learn new things. Moreover, reading every day improves my vocabulary. Therefore, I always carry a book with me."

## Latihan
1. Kembangkan topic sentence ini menjadi paragraf koheren: "Learning English requires consistent practice."
2. Tambahkan transition words ke kalimat: "I studied hard. I failed the exam."
3. Perbaiki paragraf ini dengan alat kohesi: "The city is crowded. The city has pollution. The city is expensive."
4. Identifikasi alat kohesi dalam sebuah paragraf bacaan.
5. Tulis 1 paragraf koheren tentang manfaat olahraga (5-7 kalimat).
