# 41. Basic Conjunctions (and, but, or, so, because)

## Definisi & Target
**Conjunctions** adalah kata hubung yang menggabungkan kata, frasa, atau kalimat. Target: menggunakan *and, but, or, so, because* dengan tepat.

## Analogi
Seperti lem atau jembatan — menghubungkan ide-ide agar kalimat mengalir.

## Pola & Struktur

| Conjunction | Fungsi | Contoh |
|------------|--------|--------|
| **And** | Menambahkan informasi | I like **and** coffee tea. |
| **But** | Pertentangan / kontras | I like coffee **but** I don't like tea. |
| **Or** | Pilihan | You can have coffee **or** tea. |
| **So** | Hasil / akibat | I was tired, **so** I went to bed. |
| **Because** | Alasan / sebab | I went to bed **because** I was tired. |

**Aturan Penting:**
- *And, but, or* bisa menghubungkan 2 kata, 2 frasa, atau 2 kalimat
- *So* dan *because* biasanya menghubungkan 2 klausa
- Koma sebelum *so* dan *but* untuk kalimat panjang

## Contoh
- "I bought apples **and** oranges."
- "She is smart **but** lazy."
- "We can watch a movie **or** go for a walk."
- "He was sick, **so** he didn't come."
- "I am happy **because** I passed the test."

## Latihan
1. Gabungkan: "I like apples. I like bananas." (and)
2. Gabungkan: "He is rich. He is not happy." (but)
3. Gabungkan: "I was hungry. I ate lunch." (so / because)
4. Isi *and, but, or, so, because*: "She studied hard ___ she passed."
5. Buat 5 kalimat, masing-masing pakai 1 conjunction berbeda.
