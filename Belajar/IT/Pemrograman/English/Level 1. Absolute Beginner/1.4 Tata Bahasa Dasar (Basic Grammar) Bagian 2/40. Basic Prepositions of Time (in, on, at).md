# 40. Basic Prepositions of Time (in, on, at)

## Definisi & Target
**Prepositions of time** menunjukkan kapan sesuatu terjadi. Target: menggunakan *in, on, at* dengan benar untuk waktu.

## Analogi
Seperti kalender dan jam — *at* untuk jam, *on* untuk hari/tanggal, *in* untuk bulan/tahun/bagian hari.

## Pola & Struktur

| Preposition | Penggunaan | Contoh |
|------------|-----------|--------|
| **At** | Jam, waktu spesifik, momen tertentu | **at** 7 o'clock, **at** noon, **at** midnight |
| **On** | Hari, tanggal, hari spesial | **on** Monday, **on** June 1st, **on** my birthday |
| **In** | Bulan, tahun, musim, bagian hari (kecuali night) | **in** July, **in** 2026, **in** the morning |

**Pengecualian:**
- *In the morning / in the afternoon / in the evening*
- TAPI: *at night* (bukan *in the night*)
- Tanpa preposition: *yesterday, tomorrow, today, last week, next month*

## Contoh
- "The class starts **at** 8 AM."
- "I was born **on** March 10th."
- "We go to Bali **in** July."
- "She sleeps **at** night."
- "I wake up **in** the morning."

## Latihan
1. Isi *in, on, at*: "The movie starts ___ 7 PM."
2. Isi: "My birthday is ___ January."
3. Isi: "I have a test ___ Friday."
4. Bedakan: *in the morning* vs *at night* — kenapa beda?
5. Buat 3 kalimat tentang jadwalmu, masing-masing pakai *in, on, at*.
