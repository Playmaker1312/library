# 37. Simple Future Tense (will dan going to)

## Definisi & Target
**Simple Future Tense** menyatakan aksi yang akan terjadi. Dua cara: **will** dan **going to**. Target: membedakan dan menggunakan keduanya.

## Analogi
Will seperti keputusan spontan — "Oh, saya akan beli itu." Going to seperti rencana — "Saya sudah rencanakan untuk pergi."

## Pola & Struktur

**Will (keputusan spontan, prediksi, janji)**
| Bentuk | Rumus | Contoh |
|--------|-------|--------|
| (+) | Subject + **will** + Verb 1 | I **will call** you. |
| (-) | Subject + **will not (won't)** + Verb 1 | She **won't come**. |
| (?) | **Will** + subject + Verb 1? | **Will** you help me? |

**Going To (rencana, bukti nyata)**
| Bentuk | Rumus | Contoh |
|--------|-------|--------|
| (+) | Subject + **am/is/are + going to** + Verb 1 | I **am going to study**. |
| (-) | Subject + **am/is/are + not + going to** + Verb 1 | It **is not going to rain**. |
| (?) | **Am/Is/Are** + subject + **going to** + Verb 1? | **Are** you **going to** come? |

**Perbedaan:**
- *Will*: "The phone is ringing. I **will** answer it." (spontan)
- *Going to*: "I **am going to** visit Bali next month." (rencana)

## Contoh
- "I **will help** you with your homework." (janji)
- "She **is going to** buy a new car." (rencana)
- "It **will rain** tomorrow." (prediksi)
- "They **are not going to** attend the party."

## Latihan
1. Isi *will* atau *going to*: "I ___ (visit) grandma this weekend." (rencana)
2. Isi: "Wait, I ___ (get) the door!" (spontan)
3. Ubah ke negatif: "I will come." → "I will not come / won't come."
4. Ubah ke tanya: "She is going to study." → "Is she going to study?"
5. Bedakan: "I will buy a phone" vs "I am going to buy a phone."
