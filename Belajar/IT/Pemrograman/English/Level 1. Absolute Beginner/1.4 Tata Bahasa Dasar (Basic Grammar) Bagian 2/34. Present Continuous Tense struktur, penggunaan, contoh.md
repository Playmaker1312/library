# 34. Present Continuous Tense — struktur, penggunaan, contoh

## Definisi & Target
**Present Continuous Tense** digunakan untuk aksi yang sedang berlangsung sekarang. Target: membentuk kalimat positif, negatif, dan tanya dalam present continuous.

## Analogi
Seperti foto atau video — menangkap momen yang sedang terjadi saat ini juga.

## Pola & Struktur

**Rumus: Subject + **am/is/are** + **Verb-ing****

**Positif (+)**
| Subjek | To Be | Verb-ing | Contoh |
|--------|-------|----------|--------|
| I | am | eating | I **am eating** lunch now. |
| You/We/They | are | playing | They **are playing** football. |
| He/She/It | is | reading | She **is reading** a book. |

**Negatif (-):** Tambahkan *not* setelah to be.
- "I am **not** sleeping."
- "He is **not** (isn't) working."

**Tanya (?):** Letakkan to be di depan.
- "**Are** you listening?"
- "**Is** she coming?"

**Aturan Verb-ing:**
- Umum: +ing (*eat → eating*)
- Akhiran e + ing: *make → making* (e dihilangkan)
- Akhiran CVC + double consonant: *run → running, swim → swimming*

## Contoh
- "I **am studying** English right now." (sedang terjadi)
- "She **is cooking** dinner at the moment."
- "They **are not watching** TV." (negatif)
- "**Is** he **sleeping**?" (tanya)

## Latihan
1. Isi: "I ___ (read) a book now."
2. Ubah ke negatif: "She is singing." → "She is not singing."
3. Ubah ke tanya: "You are eating." → "Are you eating?"
4. Bedakan: "I eat" vs "I am eating" — apa bedanya?
5. Benerin: "He are running." (salah — is running)
