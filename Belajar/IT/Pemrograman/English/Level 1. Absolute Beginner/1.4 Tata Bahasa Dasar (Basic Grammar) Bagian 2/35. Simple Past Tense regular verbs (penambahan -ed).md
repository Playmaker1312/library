# 35. Simple Past Tense — regular verbs (penambahan -ed)

## Definisi & Target
**Simple Past Tense** digunakan untuk aksi yang sudah selesai di masa lalu. Target: membentuk kalimat past tense dengan regular verbs (+ed).

## Analogi
Seperti buku harian kemarin — semua yang sudah terjadi.

## Pola & Struktur

**Rumus: Subject + **Verb 2 (past)** + object**

**Positif (+)**
- Semua subjek pakai bentuk yang sama: Verb + ed
- "I **walked** to school yesterday."
- "She **played** tennis last Sunday."

**Aturan Penambahan -ed:**
- Umum: +ed (*walk → walked, play → played*)
- Akhiran e: +d (*dance → danced, like → liked*)
- Konsonan + y → ied: *study → studied, cry → cried*
- CVC (1 suku kata): double consonant + ed: *stop → stopped, plan → planned*

**Negatif (-):** **didn't** + Verb 1
- "I **didn't walk** to school."
- "She **didn't play** tennis."

**Tanya (?):** **Did** + subject + Verb 1?
- "**Did** you **walk** to school?"
- "**Did** she **play** tennis?"

## Contoh
- "I **watched** a movie last night."
- "We **visited** our grandmother yesterday."
- "He **didn't finish** his homework."
- "**Did** they **arrive** on time?"

## Latihan
1. Ubah ke past tense: "I walk" → "I ___" (walked)
2. Ubah ke past: *study → ___ , stop → ___ , dance → ___*
3. Ubah ke negatif: "She played tennis." → "She didn't play tennis."
4. Ubah ke tanya: "They arrived late." → "Did they arrive late?"
5. Buat 3 kalimat tentang apa yang kamu lakukan kemarin.
