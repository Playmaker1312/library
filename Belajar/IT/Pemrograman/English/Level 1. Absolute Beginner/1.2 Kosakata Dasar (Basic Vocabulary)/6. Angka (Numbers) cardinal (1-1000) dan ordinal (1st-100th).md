# 6. Angka (Numbers) cardinal (1-1000) dan ordinal (1st-100th)

## Definisi & Target
**Cardinal numbers** menyatakan jumlah (one, two, three). **Ordinal numbers** menyatakan urutan (first, second, third). Target: menghitung 1-1000 dan menyebutkan urutan 1st-100th.

## Analogi
Cardinal seperti jumlah permen di toples. Ordinal seperti posisi dalam lomba — juara 1, 2, 3.

**Puluhan, Ratusan, Ribuan:** 21 = twenty-one, 100 = one hundred, 1000 = one thousand

**Ordinal Numbers (1st-20th)**
| Ordinal | English | Ordinal | English |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| 1st | first | 11th | eleventh |
| 2nd | second | 12th | twelfth |
| 3rd | third | 13th | thirteenth |
| 4th | fourth | 14th | fourteenth |
| 5th | fifth | 20th | twentieth |
| 10th | tenth | 100th | one hundredth |

## Contoh
- "I have **three** books." (cardinal — jumlah)
- "She is **first** in the class." (ordinal — urutan)
- "Today is March **fifteenth**." (ordinal — tanggal)

## Latihan
1. Tulis cardinal: 7, 12, 25, 99, 500.
2. Tulis ordinal: 3rd, 9th, 12th, 21st, 100th.
3. Sebutkan tanggal lahirmu dalam ordinal (contoh: May 10th).
4. Hitung mundur dari 20 ke 1 dalam Bahasa Inggris.
