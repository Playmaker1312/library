# 50. Menyatakan kemampuan (Can / Can't)

## Definisi & Target
**Can** menyatakan kemampuan. **Can't** menyatakan ketidakmampuan. Target: menggunakan *can* dan *can't* dengan benar.

## Analogi
Seperti tombol ON/OFF — *can* = bisa (ON), *can't* = tidak bisa (OFF).

## Poin-Poin Penting

**Positif (+) — Can:**
- Subject + **can** + Verb 1
- "I **can swim**."
- "She **can speak** English."
- "They **can come** tomorrow."

**Negatif (-) — Can't / Cannot:**
- Subject + **can't (cannot)** + Verb 1
- "I **can't drive**."
- "He **cannot play** guitar."
- "We **can't go** today."

**Tanya (?) — Can... ?**
- **Can** + subject + Verb 1?
- "**Can you swim**?"
- "**Can she speak** English?"
- "**Can they come**?"

**Catatan:**
- *Can* tidak berubah untuk he/she/it: *She can* (bukan *cans*)
- Verb setelah *can* selalu Verb 1 tanpa tambahan

## Contoh
- "I **can play** the piano."
- "She **can't cook** very well."
- "**Can** you **help** me?"
- "Yes, I **can**! / No, I **can't**."

## Latihan
1. Sebutkan 3 hal yang bisa kamu lakukan: "I can..."
2. Sebutkan 3 hal yang tidak bisa kamu lakukan: "I can't..."
3. Tanya teman: "Can you...?" tentang 3 aktivitas.
4. Benerin: "She can swims." (salah — swim)
5. Jawab pertanyaan: "Can you speak English?"
