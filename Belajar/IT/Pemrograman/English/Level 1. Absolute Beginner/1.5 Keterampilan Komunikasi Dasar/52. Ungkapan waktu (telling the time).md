# 52. Ungkapan waktu (telling the time)

## Definisi & Target
Kemampuan menyatakan jam dan waktu dalam Bahasa Inggris. Target: mampu membaca jam analog/digital dan menyebutkan waktu.

## Analogi
Seperti membaca jam dinding — di Inggris ada cara khusus menyebutkan menit.

## Poin-Poin Penting

**Jam Tepat:**
- 7:00 → **It is seven o'clock.**
- 9:00 → **It is nine o'clock.**

**Setengah / Lewat / Kurang:**
- 7:05 → **It is five past seven.**
- 7:15 → **It is quarter past seven.**
- 7:30 → **It is half past seven.**
- 7:40 → **It is twenty to eight.** (20 menit menuju jam 8)
- 7:45 → **It is quarter to eight.**
- 7:55 → **It is five to eight.**

**Format Digital (lebih mudah):**
- 7:20 → **It is seven twenty.**
- 8:45 → **It is eight forty-five.**

**Pertanyaan:**
- "**What time is it?** " atau "**What's the time?** "
- Jawab: "**It is...** "

**AM / PM:**
- AM: 00:00 - 11:59 (tengah malam sampai tengah hari)
- PM: 12:00 - 23:59 (tengah hari sampai tengah malam)

## Contoh
- "**What time is it?** " "**It is three o'clock.** "
- "The class starts **at eight thirty**."
- "I wake up **at 6 AM**."

**Tabel Cepat:**
| Waktu | Cara Inggris | Cara Digital |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| 2:00 | two o'clock | two o'clock |
| 2:15 | quarter past two | two fifteen |
| 2:30 | half past two | two thirty |
| 2:45 | quarter to three | two forty-five |
| 2:50 | ten to three | two fifty |

## Latihan
1. Jawab: "What time is it?" untuk jam 8:00, 9:15, 10:30.
2. Tulis 3:45 menggunakan *quarter to / past*.
3. Bedakan: 8:15 = quarter past eight vs 8:45 = quarter to nine.
4. Sebutkan jam kamu bangun tidur dan jam tidur.
5. Tanya teman: "What time do you eat lunch?"
