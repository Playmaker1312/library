# 45. Percakapan di restoran atau kafe

## Definisi & Target
Kemampuan memesan makanan dan minuman di restoran/kafe. Target: mampu memesan, bertanya menu, dan membayar.

## Analogi
Seperti drama singkat — ada pelanggan dan pelayan. Kamu harus tahu台词nya (dialognya).

## Poin-Poin Penting
- Salam dan minta meja: "A table for two, please."
- Lihat menu: "Can I see the menu, please?"
- Pesan: "I'd like..." atau "I will have..."
- Minta tagihan: "Can I have the bill, please?"

## Langkah-Langkah
1. **Masuk & Minta Meja**: "Hi, a table for [jumlah] people, please."
2. **Lihat Menu**: "Can I see the menu?"
3. **Pesan Makanan**: "I'd like the fried rice, please."
4. **Pesan Minuman**: "And a glass of orange juice."
5. **Minta Tagihan**: "Can I have the bill / check, please?"
6. **Bayar**: "Here you are." / "Keep the change."

**Frasa Berguna:**
- "What do you recommend?" — Apa rekomendasimu?
- "I am allergic to..." — Saya alergi...
- "No ice, please." — Tanpa es.
- "Can I have it to go?" — Bungkus.

## Contoh
**Waiter**: "Welcome! What would you like to order?"
**You**: "I'd like a bowl of fried rice and an iced tea, please."
**Waiter**: "Anything else?"
**You**: "No, that's all. Thank you."
**Waiter**: "Here is your food. Enjoy!"

## Latihan
1. Buat dialog antara pelanggan dan pelayan (4-6 baris).
2. Praktikkan memesan: "I'd like a coffee, please."
3. Bagaimana cara minta menu? Tulis kalimatnya.
4. Bagaimana cara minta bill? Tulis kalimatnya.
