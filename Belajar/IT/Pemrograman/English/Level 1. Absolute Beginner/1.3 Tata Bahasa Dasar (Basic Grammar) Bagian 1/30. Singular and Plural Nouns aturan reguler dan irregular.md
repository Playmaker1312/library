# 30. Singular and Plural Nouns — aturan reguler dan irregular

## Definisi & Target
**Singular** = satu, **Plural** = lebih dari satu. Target: mengubah singular nouns ke plural dengan aturan reguler dan irregular.

## Analogi
Seperti beda "satu kucing" dan "banyak kucing" — dalam Bahasa Inggris, biasanya tinggal tambah -s atau -es.

## Pola & Struktur

**Regular Plurals (Beraturan)**
| Aturan | Singular | Plural |
|--------|----------|--------|
| + **-s** | book, cat, pen | books, cats, pens |
| + **-es** (akhiran -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -o) | box, bus, church | boxes, buses, churches |
| -y → **-ies** (konsonan + y) | baby, city | babies, cities |
| -f / -fe → **-ves** | wolf, knife | wolves, knives |

**Irregular Plurals (Tidak Beraturan)**
| Singular | Plural | Singular | Plural |
|----------|--------|----------|--------|
| child | **children** | man | **men** |
| woman | **women** | person | **people** |
| tooth | **teeth** | foot | **feet** |
| mouse | **mice** | fish | **fish** (bisa juga fishes) |
| sheep | **sheep** | deer | **deer** |

## Contoh
- "I have three **books**." (regular)
- "There are two **children** in the park." (irregular)
- "She has **beautiful eyes**." (regular: -y + ies)

## Latihan
1. Ubah ke plural: one cat → two ___, one box → two ___
2. Ubah ke plural: baby → ___, man → ___, child → ___
3. Benerin: "I have two foots." (salah — feet)
4. Tulis plural dari: *city, knife, mouse, sheep, tooth*.
5. Buat kalimat dengan plural nouns: books, children, women.
