# 28. To Be (am, is, are) — kalimat positif, negatif, tanya

## Definisi & Target
**To Be** (am/is/are) adalah kata kerja bantu yang menghubungkan subjek dengan deskripsi. Target: membentuk kalimat positif, negatif, dan tanya dengan *am/is/are*.

## Analogi
To Be seperti tanda "=" (sama dengan) dalam matematika — menghubungkan subjek dengan informasi tentangnya.

## Pola & Struktur

**Positif (+)**
| Subjek | To Be | Contoh |
|--------|-------|--------|
| I | **am** | I am a student. |
| You/We/They | **are** | You are kind. |
| He/She/It | **is** | She is smart. |

**Negatif (-)**
Tambahkan **not** setelah to be:
- I am **not** tired.
- You are **not** (aren't) late.
- She is **not** (isn't) here.

**Tanya (?)**
Letakkan to be di depan subjek:
- **Am** I late?
- **Are** you okay?
- **Is** she your sister?

## Contoh
- (+) "I **am** happy."
- (-) "They **are not** (aren't) at home."
- (?) "**Is** he your brother? — Yes, he **is**."

## Latihan
1. Isi am/is/are: "She ___ a teacher."
2. Ubah ke negatif: "I am tired." → "I am not tired."
3. Ubah ke tanya: "You are a student." → "Are you a student?"
4. Benerin: "He are my friend." (salah — is)
5. Buat 1 kalimat positif, 1 negatif, 1 tanya dengan to be.
