# 29. To Have / Has — penggunaan dasar

## Definisi & Target
**To have** menyatakan kepemilikan. Target: membedakan penggunaan *have* dan *has*, serta membentuk kalimat positif, negatif, tanya.

## Analogi
Seperti mengatakan "Saya punya..." — menunjukkan apa yang dimiliki seseorang.

## Pola & Struktur

**Positif (+)**
| Subjek | Have/Has | Contoh |
|--------|----------|--------|
| I / You / We / They | **have** | I have a car. |
| He / She / It | **has** | She has a cat. |

**Negatif (-)**
Gunakan **don't have / doesn't have**:
- I **don't have** a car.
- She **doesn't have** a pet.

**Tanya (?)**
Gunakan **Do / Does** di depan:
- **Do** you have a pen? — Yes, I do.
- **Does** she have a sister? — No, she doesn't.

**Catatan:** *Has* berubah jadi *have* setelah *does/doesn't*:
- Does she **have** (bukan *has*) a car?

## Contoh
- "I **have** two brothers."
- "He **has** a new phone."
- "They **don't have** any money."
- "**Do** you **have** a pet?"

## Latihan
1. Isi have/has: "We ___ a big house."
2. Isi: "She ___ a red bag."
3. Ubah ke negatif: "I have a car." → "I don't have a car."
4. Ubah ke tanya: "He has a bike." → "Does he have a bike?"
5. Benerin: "She have a dog." (salah — has)
