# 25. Possessive Pronouns (mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs)

## Definisi & Target
**Possessive pronouns** menggantikan noun yang menunjukkan kepemilikan. Target: membedakan possessive adjectives dan possessive pronouns.

## Analogi
Kalau possessive adjective seperti "bukuku", possessive pronoun seperti "milikku" — tanpa perlu menyebut benda.

## Pola & Struktur

| Possessive Adj | Possessive Pronoun | Contoh |
|---------------|-------------------|--------|
| my | **mine** | This book is **mine**. |
| your | **yours** | That pen is **yours**. |
| his | **his** | This bag is **his**. |
| her | **hers** | That dress is **hers**. |
| its | — | (jarang digunakan) |
| our | **ours** | This house is **ours**. |
| their | **theirs** | That car is **theirs**. |

**Aturan Penting:**
- Possessive pronoun TIDAK diikuti noun — dia sudah mewakili noun
- "This is **my** book." (adj + noun) vs "This book is **mine**." (pronoun saja)
- **His** bentuknya sama untuk adjective dan pronoun

## Contoh
- "This book is **mine**."
- "That pen is **yours**."
- "The red car is **theirs**."
- "Is this bag **hers**?"

## Latihan
1. Ubah: "This is my phone" → "This phone is ___." (mine)
2. Ubah: "Those are their shoes" → "Those shoes are ___."
3. Pilih: "This is (my / mine) ___ book."
4. Pilih: "This book is (my / mine) ___."
5. Bedakan: "It is her bag" vs "The bag is hers" — apa bedanya?
