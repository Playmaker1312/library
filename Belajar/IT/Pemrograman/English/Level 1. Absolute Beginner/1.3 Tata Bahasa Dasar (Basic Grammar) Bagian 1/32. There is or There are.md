# 32. There is / There are

## Definisi & Target
**There is** dan **There are** digunakan untuk menyatakan keberadaan sesuatu. Target: menggunakan *there is* (tunggal) dan *there are* (jamak) dengan benar.

## Analogi
Seperti "Ada" dalam Bahasa Indonesia — "Ada buku di meja" bisa jadi satu atau banyak buku.

## Pola & Struktur

| Bentuk | Tunggal | Jamak |
|--------|---------|-------|
| Positif (+) | **There is** a book on the table. | **There are** two books on the table. |
| Negatif (-) | **There isn't** (is not) any water. | **There aren't** (are not) any students. |
| Tanya (?) | **Is there** a pen? | **Are there** any chairs? |
| Jawab | Yes, there is. / No, there isn't. | Yes, there are. / No, there aren't. |

**Aturan Penting:**
- Gunakan *there is* untuk uncountable nouns: *There is some milk.*
- Kontraksi: *there's* = *there is*
- Untuk daftar: gunakan *there is* jika item pertama tunggal, *there are* jika jamak

## Contoh
- "**There is** a cat under the table."
- "**There are** three students in the room."
- "**Is there** a bank near here?"
- "**There isn't** any sugar in the coffee."

## Latihan
1. Isi *there is / there are*: "___ a book on the desk."
2. Isi: "___ many people in the park."
3. Ubah ke tanya: "There is a pen." → "Is there a pen?"
4. Ubah ke negatif: "There are two cats." → "There aren't two cats."
5. Deskripsikan ruanganmu menggunakan *there is* dan *there are*.
