# 215. Describing Trends, Charts, and Data

## Definisi & Target
Mendeskripsikan tren, grafik, dan data secara verbal. Target: mampu menjelaskan data visual dengan vocabulary yang tepat untuk presentasi bisnis dan akademik.

## Analogi
Seperti komentator bola yang menjelaskan grafik pertandingan — "penjualan naik tajam di kuartal 3, lalu stabil, kemudian turun sedikit." Ini skill penting di dunia kerja.

## Poin-Poin Penting

- **Vocabulary untuk tren:**
  - *Up:* increase, rise, grow, surge, climb, go up
  - *Down:* decrease, decline, fall, drop, plunge, go down
  - *Stable:* remain stable, stay constant, level off, plateau
  - *Adverbs:* sharply, dramatically, gradually, steadily, slightly
- **Describing changes:**
  - Prepositions: *increase from... to..., rise by 10%, drop to a low of...*
  - Peaks and troughs: *reach a peak of..., hit a low of..., bottom out*
- **Data comparison:** *compared to, whereas, in contrast, similarly.*

## Langkah-Langkah

1. Lihat grafik/ chart — pahami sumbu X dan Y.
2. Identifikasi tren umum (naik/turun/stabil).
3. Sebutkan titik penting (tertinggi, terendah, perubahan mendadak).
4. Bandingkan data antar kategori.
5. Simpulkan: "The overall trend shows..."

## Contoh

**Deskripsi chart:**
*"As the chart shows, sales **increased steadily** from January to March, **reaching a peak** of $50,000 in April. After that, they **declined sharply** in May, before **leveling off** in June at around $30,000. Overall, the second quarter was less successful than the first."*

**Vocabulary in action:**
- Reach a peak = mencapai puncak
- Decline sharply = menurun tajam
- Level off = stabil/mendatar

## Latihan

1. Cari grafik dari berita ekonomi. Deskripsikan trennya.
2. Buat 3 kalimat dengan: increase sharply, decrease gradually, remain stable.
3. Bedakan: "rise by 10%" vs "rise to 10%" — apa bedanya?
4. Gambar garis tren naik → turun → stabil. Deskripsikan.
5. Presentasikan data penjualan fiktif selama 1 menit.
