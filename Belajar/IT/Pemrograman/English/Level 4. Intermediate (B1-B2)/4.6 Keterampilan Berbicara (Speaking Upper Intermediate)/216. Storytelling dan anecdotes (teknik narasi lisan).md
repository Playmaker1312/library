# 216. Storytelling dan Anecdotes (Teknik Narasi Lisan)

## Definisi & Target
Storytelling adalah seni bercerita untuk menarik perhatian dan menyampaikan pesan. Target: mampu menceritakan pengalaman dan cerita pendek secara engaging dalam bahasa Inggris.

## Analogi
Seperti nenek yang mendongeng — ada pembukaan, tokoh, konflik, klimaks, dan pesan. Cerita yang baik membuat pendengar merasa "terbawa" dan penasaran.

## Poin-Poin Penting

- **Struktur storytelling:**
  - **Setting:** Where and when? (Last year, in a small town...)
  - **Characters:** Who? (My friend and I...)
  - **Conflict:** What went wrong? (But then something unexpected happened...)
  - **Climax:** The most intense moment. (And at that moment, I realized...)
  - **Resolution:** How did it end? (In the end, we learned...)
- **Tenses:** Past simple untuk kejadian utama, past continuous untuk latar.
- **Engagement:** Eye contact, gesture, voice variation, pause.
- **Fillers:** *Well, you know, actually, so, anyway.*

## Langkah-Langkah

1. Pilih pengalaman pribadi yang menarik.
2. Buat timeline (5 poin: setting → conflict → climax → resolution → lesson).
3. Latihan bercerita 1 menit — rekam dan dengar.
4. Tambahkan detail sensorik (what you saw, heard, felt).
5. Perbaiki berdasarkan feedback.

## Contoh

*"Last year, I decided to go hiking for the first time. (Setting) Everything went well until we got lost. (Conflict) It was getting dark, and my phone had no signal. At that point, I truly panicked. (Climax) Fortunately, we found a ranger station just before sunset. (Resolution) I learned to always bring a map and tell someone where you're going. (Lesson)"*

**Vocabulary:** *hiking, get lost, panic, fortunately, ranger station.*

## Latihan

1. Tulis cerita 1 menit tentang pengalaman lucu/mengesankan.
2. Latihan bercerita dengan struktur: setting → conflict → resolution.
3. Rekam dan evaluasi: apakah ada bagian yang kurang jelas?
4. Tambahkan 2 detail sensorik ke dalam cerita.
5. Ceritakan ulang ke teman dan minta feedback.
