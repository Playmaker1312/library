# 217. Negotiation Language

## Definisi & Target
Negotiation adalah proses mencapai kesepakatan melalui diskusi. Target: menggunakan bahasa negosiasi yang efektif untuk mendapatkan hasil terbaik sambil menjaga hubungan baik.

## Analogi
Seperti tawar-menawar di pasar — tapi versi profesional. Kamu minta harga lebih murah (propose), penjual bilang tidak bisa (reject), kamu tawar lagi (compromise), akhirnya sepakat di tengah.

## Poin-Poin Penting

- **Opening:** State your position + explain why it's reasonable.
  - *We believe that a 10% discount is appropriate given our long-term partnership.*
- **Bargaining:** Make concessions; ask for something in return.
  - *If you can offer a 15% discount, we would be willing to sign a 2-year contract.*
- **Compromising:** Find middle ground.
  - *Perhaps we can meet halfway at 12%?*
- **Closing:** Confirm agreement.
  - *So we agree on 12%, with delivery by June 1.*
- **Deadlock:** When no agreement possible.
  - *It seems we have reached an impasse. Let's take a break and revisit.*

## Langkah-Langkah

1. Tentukan posisi ideal dan BATNA (Best Alternative to Negotiated Agreement).
2. Latih opening statement — jelas tapi tidak agresif.
3. Siapkan concession (apa yang bisa kamu tawarkan).
4. Latih responses untuk skenario berbeda.
5. Simulasi negosiasi dengan teman.

## Contoh

**Skenario:** Minta kenaikan gaji.
*"I have been with the company for three years and have consistently exceeded my targets. (Opening) Based on my contributions and market rates, I believe a 15% increase is reasonable. (Position) I understand budget constraints; perhaps we could agree on 10% with additional benefits." (Compromise)*

**Key phrases:**
- *I understand your position, however...*
- *What if we...*
- *Let's find a solution that works for both of us.*

## Latihan

1. Tulis opening statement untuk negosiasi (contoh: minta deadline extension).
2. Latih: "If you ___, then I will ___."
3. Apa itu BATNA? Kenapa penting?
4. Simulasi negosiasi 5 menit dengan teman.
5. Bedakan: competitive vs collaborative negotiation language.
