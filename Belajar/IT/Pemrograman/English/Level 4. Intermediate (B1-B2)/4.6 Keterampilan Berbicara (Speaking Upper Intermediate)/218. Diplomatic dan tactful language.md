# 218. Diplomatic dan Tactful Language

## Definisi & Target
Diplomatic language adalah cara bicara yang sopan, tidak ofensif, dan menjaga hubungan. Target: menyampaikan kritik, ketidaksetujuan, atau berita buruk tanpa merusak hubungan.

## Analogi
Seperti di Indonesia bilang "Mohon maaf, mungkin bisa dipertimbangkan lagi" daripada "Kamu salah!" Diplomatic language menghindari konflik dan membuat lawan bicara tetap merasa dihargai.

## Poin-Poin Penting

- **Softening criticism:**
  - *I wonder if we could improve this by...* (bukan "This is wrong")
  - *Perhaps we could consider...* (bukan "You should...")
  - *It might be better to...* (bukan "Do this instead")
- **Disagreeing politely:**
  - *I see your point, but have we considered...*
  - *That's an interesting perspective. However, from my experience...*
  - *I respectfully disagree because...*
- **Giving bad news:**
  - *I'm afraid that...* (bukan straight "No")
  - *Unfortunately, we are unable to...*
  - *While I appreciate your offer, we have decided to...*
- **Apologizing professionally:**
  - *I apologize for the inconvenience.*
  - *Please accept my sincere apologies.*

## Langkah-Langkah

1. Pikirkan apa yang ingin disampaikan.
2. Identifikasi kata/kalimat yang mungkin terdengar kasar.
3. Ganti dengan softer alternatives.
4. Gunakan "I" statements (I feel, I think, In my opinion).
5. Tambahkan positive opening sebelum kritik.

## Contoh

**Direct (kurang diplomatis):**
*"You made a mistake. This report is wrong."*

**Diplomatic:**
*"I noticed some discrepancies in the report. Perhaps we could review the numbers together to ensure accuracy?"*

**Direct:** *"I disagree."*
**Diplomatic:** *"I understand your reasoning. However, I see it slightly differently."*

## Latihan

1. Ubah "Your presentation was boring" menjadi diplomatic.
2. Latih disagreeing politely tentang topik kontroversial.
3. Beri bad news: "We rejected your application" secara diplomatis.
4. Bedakan: "You need to fix this" vs "It might need some adjustments."
5. Simulasi: kritik pekerjaan teman secara diplomatis.
