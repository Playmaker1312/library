# 167. Emphasis and Fronting

## Definisi & Target
Emphasis dan fronting adalah teknik memindahkan elemen kalimat ke posisi depan untuk memberi penekanan. Target: bervariasi struktur kalimat untuk efek retoris dan penulisan yang lebih kuat.

## Analogi
Seperti fotografer yang menggeser objek utama ke tengah frame. Biasanya kita bilang "I love this song." Tapi kalau mau emphasise: "This song I love!" — objeknya dimajukan.

## Pola & Struktur

| Teknik | Struktur | Contoh |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Fronting object | Object + S + V | That movie I have watched three times |
| Fronting adjective | Adjective + as/though + S + V | Difficult as it was, we finished |
| Fronting preposition | Prep phrase + S + V + , ... | In the corner sat a mysterious stranger |
| Do/did + verb | S + do/does/did + V | I do believe you are right |
| Own self | Reflexive pronoun | The manager himself approved this |

## Contoh

1. **That dress I simply must buy!** — Gaun itu, saya benar-benar harus beli!
2. **Strange as it may seem, I enjoy working late.** — Meski mungkin aneh, saya suka kerja lembur.
3. **Not a single word did he say.** — Tidak sepatah kata pun dia ucapkan.
4. **Into the room walked the professor.** — Ke dalam ruangan masuklah profesor itu.
5. **I do appreciate your help.** — Saya sungguh menghargai bantuanmu.

## Latihan

1. Beri penekanan dengan "do": "She loves classical music."
2. Fronting object: "I have read that book five times."
3. Gunakan "as/though": "Although it was expensive, I bought it."
4. Buat kalimat dengan fronting prepositional phrase.
5. Bandingkan "He himself said it" vs "He said it himself."
