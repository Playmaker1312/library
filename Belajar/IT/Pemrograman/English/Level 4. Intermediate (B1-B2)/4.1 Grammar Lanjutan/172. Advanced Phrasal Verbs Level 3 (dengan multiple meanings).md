# 172. Advanced Phrasal Verbs Level 3 (dengan multiple meanings)

## Definisi & Target
Phrasal verbs level lanjutan yang memiliki dua atau lebih arti berbeda tergantung konteks. Target: memahami dan menggunakan phrasal verbs dengan banyak makna secara tepat.

## Analogi
Seperti kata "angkat" di Indonesia — bisa berarti "mengangkat barang", "mengangkat topik", atau "mengangkat telepon". Nah, phrasal verbs seperti "pick up" juga punya banyak arti.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Phrasal Verb | Multiple Meanings | Contoh |
|-------------|-------------------|--------|
| **Pick up** | (1) mengambil (2) menjemput (3) belajar (4) menjawab telepon | Pick up that book / Pick me up at 5 / I picked up some Spanish |
| **Break down** | (1) mogok (2) breakdown emosi (3) mengurai | The car broke down / She broke down crying / Break down the numbers |
| **Turn down** | (1) menolak (2) mengecilkan volume | He turned down the job / Turn down the music |
| **Bring up** | (1) membesarkan (2) mengangkat topik | She brought up three kids / Don't bring up that topic |
| **Go through** | (1) mengalami (2) memeriksa (3) menghabiskan | She went through a divorce / Go through these papers |
| **Make out** | (1) melihat/mendengar (2) berciuman (3) memahami | I can't make out the sign / They were making out / Can you make out what he said? |

## Contoh

1. **I need to pick up my dry cleaning.** — Saya harus mengambil cucian kering.
2. **She broke down the problem step by step.** — Dia mengurai masalah langkah demi langkah.
3. **He turned down a million-dollar offer.** — Dia menolak tawaran satu juta dolar.
4. **Why did you have to bring that up?** — Kenapa kamu harus angkat topik itu?
5. **I can't make out what this says — it's too blurry.** — Saya tidak bisa membaca ini — terlalu buram.

## Latihan

1. Sebutkan 3 arti "pick up" dan buat kalimat masing-masing.
2. Bedakan "break down" untuk mesin vs untuk emosi.
3. Buat 2 kalimat dengan "bring up" (membesarkan + mengangkat topik).
4. Lengkapi: He ___ ___ a lot of money on his vacation (menghabiskan).
5. Buat kalimat dengan "turn down" untuk penolakan dan volume.
