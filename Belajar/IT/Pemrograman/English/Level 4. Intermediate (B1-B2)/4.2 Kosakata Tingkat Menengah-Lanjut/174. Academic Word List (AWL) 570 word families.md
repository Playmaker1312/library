# 174. Academic Word List (AWL) 570 Word Families

## Definisi & Target
AWL adalah daftar 570 keluarga kata yang paling sering muncul dalam teks akademik, dikembangkan oleh Averil Coxhead. Target: menguasai kosakata esensial untuk membaca jurnal, esai, dan ujian IELTS/TOEFL.

## Analogi
Seperti "kata-kata wajib" di pelajaran sekolah — ini bukan kosakata sehari-hari yang dipakai ngobrol di warung, tapi kata-kata yang muncul di buku teks, jurnal ilmiah, dan perkuliahan.

## Daftar & Penjelasan (Sublist 1 — 60 kata paling umum)

| Kata | Makna | Contoh Akademik |
|------|-------|----------------|
| **Analyze** | Menganalisis | We need to analyze the data carefully |
| **Concept** | Konsep | This concept is fundamental to the theory |
| **Data** | Data | The data suggest a different conclusion |
| **Environment** | Lingkungan | The learning environment affects student performance |
| **Factor** | Faktor | Several factors contributed to the decline |
| **Income** | Pendapatan | Low-income families struggle with education costs |
| **Method** | Metode | The research method used was qualitative |
| **Percent** | Persen | Only 15 percent of participants agreed |
| **Process** | Proses | The decision-making process takes time |
| **Significant** | Signifikan | There was no significant difference between groups |
| **Theory** | Teori | This theory was proposed in the 1990s |
| **Variable** | Variabel | We controlled for age as a variable |

## Contoh

1. **The research analyzes the impact of social media on mental health.** — Penelitian menganalisis dampak media sosial terhadap kesehatan mental.
2. **Several environmental factors were considered in the study.** — Beberapa faktor lingkungan dipertimbangkan dalam studi.
3. **There is a significant correlation between exercise and mood.** — Ada korelasi signifikan antara olahraga dan suasana hati.

## Latihan

1. Cari 5 kata AWL dari sublist 2 dan tulis maknanya.
2. Buat kalimat akademik dengan "method", "process", dan "significant".
3. Gunakan "analyze" dalam konteks penelitian bisnis.
4. Jelaskan perbedaan "data" (plural) vs "datum" (singular).
5. Identifikasi 10 kata AWL dari abstrak jurnal berbahasa Inggris.
