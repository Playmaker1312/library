# 199. Report Writing (Formal Report)

## Definisi & Target
Formal report adalah laporan objektif yang menyajikan informasi, data, dan rekomendasi untuk pengambilan keputusan. Target: menulis laporan formal untuk keperluan akademik dan profesional.

## Analogi
Seperti laporan magang atau laporan penelitian: ada pendahuluan, metodologi, temuan, dan saran. Ini berbeda dari esai — report lebih faktual dan terstruktur dengan heading/subheading.

## Poin-Poin Penting

- **Sections:** Title page → Table of contents → Executive summary → Introduction → Findings → Conclusion → Recommendations → References.
- **Objective tone:** impersonal, netral, berbasis data.
- **Headings and subheadings:** memudahkan navigasi.
- **Data presentation:** gunakan tabel, grafik, bullet points.
- **Executive summary:** ringkasan seluruh laporan (1 halaman) — ditulis terakhir tapi diletakkan di depan.

## Struktur Penulisan

1. **Title Page:** Judul, penulis, tanggal.
2. **Executive Summary:** 1 paragraf — problem, method, key findings, recommendation.
3. **Introduction:** Background, purpose, scope.
4. **Findings:** Data dan analisis (bisa dibagi sub-sections).
5. **Conclusion:** Ringkasan temuan.
6. **Recommendations:** Saran aksi berdasarkan temuan.
7. **References:** Sumber data.

## Contoh

**Executive Summary:**
*"This report investigates customer satisfaction at XYZ Cafe. A survey of 100 customers was conducted. The findings indicate that while food quality is highly rated, service speed needs improvement. It is recommended that the cafe hires additional staff during peak hours."*

**Findings section:**
*"3.1 Customer Satisfaction Rating. As shown in Table 1, 75% of customers rated food quality as 'excellent,' but only 45% rated service speed as 'satisfactory.'"*

## Latihan

1. Tulis executive summary untuk laporan survei kepuasan siswa.
2. Buat outline laporan formal tentang "kebiasaan belajar mahasiswa."
3. Tulis satu findings section dengan data (buat data fiktif).
4. Tulis 3 rekomendasi berdasarkan findings tersebut.
5. Bedakan: apa perbedaan report dengan essay?
