# 205. Hedging Language (It seems that, It is likely that, tends to)

## Definisi & Target
Hedging adalah bahasa yang menunjukkan kehati-hatian dalam menyampaikan klaim. Target: menggunakan hedging untuk tulisan akademik dan profesional agar tidak terlalu absolut.

## Analogi
Seperti saksi di pengadilan: "Saya *kira-kira* melihat dia *mungkin* perjam *sekitar* jam 8" — bukan "Saya melihat dia pergi jam 8 tepat." Hedging melindungimu dari klaim yang terlalu pasti.

## Poin-Poin Penting

- **Modal verbs:** *may, might, could, would.*
- **Adverbs:** *probably, possibly, presumably, apparently, arguably.*
- **Impersonal phrases:** *It is believed that, It has been suggested that, It seems that.*
- **Hedging verbs:** *appears, seems, suggests, indicates, tends to.*
- **Quantity hedges:** *some, many, most, a majority of, a number of.*
- **Purpose:** menunjukkan ketidakpastian, sopan, menghindari klaim berlebihan.

## Struktur Penulisan

| Tanpa Hedging | Dengan Hedging |
|---------------|----------------|
| This proves that... | This suggests that... |
| Smoking causes cancer | Smoking is likely to cause cancer |
| The results show... | The results appear to indicate... |
| This is the best solution | This could be considered one of the better solutions |

## Contoh

1. **Without hedging:** *"Social media destroys real relationships."*
   **With hedging:** *"Social media **may** negatively affect face-to-face relationships."*
2. **Without hedging:** *"The government's policy failed."*
   **With hedging:** *"It could be argued that the government's policy was not entirely successful."*
3. **Without hedging:** *"Exercise improves mental health."*
   **With hedging:** *"Research suggests that exercise tends to improve mental health."*

## Latihan

1. Tambahkan hedging: "Online learning is less effective than face-to-face learning."
2. Ubah "This proves that the theory is correct" menggunakan hedging.
3. Gunakan "It seems that" untuk klaim tentang perubahan iklim.
4. Bedakan "may" vs "might" dalam tingkat kepastian.
5. Tulis 3 kalimat akademik dengan hedging yang tepat.
