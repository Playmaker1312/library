# 103. Percakapan Transaksional (di Hotel, Bandara, Rumah Sakit)

## Definisi & Target
Percakapan transaksional untuk situasi formal/layanan publik. Target: mampu berkomunikasi di hotel, bandara, dan rumah sakit dalam bahasa Inggris.

## Analogi
Seperti script drama — setiap situasi punya dialog standar. Di hotel, resepsionis punya pertanyaan baku; di bandara, petugas punya prosedur tetap.

## Poin-Poin Penting

### Di Hotel (At the Hotel)
| Frasa Petugas | Frasa Tamu |
|---|---|
| "Do you have a reservation?" | "I have a reservation under..." |
| "May I see your ID?" | "Here is my passport." |
| "Your room number is 305." | "What time is check-out?" |
| "Breakfast is from 7-10 AM." | "Can I get a wake-up call?" |

### Di Bandara (At the Airport)
| Frasa Petugas | Frasa Penumpang |
|---|---|
| "May I see your passport?" | "Here you go." |
| "Please put your bag here." | "Is this the right gate?" |
| "Your flight is delayed." | "What gate does my flight leave from?" |
| "Boarding starts at 7:30." | "How many kilograms can I carry?" |

### Di Rumah Sakit (At the Hospital)
| Frasa Dokter/Perawat | Frasa Pasien |
|---|---|
| "What seems to be the problem?" | "I have a headache / stomachache." |
| "How long have you felt this?" | "Since yesterday." |
| "Do you have any allergies?" | "I'm allergic to penicillin." |
| "Take this medicine 3 times a day." | "Do I need to come back?" |

### Tips Percakapan Transaksional
| Tips | Alasan |
|---|---|
| Gunakan "please" dan "thank you" | Sopan dan standar |
| Ucapkan informasi penting jelas | Menghindari kesalahan |
| Konfirmasi ulang info kritis | "So, room 305, correct?" |
| Jangan takut minta ulang | "Could you repeat that, please?" |

## Langkah-Langkah
1. Pelajari frasa standar untuk setiap situasi
2. Praktik role-play dengan teman
3. Hafalkan pertanyaan umum yang akan ditanyakan
4. Siapkan jawaban untuk info pribadi (nama, alamat, dll.)
5. Praktik minta klarifikasi jika tidak paham

## Contoh
Dialog Check-in Hotel:
- **Tamu:** "Hi, I have a reservation under the name Budi Santoso."
- **Resepsionis:** "Welcome, Mr. Santoso. Your room is 408. Here's your key card."
- **Tamu:** "Thank you. What time is breakfast?"
- **Resepsionis:** "From 7 to 10 AM at the restaurant on the second floor."

## Latihan
1. Role-play: check-in hotel (tamu + resepsionis)
2. Role-play: check-in bandara (petugas + penumpang)
3. Buat dialog: pasien ke dokter dengan keluhan sakit kepala
4. Tulis 3 pertanyaan yang bisa kamu tanyakan di hotel
5. Praktik: reserve hotel room via telepon dalam bahasa Inggris
