# 101. Pronunciation — Sentence Stress

## Definisi & Target
Sentence stress adalah penekanan pada kata-kata tertentu dalam kalimat. Target: berbicara dengan ritme yang alami.

## Analogi
Seperti irama dalam musik — ada ketukan yang lebih keras (stressed) dan lebih pelan (unstressed). Kalimat Inggris punya pola irama: kata penting ditekankan.

## Poin-Poin Penting

### Content Words vs Function Words
| Content Words (ditekankan) | Function Words (tidak ditekankan) |
|---|---|
| **Nouns** (book, house) | Articles (a, an, the) |
| **Main verbs** (run, eat) | Auxiliary verbs (is, have) |
| **Adjectives** (big, happy) | Prepositions (on, in, at) |
| **Adverbs** (quickly, very) | Pronouns (I, you, she) |
| **Question words** (what, where) | Conjunctions (and, but) |

### Aturan Dasar
- Content words ditekankan (lebih keras, lebih panjang)
- Function words diperlemah (lebih pelan, lebih pendek)
- Pola: da-DA-da-DA-da-DA

### Contoh Pola
| Kalimat | Pola stress |
|---|---|
| I **WENT** to **SCHOOL**. | da-DA-da-DA |
| She **LOVES** **COF**fee. | da-DA-DA-da |
| **WHAT** are you **DO**ing? | DA-da-da-DA-da |

### Fungsi Sentence Stress
| Fungsi | Contoh |
|---|---|
| Menekankan informasi baru | "I saw **Budi**, not Rina." |
| Kontras | "I **can** do it." (bisa, bukan tidak bisa) |
| Emosi | "That is **amazing**!" |
| Koreksi | "He **didn't** steal it." |

## Langkah-Langkah
1. Identifikasi content words dalam kalimat
2. Tekankan content words (ucap lebih keras)
3. Perlemah function words (ucap lebih pelan/cepat)
4. Praktik dengan pola ritme
5. Rekam dan bandingkan

## Contoh
Kalimat: "I am going to the market."
- Content words: **going**, **market**
- Fungsi: I (function) AM (aux — unstressed) GOING (stressed) TO (function) THE (function) MARket (stressed)
- Ucapan alami: "I'm goin' t' the market."

## Latihan
1. Tandai content words: "She bought a new car yesterday."
2. Ucapkan: "I love reading books" — tekan love dan books
3. Ucapkan: "What are you doing?" — tekan what dan doing
4. Praktik: "He arrived at the airport at 6 PM."
5. Rekam dirimu membaca 2 kalimat — perbaiki sentence stress
