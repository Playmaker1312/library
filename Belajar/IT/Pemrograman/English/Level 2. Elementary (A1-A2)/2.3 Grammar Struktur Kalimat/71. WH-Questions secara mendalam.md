# 71. WH-Questions Secara Mendalam

## Definisi & Target
WH-Questions adalah pertanyaan menggunakan kata tanya: What, Who, When, Where, Why, How, Which, Whose. Target: membuat pertanyaan dengan struktur yang benar.

## Analogi
Seperti detektif yang mengumpulkan informasi — setiap kata tanya mencari jenis informasi yang berbeda: orang, tempat, waktu, alasan, dll.

## Pola & Struktur

**Rumus:** WH-word + auxiliary + subject + main verb?

| WH-Word | Fungsi | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| What | benda / informasi | What is your name? |
| Who | orang (subjek) | Who is he? |
| Whom | orang (objek) | Whom did you call? |
| When | waktu | When does it start? |
| Where | tempat | Where do you live? |
| Why | alasan | Why are you sad? |
| How | cara / kondisi | How are you? |
| Which | pilihan | Which one is yours? |
| Whose | kepemilikan | Whose book is this? |

### WH-Question sebagai Subjek
Jika who/what adalah subjek, tidak pakai auxiliary:
- "**Who called** you?" (who = subjek)
- "**What happened**?" (what = subjek)
- Bandingkan: "**Who did you call**?" (who = objek)

### Perbedaan What vs Which
- **What:** pilihan umum/tidak terbatas — "What food do you like?"
- **Which:** pilihan terbatas — "Which flavor do you want: chocolate or vanilla?"

## Contoh
- "**Where** do you live?" — Di mana kamu tinggal?
- "**Why** are you studying English?" — Mengapa kamu belajar bahasa Inggris?
- "**How long** have you been here?" — Berapa lama kamu di sini?

## Latihan
1. Buat pertanyaan dengan What, Where, When untuk kalimat: "She goes to school at 7 AM."
2. Tanya alasan: "___ are you crying?"
3. Tanya cara: "___ do you make fried rice?"
4. Bedakan: "What movie do you want to watch?" vs "Which movie..."
5. Buat 5 pertanyaan WH tentang diri sendiri.
