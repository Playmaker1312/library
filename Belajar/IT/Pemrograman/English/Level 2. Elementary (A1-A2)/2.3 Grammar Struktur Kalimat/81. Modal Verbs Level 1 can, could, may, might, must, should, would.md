# 81. Modal Verbs Level 1: can, could, may, might, must, should, would

## Definisi & Target
Modal verbs adalah kata kerja bantu yang mengekspresikan kemampuan, izin, kewajiban, kemungkinan, saran. Target: menggunakan 7 modal verbs dasar.

## Analogi
Modal verbs seperti bumbu dapur — memberi nuansa pada kata kerja utama. "I go" (saya pergi) vs "I **must** go" (saya **harus** pergi) — nuansanya sangat berbeda.

## Pola & Struktur

**Rumus:** Subject + modal + base verb (V1 tanpa to)

| Modal | Fungsi | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Can** | kemampuan, izin informal | I **can** swim. / **Can** I go? |
| **Could** | kemampuan lampau, sopan | I **could** run fast. / **Could** you help? |
| **May** | izin formal, kemungkinan | **May** I come in? / It **may** rain. |
| **Might** | kemungkinan kecil | It **might** snow tonight. |
| **Must** | kewajiban kuat, kesimpulan | You **must** wear a helmet. / She **must** be tired. |
| **Should** | saran, rekomendasi | You **should** see a doctor. |
| **Would** | permintaan sopan, kebiasaan | **Would** you like tea? / I **would** often visit. |

### Negatif
- can → cannot / can't
- could → could not / couldn't
- may → may not
- must → must not / mustn't (larangan)
- should → should not / shouldn't

### Perbedaan Halus
| Situasi | Modal yang Tepat |
|---|---|
| Izin informal | Can I...? |
| Izin formal | May I...? |
| Permintaan sopan | Could you...? / Would you...? |
| Saran | You should... |
| Kewajiban | You must... |
| Kemungkinan (besar) | It may... |
| Kemungkinan (kecil) | It might... |

## Contoh
- "**Can** you speak English?" — Bisakah kamu bicara bahasa Inggris?
- "You **should** study harder." — Kamu harus belajar lebih giat.
- "**May** I borrow your pen?" — Bolehkah saya pinjam pulpenmu?
- "We **must** arrive on time." — Kami harus tiba tepat waktu.

## Latihan
1. Pilih modal: "You (can/must) ___ stop at a red light."
2. Pilih modal: "She (can/might) ___ come to the party, but she's not sure."
3. Minta izin sopan menggunakan "May I..."
4. Beri saran menggunakan "You should..."
5. Buat 5 kalimat menggunakan 5 modal verbs berbeda.
