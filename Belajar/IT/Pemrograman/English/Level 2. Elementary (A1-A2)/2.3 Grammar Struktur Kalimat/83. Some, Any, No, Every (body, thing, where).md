# 83. Some, Any, No, Every (body, thing, where)

## Definisi & Target
Compound words dengan some, any, no, every: somebody, anybody, nobody, everybody; something, anything, nothing, everything; somewhere, anywhere, nowhere, everywhere. Target: menggunakannya dalam kalimat.

## Analogi
Seperti "seseorang", "siapa pun", "tidak seorang pun", "setiap orang" — ini adalah padanan sistematis dalam bahasa Inggris dengan pola yang konsisten.

## Pola & Struktur

### Tabel Compound Words

| | some | any | no | every |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **-body** | somebody | anybody | nobody | everybody |
| **-one** | someone | anyone | no one | everyone |
| **-thing** | something | anything | nothing | everything |
| **-where** | somewhere | anywhere | nowhere | everywhere |

### Aturan Penggunaan

| Kata | Penggunaan | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Some-** | Kalimat positif, tawaran, permintaan | **Somebody** is at the door. / Would you like **something** to drink? |
| **Any-** | Kalimat negatif, pertanyaan | I don't know **anybody** here. / Do you have **anything** to say? |
| **No-** | Kalimat positif (artinya negatif) | **Nobody** knows. / There is **nothing** to do. |
| **Every-** | Semua/segala | **Everybody** loves her. / **Everything** is fine. |

### Perbedaan Important
- "I have **something**." (positif — saya punya sesuatu)
- "I don't have **anything**." (negatif — saya tidak punya apa-apa)
- "I have **nothing**." (positif bentuk — sama artinya dengan di atas)

### Kata Sifat Setelah Compound
Kata sifat ditempatkan **setelah** compound word:
- "I need **something cold** to drink." (bukan cold something)
- "**Someone special** is coming."

## Contoh
- "**Somebody** is knocking on the door." — Seseorang mengetuk pintu.
- "I can't find my keys **anywhere**." — Saya tidak bisa menemukan kunci saya di mana pun.
- "**Nothing** is impossible." — Tidak ada yang mustahil.
- "**Everything** is going to be okay." — Semuanya akan baik-baik saja.

## Latihan
1. Isi: "There is ___ (some/any) milk in the fridge."
2. Isi: "I don't know ___ (somebody/anybody) here."
3. Ubah ke negatif: "I have something."
4. Ubah ke no-: "There isn't anything to eat."
5. Buat 5 kalimat menggunakan 5 compound words berbeda.
