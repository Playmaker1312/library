# 79. Too and Enough

## Definisi & Target
"Too" berarti kelebihan (negatif), "enough" berarti cukup (positif atau negatif). Target: membedakan dan menggunakan too/enough dengan benar.

## Analogi
Too seperti "kebanyakan" — "kopinya kepanasan" (too hot, jadi tidak bisa diminum). Enough seperti "cukup" — "kopinya sudah cukup dingin" (cool enough, jadi bisa diminum).

## Pola & Struktur

### Too (terlalu — berlebihan, negatif)
**Rumus:** too + adjective/adverb (+ for someone) + to-infinitive

- "This coffee is **too hot** to drink."
- "She is **too young** to drive."
- "The box is **too heavy** for me to carry."

### Enough (cukup)
**Rumus 1 (adjective):** adjective + enough (+ for someone) + to-infinitive
- "This coffee is **cool enough** to drink."
- "He is **old enough** to drive."

**Rumus 2 (noun):** enough + noun (+ for someone) + to-infinitive
- "I have **enough money** to buy that."
- "There isn't **enough time**."

### Perbedaan Penting
| Too (negatif) | Enough (positif/negatif) |
|---|---|
| "too slow" → terlalu lambat (tidak bisa) | "fast enough" → cukup cepat (bisa) |
| "too expensive" → terlalu mahal | "cheap enough" → cukup murah |
| "too much sugar" → terlalu banyak gula | "enough sugar" → gula cukup |

## Contoh
- "This bag is **too small** for my books." — Tas ini terlalu kecil untuk buku-bukuku.
- "She is **tall enough** to reach the shelf." — Dia cukup tinggi untuk meraih rak.
- "There is **too much** traffic." — Terlalu banyak kemacetan.
- "Do you have **enough** money?" — Apakah kamu punya cukup uang?

## Latihan
1. Isi too/enough: "This soup is ___ hot to eat."
2. Isi too/enough: "He is not old ___ to vote."
3. Gabungkan: "The car is expensive. I can't buy it." (gunakan too)
4. Gabungkan: "She is strong. She can lift the box." (gunakan enough)
5. Buat 2 kalimat too dan 2 kalimat enough.
