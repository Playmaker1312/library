# 73. Subject-Verb Agreement (Aturan Lengkap)

## Definisi & Target
Subject-Verb Agreement: subjek dan kata kerja harus sesuai jumlah (singular/plural). Target: menghindari kesalahan umum dalam mencocokkan subjek-verb.

## Analogi
Seperti sepatu — kiri dengan kiri, kanan dengan kanan. Subjek singular pakai verb singular, subjek plural pakai verb plural.

## Pola & Struktur

### Aturan Dasar
| Subjek | Verb | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| Singular (he/she/it) | Verb + s/es | **He walks** to school. |
| Plural (I/you/we/they) | Verb tanpa s | **They walk** to school. |

### Aturan Khusus
1. **Subjek dengan and** → plural: "**Rina and Budi are** friends."
2. **Either/or, neither/nor** → verb sesuai subjek terdekat: "Either my dad **or** my sisters **are** coming."
3. **Each, every, everyone** → singular: "**Everyone is** here."
4. **Kolektif (team, family)** → bisa singular/plural: "The team **is** playing well." (kesatuan)
5. **There is/are** → sesuai noun setelahnya: "**There is** a book." / "**There are** two books."
6. **Indefinite pronoun (some, any, all)** → tergantung noun: "Some of the water **is** gone." / "Some of the books **are** missing."

### Kesalahan Umum
- ✗ "He **don't** like it." → ✓ "He **doesn't** like it."
- ✗ "She **go** to school." → ✓ "She **goes** to school."
- ✗ "The list of items **are** long." → ✓ "The list **is** long."

## Contoh
- "**My sister likes** swimming." (singular) — Adikku suka berenang.
- "**My sisters like** swimming." (plural) — Adik-adikku suka berenang.
- "**Each of the students has** a book." — Setiap murid punya buku.

## Latihan
1. Pilih: "She (go/goes) to school by bus."
2. Pilih: "The dogs (bark/barks) loudly."
3. Perbaiki: "Everyone are happy."
4. Isi: "Either the teacher or the students ___ (is/are) wrong."
5. Buat 3 kalimat dengan subject-verb agreement yang benar.
