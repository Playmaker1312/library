# 72. Tag Questions (You're a student, aren't you?)

## Definisi & Target
Tag question adalah pertanyaan pendek di akhir kalimat untuk konfirmasi. Target: membentuk tag question dengan benar.

## Analogi
Seperti di Indonesia kita bilang "kan?" di akhir kalimat — "Kamu murid, kan?" Tag question versi Inggrisnya: "You're a student, aren't you?"

## Pola & Struktur

**Aturan Emas:** Kalimat positif → tag negatif. Kalimat negatif → tag positif.

| Kalimat | Tag | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| Positif | Negatif | "You are tired, **aren't you**?" |
| Negatif | Positif | "You aren't tired, **are you**?" |

### Tenses dalam Tag Questions
| Tense | Contoh |
|---|---|
| Present (is/am/are) | She is nice, **isn't she**? |
| Present (do/does) | You like coffee, **don't you**? |
| Past (was/were) | He was late, **wasn't he**? |
| Past (did) | They came, **didn't they**? |
| Present Perfect | She has left, **hasn't she**? |
| Will | You will come, **won't you**? |

### Kasus Khusus
- **I am** → "I am wrong, **aren't I**?" (bukan amn't I)
- **Let's** → "Let's go, **shall we**?"
- **Imperatif** → "Open the door, **will you**?"
- **There is/are** → "There is a problem, **isn't there**?"

## Contoh
- "You **are** from Indonesia, **aren't you**?" — Kamu dari Indonesia, kan?
- "She **doesn't** like spicy food, **does she**?" — Dia tidak suka pedas, kan?
- "**Let's** eat out, **shall we**?" — Ayo makan di luar, yuk?

## Latihan
1. Tambah tag: "You're a teacher, ___?"
2. Tambah tag: "She doesn't smoke, ___?"
3. Tag untuk: "They have finished, ___?"
4. Tag untuk: "I am correct, ___?"
5. Buat 3 tag question tentang dirimu sendiri.
