# 53. Kata Kerja Frase Dasar (Phrasal Verbs Level 1)

## Definisi & Target
Phrasal verbs adalah gabungan kata kerja + preposisi/adverb yang menghasilkan makna baru. Target: menguasai 20 phrasal verbs dasar sehari-hari.

## Analogi
Seperti kata "angkat kaki" dalam bahasa Indonesia — artinya bukan benar-benar mengangkat kaki, tapi pergi. Phrasal verbs juga punya arti khusus yang tidak selalu literal.

## Daftar Kosakata

| Phrasal Verb | Arti | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| get up | bangun tidur | I get up at 6 AM. |
| wake up | terbangun | She wakes up early. |
| turn on | menyalakan | Turn on the light. |
| turn off | mematikan | Turn off the TV. |
| pick up | menjemput/mengambil | Pick me up at 5. |
| put down | meletakkan | Put down the book. |
| put on | memakai (baju) | Put on your jacket. |
| take off | melepas/lepas landas | Take off your shoes. |
| look for | mencari | I'm looking for my keys. |
| look after | merawat | She looks after her sister. |
| come in | masuk | Please come in. |
| go out | keluar/berpergian | Let's go out tonight. |
| sit down | duduk | Please sit down. |
| stand up | berdiri | Stand up, please. |
| slow down | melambat | Slow down! |

## Contoh
- "Wake up! It's 7 AM." — Bangun! Sudah jam 7 pagi.
- "Can you turn on the fan?" — Bisakah kamu menyalakan kipas angin?
- "I need to pick up my friend at the station." — Aku perlu menjemput temanku di stasiun.

## Latihan
1. Terjemahkan: "Tolong matikan AC."
2. Isi titik-titik: "Every morning, I ___ at 5 AM and ___ my clothes."
3. Pasangkan phrasal verb dengan artinya: get up, look after, turn off, put down.
4. Buat 3 kalimat menggunakan pick up, turn on, dan go out.
5. Perbaiki kalimat: "I need to look after my keys."
