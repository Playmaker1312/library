# 55. Word Families (act, action, active, actively)

## Definisi & Target
Word family adalah kelompok kata yang berasal dari akar (root) yang sama. Target: memahami perubahan bentuk kata (noun, verb, adjective, adverb) dari akar yang sama.

## Analogi
Seperti pohon dengan cabang-cabang: akarnya sama tapi cabangnya berbeda — verb, noun, adjective, adverb dari satu induk kata.

## Daftar Kosakata

| Root | Verb | Noun (person) | Noun (thing) | Adjective | Adverb |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| act | act | actor/actress | action | active | actively |
| beauty | beautify | beautician | beauty | beautiful | beautifully |
| care | care | caregiver | care | careful/careless | carefully |
| differ | differ | — | difference | different | differently |
| educate | educate | educator | education | educational | educationally |
| employ | employ | employer/employee | employment | employed | — |
| happy | — | — | happiness | happy | happily |
| help | help | helper | help | helpful | helpfully |
| succeed | succeed | successor | success | successful | successfully |
| create | create | creator | creation | creative | creatively |

## Contoh
- "He **acts** in a movie." (verb)
- "His **action** was brave." (noun)
- "She is an **active** student." (adjective)
- "She **actively** participates." (adverb)

## Latihan
1. Tentukan word family dari kata **beauty** (verb, adjective, adverb).
2. Isi: "The ___ (create) team worked ___ (creative)."
3. Ubah "success" menjadi adjective dan adverb.
4. Buat kalimat menggunakan "act" sebagai verb dan "action" sebagai noun.
5. Lengkapi: "She is a ___ (care) person. She drives ___ (care)."
