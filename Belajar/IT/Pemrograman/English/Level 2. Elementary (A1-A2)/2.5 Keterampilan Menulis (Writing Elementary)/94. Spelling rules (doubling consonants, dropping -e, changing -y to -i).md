# 94. Spelling Rules (Doubling Consonants, Dropping -e, Changing -y to -i)

## Definisi & Target
Aturan ejaan saat menambahkan akhiran (-ing, -ed, -er, -est, -ly). Target: mengeja kata turunan dengan benar.

## Analogi
Seperti aturan tata bahasa Indonesia "di-" vs "di " — ada aturan yang harus diikuti agar ejaan benar. Dalam Inggris ada 3 aturan utama.

## Poin-Poin Penting

### Aturan 1: Double the Consonant (Gandakan Konsonan)
**Syarat:** 1 suku kata, 1 vowel + 1 consonant di akhir

| Base | + -ing | + -ed | + -er |
|---|---|---|---|
| run | running | — | runner |
| big | — | — | bigger |
| stop | stopping | stopped | stopper |
| sit | sitting | — | — |

**Pengecualian:** Jika akhir kata adalah -w, -x, -y: **play** → playing, **fix** → fixing

### Aturan 2: Drop the -e (Hilangkan -e)
**Syarat:** Kata berakhir dengan -e, dan akhiran dimulai dengan vowel

| Base | + -ing | + -ed | Aturan |
|---|---|---|---|
| make | making | — | hilang -e |
| write | writing | — | hilang -e |
| dance | dancing | danced | hilang -e |
| hope | hoping | hoped | hilang -e |
| **Kecuali:** | | | |
| be | being | — | -e tetap |
| see | seeing | — | -e tetap |

**Untuk akhiran konsonan (-ful, -ly, -ment):** -e tetap: **hope** → hopeful, **care** → carefully

### Aturan 3: Change -y to -i (Ubah -y menjadi -i)
**Syarat:** Akhiran -y didahului konsonan

| Base | + -er | + -est | + -ed | + -ly |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| happy | happier | happiest | — | happily |
| easy | easier | easiest | — | easily |
| study | — | — | studied | — |
| carry | — | — | carried | — |

**Kecuali:** Jika -y didahului vowel: **play** → played, **enjoy** → enjoyed
**Pengecualian untuk -ing:** tetap -y: **study** → studying, **carry** → carrying

## Struktur Penulisan
1. Identifikasi akhiran kata (vowel/konsonan)
2. Tentukan aturan yang berlaku
3. Terapkan perubahan ejaan
4. Verifikasi dengan kamus jika ragu

## Contoh
- **run** + ing = **running** (double consonant)
- **make** + ing = **making** (drop -e)
- **happy** + er = **happier** (y → i)
- **play** + ed = **played** (y didahului vowel, tetap)

## Latihan
1. Double consonant: stop + ing = ___, sit + ing = ___
2. Drop -e: write + ing = ___, dance + ed = ___
3. Y → i: easy + ly = ___, study + ed = ___
4. Perbaiki: "I am swiming in the pool."
5. Tulis 5 kata + -ing dengan ejaan yang benar
