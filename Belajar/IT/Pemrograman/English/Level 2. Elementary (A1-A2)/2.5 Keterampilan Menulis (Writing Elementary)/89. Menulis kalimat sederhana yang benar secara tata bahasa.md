# 89. Menulis Kalimat Sederhana yang Benar Secara Tata Bahasa

## Definisi & Target
Menulis kalimat sederhana dengan struktur yang benar (S + V + O/C). Target: menghasilkan kalimat gramatikal tanpa error dasar.

## Analogi
Seperti membangun rumah — pondasinya adalah subjek, dindingnya kata kerja, atapnya objek. Kalau pondasi miring, seluruh rumah bermasalah.

## Poin-Poin Penting

### Struktur Dasar Kalimat
| Tipe | Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| S + V | Subject + Verb | Birds fly. |
| S + V + O | Subject + Verb + Object | She reads a book. |
| S + V + C | Subject + Verb + Complement | He is a teacher. |
| S + V + O + O | Subject + Verb + O indirect + O direct | She gave me a gift. |
| S + V + O + C | Subject + Verb + Object + Complement | They made her happy. |

### Ciri Kalimat Benar
- **Capital letter** di awal, **punctuation** di akhir
- **Subject-verb agreement** (She goes, not She go)
- **Tense yang konsisten** (tidak campur aduk)
- **Kata lengkap** — bukan fragmen

### Fragmen yang Harus Dihindari
| Fragmen (salah) | Kalimat benar |
|---|---|
| Because I was tired. | I went home because I was tired. |
| A beautiful day. | It is a beautiful day. |
| Running in the park. | She is running in the park. |

## Struktur Penulisan
1. Tentukan subjek (siapa/apa?)
2. Pilih verb (kata kerja)
3. Tambah objek/pelengkap jika perlu
4. Pastikan tense sesuai
5. Cek subject-verb agreement
6. Tambah capital letter dan punctuation

## Contoh
- ✗ "she go to school everyday" → ✓ "She **goes** to school every day."
- ✗ "He is a student and study hard." → ✓ "He is a student and **studies** hard."
- ✗ "Because I love reading." → ✓ "**I love reading because** it is fun."

## Latihan
1. Perbaiki: "i like to swimming"
2. Perbaiki: "She don't like coffee"
3. Perbaiki fragmen: "Walking to school every morning"
4. Buat 3 kalimat S + V + O tentang rutinitas harian
5. Tulis 5 kalimat tentang dirimu dalam bahasa Inggris
