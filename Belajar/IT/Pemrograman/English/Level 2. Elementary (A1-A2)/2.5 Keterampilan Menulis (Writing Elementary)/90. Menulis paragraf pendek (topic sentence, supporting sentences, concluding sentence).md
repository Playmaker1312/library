# 90. Menulis Paragraf Pendek (Topic Sentence, Supporting Sentences, Concluding Sentence)

## Definisi & Target
Menulis paragraf pendek (3-5 kalimat) dengan struktur: topik, penjelasan, kesimpulan. Target: mampu mengorganisir ide dalam paragraf.

## Analogi
Paragraf seperti burger: roti atas (topic sentence), isi daging sayur (supporting sentences), roti bawah (concluding sentence). Ketiganya harus ada.

## Poin-Poin Penting

### Struktur Paragraf
| Bagian | Fungsi | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **Topic Sentence** | Ide utama paragraf | "I have a busy daily routine." |
| **Supporting Sentences** | Detail, contoh, alasan | "I wake up at 5 AM. Then I go to school. After school, I study." |
| **Concluding Sentence** | Simpulan/penutup | "That's why my day is always busy." |

### Ciri Topic Sentence yang Baik
- Kalimat pertama dalam paragraf
- Berisi ide utama secara umum
- Tidak terlalu spesifik, tidak terlalu luas
- Mengundang pembaca untuk tahu lebih lanjut

### Ciri Supporting Sentences yang Baik
- Mendukung topic sentence
- Bisa berupa fakta, contoh, alasan, atau detail
- Urut secara logis (kronologis, prioritas, dll)

### Ciri Concluding Sentence yang Baik
- Merangkum ide paragraf
- Bisa menggunakan sinonim dari topic sentence
- Sering pakai: In conclusion, Therefore, That's why

## Struktur Penulisan
1. Tulis topic sentence (ide utama)
2. Tulis 2-3 supporting sentences
3. Tulis concluding sentence
4. Pastikan semua kalimat relevan dengan topik
5. Cek koherensi (hubungan antar kalimat)

## Contoh
**Topic Sentence:** "My favorite hobby is reading."
**Supporting:** "I read every night before sleeping. I like mystery and fantasy books. Reading helps me relax and learn new things."
**Concluding:** "That's why I spend at least one hour reading every day."

## Latihan
1. Tulis topic sentence untuk: "tentang keluarga"
2. Buat supporting sentences untuk: "I love cooking."
3. Tulis concluding sentence untuk paragraf tentang hobi
4. Tulis paragraf lengkap (4-5 kalimat) tentang rutinitas harian
5. Tulis paragraf tentang tempat favoritmu
