# 67. Perbedaan Simple Past vs Past Continuous

## Definisi & Target
Membedakan Simple Past (aksi selesai) dengan Past Continuous (aksi sedang berlangsung) di masa lalu. Target: mengombinasikan kedua tense dengan tepat.

## Analogi
Simple Past seperti foto — menangkap momen selesai. Past Continuous seperti video — menangkap proses yang sedang terjadi.

## Pola & Struktur

| Aspek | Simple Past | Past Continuous |
|---|---|---|
| Aksi | Selesai | Sedang berlangsung |
| Durasi | Biasanya singkat | Biasanya lebih panjang |
| Rumus | Subject + V2 | Subject + was/were + V-ing |
| Contoh | "I ate." | "I was eating." |

### Kombinasi Keduanya
Sering digunakan bersama. Simple Past untuk aksi yang **memotong** (interrupt), Past Continuous untuk latar belakang.

- "**I was watching** TV **when** she **came** home."
  (sedang nonton → latar; datang → interrupt)
- "**While** I **was cooking**, I **cut** my finger."
  (sedang masak → latar; teriris → interrupt)

### Kata Kunci
- **Simple Past:** suddenly, then, when (interruption)
- **Past Continuous:** while, as, at that moment

## Contoh
- "**She walked** to school." (sudah selesai — Simple Past)
- "**She was walking** to school when it rained." (sedang berjalan — Past Continuous, diguyur hujan — Simple Past)
- "**They played** football yesterday." (selesai) vs "**They were playing** football at 4 PM." (sedang bermain)

## Latihan
1. Pilih tense: "I ___ (have) dinner when the phone ___ (ring)."
2. Lengkapi: "While we ___ (travel), we ___ (meet) an old friend."
3. Bedakan arti: "He wrote a letter" vs "He was writing a letter."
4. Buat kalimat kombinasi (Past Continuous + Simple Past).
5. Ceritakan apa yang kamu lakukan ketika hujan turun kemarin.
