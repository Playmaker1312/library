# 65. Perbedaan Simple Past vs Present Perfect

## Definisi & Target
Membedakan Simple Past (aksi selesai di masa lalu) dengan Present Perfect (aksi terkait sekarang). Target: memilih tense yang tepat dalam konteks.

## Analogi
Simple Past seperti film yang sudah selesai ditonton — waktunya jelas dan selesai. Present Perfect seperti trailer yang masih terasa dampaknya sampai sekarang.

## Pola & Struktur

| Aspek | Simple Past | Present Perfect |
|---|---|---|
| Waktu | Spesifik (yesterday, last year, in 2010) | Tidak spesifik (ever, never, already, just) |
| Fokus | Aksi selesai | Hubungan dengan sekarang |
| Rumus | Subject + V2 | Subject + have/has + V3 |

### Kapan pakai Simple Past?
- Waktu disebut: "**I went** to Bali **last year**."
- Aksi selesai total: "**She called** me **an hour ago**."
- Kebiasaan masa lalu: "**I always walked** to school when I was young."

### Kapan pakai Present Perfect?
- Pengalaman: "**I have visited** Bali three times."
- Baru saja: "**He has just left**."
- Belum terjadi: "**She hasn't arrived yet**."
- Masih berlangsung: "**We have known** each other for 10 years."

## Contoh
- "**I saw** that movie yesterday." (Simple Past — waktu jelas) vs "**I have seen** that movie." (Present Perfect — tidak penting kapan)
- "**She lived** in London in 2015." (sudah selesai) vs "**She has lived** in London since 2015." (masih tinggal)

## Latihan
1. Pilih tense: "I ___ (go) to the doctor yesterday."
2. Pilih tense: "She ___ (never/be) to Europe."
3. Ubah Simple Past ke Present Perfect: "I ate lunch at 12."
4. Isi titik-titik: "We ___ (know) each other since childhood."
5. Buat 2 kalimat — satu Simple Past, satu Present Perfect — tentang liburan.
