# 323. IELTS General Training

## Definisi & Target
IELTS General Training untuk keperluan migrasi, kerja, atau sekolah non-akademik. Target: Band 6.0–7.5 (B2–C1).

## Analogi
IELTS GT seperti tes SIM untuk "daily driving" — bukan balap akademik, tapi navigasi kehidupan sehari-hari dan kantor.

## Struktur & Strategi

### Reading (60 menit, 3 sections, 40 soal)
- **Section 1:** Social survival — iklan, jadwal, brochure (2–3 teks pendek).
- **Section 2:** Workplace — kontrak kerja, training manual, company policy.
- **Section 3:** General interest — koran, majalah, narasi (1 teks panjang).
- **Strategi:** Fokus pada scanning untuk factual info. Section 3 baca dulu pertanyaan.

### Listening (30 menit — SAMA dengan Academic)
Sama persis dengan IELTS Academic. Semua peserta tes mendengarkan audio yang identik.

### Speaking (11–14 menit — SAMA dengan Academic)
Format dan kriteria penilaian identik dengan Academic.

### Writing (60 menit, 2 tasks)
- **Task 1 (20m, 150+ words):** Surat (formal, semi-formal, informal).
- **Task 2 (40m, 250+ words):** Essay (topik umum, sama dengan Academic tetapi lebih simpel).
- **Tips Task 1:** Formal letter = request/complaint; Semi-formal = boss/colleague; Informal = friend.
- **Tips Task 2:** Topik: family, technology, environment, society. Gaya bahasa tidak perlu terlalu akademik.

## Contoh
**Task 1 Informal Letter:** "Write a letter to a friend inviting them to stay at your house."
- Opening: Dear [Name], How have you been?...
- Body: Invitation details + dates + activities planned
- Closing: Can't wait to see you! Best wishes,...

## Latihan
1. Baca 1 job advertisement, jawab 5 soal multiple choice, skor sendiri.
2. Listening Section 1 latihan — booking hotel via phone, lengkapi form booking.
3. Speaking Part 1: "What do you like about your job or studies?" jawab 4–5 kalimat.
4. Tulis 1 formal letter complaint: "You bought a faulty product online."
5. Tulis Task 2 essay: "Some people think working from home is better than the office. Discuss."
