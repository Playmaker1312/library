# 324. Cambridge Exams FCE (B2 First)

## Definisi & Target
B2 First (FCE) adalah sertifikat Cambridge English level upper-intermediate. Target: Grade B atau A (setara IELTS 5.5–6.5).

## Analogi
FCE seperti "SIM level provinsi" — sudah bisa bepergian mandiri, kadang masih perlu GPS untuk kata-kata rumit.

## Struktur & Strategi

### Reading & Use of English (1 jam 15 menit, 7 parts)
- **Part 1:** Multiple-choice cloze (vocabulary — collocations, phrasal verbs).
- **Part 2:** Open cloze (grammar — prepositions, articles, auxiliaries).
- **Part 3:** Word formation (prefix/suffix — e.g., "able" → "disable").
- **Part 4–7:** Gapped text, multiple matching, multiple choice.
- **Strategi:** Perhatikan collocations dan dependent prepositions. Use of English = grammar in context.

### Writing (1 jam 20 menit, 2 tasks)
- **Task 1:** Essay (140–190 words).
- **Task 2:** Pilihan — article, email/letter, report, atau review.
- **Strategi:** Pelajari format tiap jenis writing. Essay selalu butuh introduction + 2 arguments + conclusion.

### Listening (40 menit, 4 parts)
- **Part 1:** Multiple choice (short extracts).
- **Part 2:** Sentence completion.
- **Part 3:** Multiple matching (5 speakers, 8 options).
- **Part 4:** Multiple choice (long interview/discussion).
- **Strategi:** Part 2 — baca pertanyaan, tebak jenis kata yang hilang (noun? verb? number?).

### Speaking (14 menit, 4 parts)
- Interview 2 candidates + 1 examiner.
- **Part 1:** Personal questions (2 menit).
- **Part 2:** Individual long turn — compare 2 photos (1 menit).
- **Part 3:** Collaborative task — discussion dengan partner (3 menit).
- **Part 4:** Discussion related to Part 3 (4 menit).

## Contoh
**Word Formation (Reading & Use of English Part 3):**
- Base word: "COMPETE"
- Jawaban: "competition" (noun), "competitive" (adj), "competitively" (adv)

## Latihan
1. Kerjakan 1 part Use of English — Word Formation: "able" → ability, disability, inability.
2. Tulis essay 180 kata: "Some people prefer online shopping. Do you agree or disagree?"
3. Listening Part 2 — dengar audio singkat, isi 5 blank dalam teks.
4. Speaking Part 2 — ambil 2 foto, deskripsikan + compare dalam 1 menit (rekam).
5. Review jawaban dengan sample answer dari Cambridge Official Guide.
