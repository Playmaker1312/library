# 326. Cambridge Exams CPE (C2 Proficiency)

## Definisi & Target
CPE (C2 Proficiency) adalah sertifikat tertinggi Cambridge — menandakan kemampuan near-native. Target: Pass (Grade C) atau Pass with A/B.

## Analogi
CPE seperti "SIM balap F1" — bukan cuma bisa nyetir kencang, tapi paham mesin, aerodinamika, dan strategi pit stop dalam bahasa Inggris.

## Struktur & Strategi

### Reading & Use of English (1 jam 30 menit, 7 parts)
- **Part 1–3:** Multiple-choice cloze, open cloze, word formation — vocabulary sangat sophisticated.
- **Part 4:** Key word transformations — tes grammar kompleks (inversion, conditional mixed, passive reporting).
- **Part 5–7:** Multiple choice, gapped text, multiple matching — teks dari literature, journal, editorial.
- **Strategi:** Reading antara baris (inferensi, tone, implied meaning). Vocabulary: idioms, collocations tingkat lanjut.

### Writing (1 jam 30 menit, 2 tasks)
- **Task 1:** Essay (240–280 words) — formal, analytical.
- **Task 2:** Pilihan — article, report, review, letter, atau proposal.
- **Strategi:** C2 writing butuh nuanced argument + precise vocabulary + complex structures.

### Listening (40 menit, 4 parts)
- Semua audio sekali putar. Topics: lectures, interviews, discussions, documentaries.
- Frequently tests inference, gist, speaker attitude.

### Speaking (16 menit, 3 parts)
- **Part 1 (2m):** Interview questions.
- **Part 2 (4m):** Collaborative task — discuss abstract visual prompt.
- **Part 3 (10m):** Long turn + discussion — present and defend position.
- **Strategi Kunci:** Gunakan discourse markers (furthermore, nevertheless, having said that) dan hedging (tends to, may suggest).

## Contoh
**Part 4 Key Word Transformation C2 Level:**
- Prompt: "People think that the CEO was aware of the scandal." (IS)
- Jawaban: "The CEO IS believed to HAVE BEEN aware of the scandal."
- Tes: passive reporting structure.

## Latihan
1. Baca 1 editorial The Guardian, identifikasi 5 implied meanings + tone penulis.
2. Transform 5 kalimat dengan "regret + gerund/infinitive" dan "would rather + past perfect".
3. Tulis review 280 kata tentang film/serial yang baru ditonton — gunakan 10 idioms.
4. Listening — dengar 1 podcast Radiolab, transkrip 2 menit, cari 5 discourse markers.
5. Speaking Part 3 — record yourself discussing: "The role of art in modern society" (4 menit).
