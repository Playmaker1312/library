# 322. IELTS Academic Reading, Listening, Speaking, Writing — Strategi dan Latihan

## Definisi & Target
IELTS Academic mengukur kesiapan studi di lingkungan berbahasa Inggris. Target: Band 7.0–8.5 (C1–C2).

## Analogi
IELTS seperti ujian SIM internasional — tidak cukup bisa baca, harus paham nuansa, aksen, dan gaya tulisan akademik.

## Struktur & Strategi

### Reading (60 menit, 3 passages, 40 soal)
- **Tipe soal:** Multiple choice, matching headings, True/False/Not Given, summary completion.
- **Strategi:** Jawab pertanyaan sambil baca (scanning). Latih skimming 300 kata/menit.
- **Common trap:** "Not Given" bukan "False" — bedakan dengan cermat.

### Listening (30 menit, 4 sections, 40 soal)
- **Section 1–2:** Social survival (booking hotel, directions).
- **Section 3–4:** Academic discussion dan lecture.
- **Strategi:** Baca pertanyaan SEBELUM audio dimulai. Perhatikan synonyms — jawaban jarang kata persis dari audio.

### Speaking (11–14 menit, 3 parts)
- **Part 1 (4–5m):** Daily topics (work, hobbies). Jawab 2–3 kalimat.
- **Part 2 (3–4m):** Long turn — 1 menit preparation, 1–2 menit bicara.
- **Part 3 (4–5m):** Abstract discussion — beri opinion + example + contrast.
- **Strategi:** Jangan hafal jawaban. Latih fluency dengan speaking partner.

### Writing (60 menit, 2 tasks)
- **Task 1 (20m, 150+ words):** Describe chart/graph/diagram.
- **Task 2 (40m, 250+ words):** Essay — opinion, discussion, problem-solution.
- **Strategi:** Task 1: Overview dulu, baru detail. Task 2: 4 paragraf, gunakan cohesive devices.

## Contoh
**Writing Task 1:** "The chart shows population growth in three cities..."
**Struktur:**
- Intro: Paraphrase soal
- Overview: Trend umum (all three cities showed steady increase...)
- Detail 1: Data city A dan B
- Detail 2: Data city C dengan perbandingan

## Latihan
1. Cari 1 artikel BBC News, skimming 3 menit, jawab 10 True/False/Not Given.
2. Dengar 1 section IELTS Listening di YouTube, transkrip bagian yang salah.
3. Speaking Part 2: "Describe a place you visited that surprised you" — rekam, transkrip, evaluasi.
4. Tulis Task 1 Academic: cari contoh grafik di IELTS Liz, tulis 150 kata.
5. Bandingkan jawaban dengan sample band 9, identifikasi gap vocabulary.
