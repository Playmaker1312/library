# 321. TOEFL iBT Reading, Listening, Speaking, Writing — Strategi dan Latihan

## Definisi & Target
TOEFL iBT menguji kemampuan English for academic purposes level universitas. Target: skor 90–100+ (setara C1–C2).

## Analogi
TOEFL iBT seperti lomba triathlon akademik — kamu harus jago di empat cabang (R, L, S, W) sekaligus.

## Struktur & Strategi

### Reading (54–72 menit, 3–4 passages, 30–40 soal)
- **Strategi:** Skim passage dulu (2 menit), baca pertanyaan baru cari detail. Fokus pada topic sentence tiap paragraf.
- **Latihan:** Daily reading artikel Nature, The Economist, National Geographic.

### Listening (41–57 menit, 3–4 lectures, 2–3 conversations)
- **Strategi:** Catat main idea, attitude pembicara, dan transitions (however, therefore, in contrast). Jangan catat terlalu detail.
- **Latihan:** TED Talks, lecture di YouTube tanpa subtitle.

### Speaking (17 menit, 4 tasks — 1 independent, 3 integrated)
- **Strategi:** Template untuk tiap task. Task 1: State opinion + 2 reasons. Task 2–4: Summarize reading + listening.
- **Latihan:** Rekam jawaban, dengarkan, perbaiki pronunciation dan fluency.

### Writing (50 menit, 2 tasks — Integrated + Independent)
- **Strategi:** Integrated: baca → dengar → tulis summary (150–225 words). Independent: 5-paragraph essay (300+ words).
- **Latihan:** Tulis 1 essay per hari, minta feedback di forum seperti TestMagic.

## Contoh
**Independent Writing prompt:** "Do you agree that it is better to work in a team than alone?"
**Struktur jawaban:**
- Introduction: State position (I strongly agree...)
- Body 1: Collaboration sparks creativity (e.g., brainstorming sessions)
- Body 2: Shared workload reduces stress (e.g., group projects)
- Conclusion: Restate + final thought

## Latihan
1. Baca 1 artikel Nature/Seed, buat outline dalam 2 menit, jawab 5 soal detail.
2. Dengar 1 lecture TED, catat 5 poin utama, rangkum lisan dalam 60 detik.
3. Rekam jawaban Task 1 Speaking: "Describe a skill you want to learn" — transkrip dan evaluasi.
4. Tulis Independent Essay 300 kata: "Should university education be free?"
5. Skor jawaban menggunakan rubrik TOEFL official, cari celah improvement.
