# 327. TOEIC (Listening and Reading, Speaking and Writing)

## Definisi & Target
TOEIC mengukur English for business/workplace — standar korporasi global. Target: 800–990 (L&R), 160–200 (S&W).

## Analogi
TOEIC seperti "health check-up" bahasa Inggris untuk dunia kerja — tidak menguji kreativitas, tapi fungsionalitas sehari-hari.

## Struktur & Strategi

### Listening & Reading (2 jam, 200 soal)
- **Listening (45 menit, 100 soal):**
  - Part 1: Photographs (6 soal) — deskripsi gambar.
  - Part 2: Question-Response (25 soal) — pilih respon tepat.
  - Part 3: Conversations (39 soal) — 13 percakapan × 3 soal.
  - Part 4: Talks (30 soal) — 10 monolog × 3 soal.
  - **Strategi:** Part 2 — jangan analisis berlebihan, "first thought = right thought."

- **Reading (75 menit, 100 soal):**
  - Part 5: Incomplete sentences (30 soal) — grammar + vocabulary.
  - Part 6: Text completion (16 soal) — 4 teks × 4 soal.
  - Part 7: Reading comprehension (54 soal) — single + double passages.
  - **Strategi:** Part 7 — baca pertanyaan dulu, lalu scan passage.

### Speaking & Writing (opsional, 1 jam 20 menit)
- **Speaking (20 menit, 11 tasks):** Read aloud → describe photo → respond to questions → propose solution.
- **Writing (1 jam, 8 tasks):** Write sentences → email → opinion essay.
- **Note:** S&W lebih jarang diminta, tapi valuable untuk corporate roles.

## Contoh
**TOEIC Part 2 (Question-Response):**
- Audio: "When is the meeting scheduled?"
- Pilihan: A) At 3 PM ✓ | B) In the conference room | C) Mr. Johnson
- Hanya A yang menjawab "when". B = where, C = who.

## Latihan
1. Listening Part 1 — cari 5 foto online, deskripsikan dalam 1 kalimat tiap foto.
2. Reading Part 5 — kerjakan 20 soal grammar: tenses, prepositions, conjunctions.
3. Reading Part 7 — baca 1 email + 1 memo (double passage), jawab 5 soal.
4. Speaking — rekam "Read Aloud" (cari script di buku TOEIC official).
5. Writing email: "Write to a colleague rescheduling a meeting" (50 kata).
