# 355. Podcast untuk Advanced Learners — This American Life, Radiolab, Freakonomics

## Definisi & Target
Mendengarkan podcast native-level dengan konten kompleks dan bahasa natural. Target: memahami 90%+ tanpa transcript.

## Daftar & Deskripsi

| Podcast | Durasi | Gaya | Keunggulan untuk Advanced Learners |
|---------|--------|------|------------------------------------|
| **This American Life** | 60 menit | Storytelling jurnalistik — personal narratives | Aneka aksen, natural speech, emotional range luas |
| **Radiolab** | 30–60 menit | Science + philosophy — creative production | Vocab ilmiah, abstract concepts, fast-paced editing |
| **Freakonomics Radio** | 30–45 menit | Economics applied to everyday life | Argument structures, data presentation, interview styles |
| **The Economist Podcast** | 20–30 menit | Current affairs + business | Formal English, sophisticated vocabulary, global topics |
| **Revisionist History (Malcolm Gladwell)** | 30–40 menit | Rethinking historical events | Narrative arcs, persuasive language, cultural references |

## Tips Penggunaan

### Mendengarkan Native-Level Podcast
1. **Jangan pause setiap detik** — dengarkan dulu untuk gist, baru ulang untuk detail.
2. **Active listening** — prediksi apa yang akan dikatakan pembicara selanjutnya.
3. **Note cultural references** — This American Life penuh dengan US culture context.
4. **Transcribe 2 menit** — pilih bagian sulit, transkrip manual untuk listening workout.
5. **Discuss with others** — cari subreddit podcast (r/ThisAmericanLife) untuk diskusi.

### Level Progression
- **Start with:** Freakonomics (clear speech, structured arguments)
- **Intermediate:** Radiolab (creative, varied speed)
- **Advanced:** This American Life (emotional, nuanced, multiple narratives)

## Contoh
**This American Life — Episode: "The Giant Pool of Money"**
- **Topik:** 2008 financial crisis explained through personal stories
- **Vocab:** subprime mortgage, default, foreclosure, derivatives, collateralized debt obligation
- **Frasa:** "The whole system was built on a house of cards."

## Latihan
1. Dengar 15 menit 1 episode Freakonomics — tulis main argument + 3 supporting points.
2. Pilih 3 menit dari Radiolab, transkrip manual, bandingkan dengan transcript resmi.
3. Catat 10 new vocabulary dari The Economist Podcast, buat Anki cards.
4. Cari 1 idiom atau frase menarik dari This American Life, gunakan dalam kalimat.
5. Tulis review 200 kata dari 1 episode podcast — bagikan di Letterboxd/Goodreads style.
