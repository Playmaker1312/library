# 356. YouTube Channels untuk Belajar Bahasa Inggris

## Definisi & Target
Mengoptimalkan YouTube sebagai sumber belajar listening, vocabulary, dan cultural immersion. Target: subscribe dan consume secara rutin channel-channel ini.

## Daftar & Deskripsi

| Channel | Fokus | Level | Kenapa Bagus |
|---------|-------|-------|-------------|
| **English with Lucy** | British English, pronunciation, grammar | B1–C2 | Clear explanation, aksen British modern |
| **Interactive English** | Vocabulary, phrasal verbs, listening | B2–C2 | Vocabulary in context, interactive quizzes |
| **Papa Teach Me** | British slang, expressions, humor | B2–C2 | Humor + colloquial English, belajar sambil tertawa |
| **Rachel's English** | American pronunciation, linking, intonation | B1–C1 | Detail banget soal sounds — cocok untuk accent training |
| **English Addict with Mr. Duncan** | Daily life English | B1–B2 | Enthusiastic, speech lambat, cocok intermediate |
| **Vox** | Documenter pendek, topical | B2–C2 | Visual + narrated, great for listening + general knowledge |
| **Kurzgesagt** | Science explained | B2–C2 | Visual stunning, vocabulary akademik, aksen British |
| **TED-Ed** | Educational animation | B2–C2 | Short (5 menit), padat, transcript tersedia |

## Tips Penggunaan

### Teknik Belajar dengan YouTube
1. **Aktifkan English captions** — bukan auto-translate Indonesia.
2. **YouTube play speed** — 0.75x untuk detail, 1x normal, 1.25x untuk challenge.
3. **Use YouTube transcript** — copy paste ke note, highlight vocabulary.
4. **Comment in English** — tulis komentar untuk writing practice.
5. **Create playlist** — "Pronunciation", "Business English", "Listening Challenge".

## Contoh
**Routine dengan YouTube:**
- 5 menit: English with Lucy — 1 grammar tip
- 10 menit: Vox — 1 documentary (with captions)
- 5 menit: Catat 3 new words + 2 frasa ke journal

## Latihan
1. Cari 1 video English with Lucy tentang "10 Advanced Adjectives", catat semua adjective.
2. Watch 1 Vox video (no subtitles), tulis 5 poin yang kamu tangkap.
3. Rachel's English — video "How to pronounce -ed endings", praktikkan 10 contoh.
4. Subscribe ke 3 channel baru dari daftar, tonton 1 video masing-masing.
5. Buat YouTube playlist "My English Learning" dengan 10 video — categorikan.
