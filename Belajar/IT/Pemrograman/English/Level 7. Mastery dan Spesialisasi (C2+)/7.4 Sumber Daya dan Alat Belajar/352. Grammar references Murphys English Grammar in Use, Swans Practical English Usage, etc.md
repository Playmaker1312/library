# 352. Grammar References — Murphy's English Grammar in Use, Swan's Practical English Usage, etc.

## Definisi & Target
Mengenal buku grammar reference terbaik untuk level advanced dan cara menggunakannya. Target: dapat merujuk ke sumber tepat saat ragu dengan grammar.

## Daftar & Deskripsi

| Buku | Level | Keunggulan |
|------|-------|-----------|
| **English Grammar in Use (Raymond Murphy)** | Intermediate (B1–B2) | 145 units, 1 unit = 1 grammar point, exercises included |
| **Advanced Grammar in Use (Martin Hewings)** | Advanced (C1–C2) | Level lebih tinggi dari Murphy, fokus pada nuanced grammar |
| **Practical English Usage (Michael Swan)** | All levels (B2–C2) | BUKAN textbook — encyclopedia grammar. Cari berdasarkan masalah, bukan unit |
| **Oxford English Grammar (Sidney Greenbaum)** | Advanced | Comprehensive academic reference |
| **The Cambridge Grammar of the English Language (Huddleston & Pullum)** | Very advanced | The "bible" of English grammar — 1800 halaman |

## Tips Penggunaan

### Cara Menggunakan Buku Grammar Reference
1. **Troubleshoot, not study cover-to-cover** — cari topik spesifik saat error.
2. **Gunakan index** — Practical English Usage diorganisir alphabetical (easy to search).
3. **Do exercises selectively** — fokus pada area lemah, jangan kerjakan semua unit.
4. **Pair with corpus** — cek contoh real-world di COCA setelah baca aturan.
5. **Digital version** — beli e-book atau cari di PDF untuk searchability.

### Topik C1–C2 yang Sering Dibutuhkan
- Inversion (never have I seen, not only... but also)
- Cleft sentences (it was John who...)
- Hedging (tends to, may suggest, arguably)
- Mixed conditionals (if I had studied, I would be...)
- Passive reporting (it is believed that...)

## Contoh
**Practical English Usage — Entry 579: "the" (articles)**
Masalah: kapan pakai "the" dengan proper nouns?
Jawaban: "the" dipakai dengan rivers (the Amazon), mountain ranges (the Alps), plural countries (the Netherlands), regions (the Middle East). Tidak dipakai dengan most single mountains (Mount Everest), most countries (Indonesia).
