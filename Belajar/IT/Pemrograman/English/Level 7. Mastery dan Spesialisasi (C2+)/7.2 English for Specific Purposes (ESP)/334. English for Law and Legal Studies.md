# 334. English for Law and Legal Studies

## Definisi & Target
Mempelajari legal terminology, contract language, dan argumentasi hukum dalam Bahasa Inggris. Target: mampu membaca case law, menulis legal memo, dan presentasi argumentasi.

## Analogi
Legal English seperti "kode program" — setiap kata punya bobot hukum, satu kata salah bisa ubah makna kontrak.

## Daftar Istilah & Frasa

| Istilah | Arti | Contoh Kalimat |
|---------|------|----------------|
| Plaintiff | Penggugat | "The plaintiff filed a lawsuit against the company." |
| Defendant | Tergugat | "The defendant pleaded not guilty." |
| Tort | Perbuatan melawan hukum (perdata) | "The case involves a tort of negligence." |
| Breach of contract | Wanprestasi | "The supplier was sued for breach of contract." |
| Injunction | Putusan pengadilan untuk menghentikan tindakan | "The court issued an injunction to stop construction." |
| Precedent | Yurisprudensi | "The judge based the ruling on a precedent from 1998." |
| Affidavit | Pernyataan tertulis di bawah sumpah | "The witness submitted an affidavit." |
| Due diligence | Pemeriksaan hukum | "The lawyer conducted due diligence before the merger." |

**Frasa Kunci:**
- "Without prejudice" — tanpa merugikan hak hukum salah satu pihak
- "Subject to contract" — tergantung pada kontrak final
- "Hereinafter referred to as..." — selanjutnya disebut sebagai...

## Contoh
**Contract Clause:**
"The Lessee hereby agrees to pay the Lessor a monthly rent of $1,000, payable on the first day of each month. In the event of late payment, a penalty of 5% shall apply."
Artinya: Penyewa setuju bayar sewa $1000/bulan, denda 5% jika terlambat.

## Latihan
1. Baca 1 case brief dari Supreme Court AS (Oyez.org), identifikasi: issue, rule, application, conclusion.
2. Tulis 1 legal memo 300 kata: "Whether the non-compete clause is enforceable."
3. Terjemahkan 5 klausa kontrak dari Bahasa Indonesia ke English.
4. Debat role-play: Prosecution vs Defense dalam kasus theft.
5. Pelajari 10 Latin terms used in law: habeas corpus, prima facie, bona fide, etc.
