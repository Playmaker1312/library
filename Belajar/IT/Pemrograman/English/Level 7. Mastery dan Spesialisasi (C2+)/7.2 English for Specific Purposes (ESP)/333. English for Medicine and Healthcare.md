# 333. English for Medicine and Healthcare

## Definisi & Target
Mempelajari vocabulary, frasa, dan komunikasi klinis dalam Bahasa Inggris untuk profesi medis. Target: mampu melakukan consultation, documentation, dan case presentation dalam bahasa Inggris.

## Analogi
Seperti belajar Bahasa "Latin" untuk dokter — beda dengan English sehari-hari, ada istilah khusus yang wajib dikuasai.

## Daftar Istilah & Frasa

| Istilah | Arti | Contoh Kalimat |
|---------|------|----------------|
| Symptom | Gejala (subjektif, pasien rasakan) | "The patient reports symptoms of fatigue and dizziness." |
| Sign | Tanda (objektif, teramati) | "Vital signs show elevated blood pressure." |
| Diagnosis | Diagnosis | "The diagnosis was confirmed through blood tests." |
| Prognosis | Proyeksi penyakit | "The prognosis is favorable with early intervention." |
| Contraindication | Kontraindikasi | "This drug is contraindicated in patients with liver disease." |
| Adverse reaction | Efek samping | "The patient experienced an adverse reaction to the antibiotic." |

**Frasa Kunci:**
- "On examination..." (Saat diperiksa...)
- "The patient presents with..." (Pasien datang dengan...)
- "Differential diagnosis includes..." (Diagnosis banding meliputi...)

## Contoh
**Doctor-Patient Consultation:**
Doctor: "Can you describe the pain? Is it sharp or dull?"
Patient: "It's a dull ache that started about three days ago."
Doctor: "On a scale of one to ten, how would you rate it?"
Artinya: Dokter menanyakan karakteristik nyeri — penting untuk diagnosis banding.

## Latihan
1. Tulis referral letter untuk pasien dengan appendicitis (150 kata).
2. Role-play: Doctor explains diabetes management to newly diagnosed patient.
3. Baca 1 abstract jurnal PubMed, catat 10 medical terms baru.
4. Dengar 1 episode podcast "The Lancet Voice", transkrip 1 bagian konsultasi.
5. Tulis SOAP note (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) untuk 1 kasus fiktif.
