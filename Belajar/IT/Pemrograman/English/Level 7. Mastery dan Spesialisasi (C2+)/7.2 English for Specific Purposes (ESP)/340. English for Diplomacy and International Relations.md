# 340. English for Diplomacy and International Relations

## Definisi & Target
Mempelajari diplomatic language, negotiation, dan komunikasi internasional. Target: mampu menyusun diplomatic note, joint statement, dan berpartisipasi dalam multilateral meeting.

## Analogi
Diplomatic English seperti "jalan di atas telur" — setiap kata dipilih hati-hati, nadanya netral, dan selalu ada "face-saving" mechanism.

## Daftar Istilah & Frasa

| Istilah | Arti | Contoh |
|---------|------|--------|
| Note verbale | Nota diplomatik resmi | "The embassy sent a note verbale regarding the trade agreement." |
| Joint statement | Pernyataan bersama | "The two leaders issued a joint statement on climate cooperation." |
| Ratification | Ratifikasi — pengesahan perjanjian | "The treaty requires ratification by both parliaments." |
| Sanctions | Sanksi (ekonomi/diplomatik) | "The UN imposed sanctions against the regime." |
| Extradition | Ekstradisi — penyerahan tersangka antarnegara | "The government approved the extradition request." |
| Diplomatic immunity | Kekebalan diplomatik | "The ambassador cannot be prosecuted due to diplomatic immunity." |
| Démarche | Komunikasi diplomatik formal (biasanya protes) | "The embassy delivered a démarche protesting the detention." |
| Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) | Nota kesepahaman | "Both countries signed an MoU on cultural exchange." |
| Good offices | Mediasi diplomatik | "The UN Secretary-General offered his good offices." |

**Frasa Diplomatis (diplo-speak):**
- "We view with concern..." (Kami memandang dengan prihatin...)
- "We express grave concern over..." (Kami menyatakan keprihatinan mendalam atas...)
- "We urge all parties to exercise restraint" (Kami mendorong semua pihak untuk menahan diri)
- "We take note of the statement" (Kami mencatat pernyataan tersebut — sering berarti "kami tidak setuju tapi tidak mau konfrontasi")

## Contoh
**Joint Statement Excerpt:**
"The two parties reaffirmed their commitment to the peaceful resolution of the dispute through diplomatic channels and agreed to convene further negotiations within 30 days."

4. Parafrase pernyataan kontroversial menjadi diplomatic language.
