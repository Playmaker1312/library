# 337. English for Aviation

## Definisi & Target
Mempelajari phraseology dan komunikasi penerbangan dalam Bahasa Inggris — standar ICAO. Target: mampu berkomunikasi pilot-ATC (Air Traffic Control) dengan jelas dan aman.

## Analogi
Aviation English seperti "kode morse modern" — setiap kata punya aturan baku, improvisasi bisa berbahaya.

## Daftar Istilah & Frasa

### Standard ICAO Phraseology

| Frasa | Arti | Konteks |
|-------|------|---------|
| Roger | Message received | Bukan berarti "yes", hanya konfirmasi terima pesan |
| Wilco | "Will comply" — akan dilaksanakan | "Climb to 10,000 feet." — "Wilco" |
| Affirm / Negative | Ya / Tidak | "Are you ready for departure?" — "Affirm" |
| Mayday | Emergency — bahaya mengancam jiwa | "Mayday, mayday, mayday — engine failure" |
| Pan-Pan | Urgent — butuh bantuan tapi tidak immediate danger | "Pan-pan, medical emergency on board" |
| Squawk | Set transponder code | "Squawk 7700" (emergency code) |
| Hold short | Berhenti sebelum runway | "Hold short of runway 27" |
| Line up and wait | Masuk runway dan tunggu | "Line up and wait, runway 27" |
| Cleared for takeoff | Izin lepas landas | "Cleared for takeoff, runway 27" |
| Go around | Batalkan pendaratan | "Go around, traffic on the runway" |

**Numerik dalam Aviation:**
3 = "Tree", 4 = "Fower", 5 = "Fife", 9 = "Niner" — untuk menghindari miskomunikasi.

## Contoh
**ATC-Pilot Communication:**
ATC: "Garuda 742, descend to flight level 180, expect approach runway 25 Right."
Pilot: "Descend to flight level 180, approach runway 25 Right, Garuda 742."
**Readback penting** — pilot harus mengulang instruksi untuk konfirmasi.

## Latihan
1. Dengarkan LiveATC.net (bandara besar), transkrip 2 menit percakapan.
2. Role-play: Pilot request start-up, taxi, takeoff clearance.
3. Tulis flight plan sederhana: destination, altitude, route, fuel.
4. Pelajari 10 emergency phrases: mayday, pan-pan, emergency descent, etc.
5. Baca 1 laporan incident (aviation-safety.net), identifikasi miskomunikasi.
