# 338. English for Hospitality and Tourism

## Definisi & Target
Mempelajari vocabulary dan komunikasi layanan di industri hospitality — hotel, restoran, travel, dan customer service. Target: mampu menangani tamu internasional dengan profesional.

## Analogi
English for Hospitality seperti "menu restoran" — harus lengkap, jelas, dan ramah karena langsung dinilai oleh tamu.

## Daftar Istilah & Frasa

| Istilah/Frasa | Arti | Contoh |
|---------------|------|--------|
| Check-in / Check-out | Proses masuk/keluar hotel | "Check-in is at 2 PM, check-out at 12 PM." |
| Amenities | Fasilitas tambahan | "The room includes complimentary amenities: coffee, toiletries, and Wi-Fi." |
| Concierge | Petugas bantuan tamu | "The concierge can arrange tours and restaurant reservations." |
| Housekeeping | Kebersihan kamar | "Housekeeping will clean your room daily." |
| Room service | Layanan kamar | "Room service is available 24 hours." |
| Itinerary | Jadwal perjalanan | "Here is your tour itinerary for tomorrow." |
| All-inclusive | Semua termasuk | "The resort offers all-inclusive packages with meals and activities." |
| Duty-free | Bebas bea | "You can shop at duty-free stores at the airport." |
| Overbooked | Kelebihan reservasi | "Unfortunately, the flight is overbooked." |
| Complimentary | Gratis (sebagai fasilitas) | "Complimentary breakfast is served from 6 to 10 AM." |

**Frasa Layanan:**
- "How may I assist you today?" — Ada yang bisa dibantu?
- "I apologize for the inconvenience" — Mohon maaf atas ketidaknyamanan
- "Let me check that for you" — Biar saya cek

## Contoh
**Hotel Front Desk Conversation:**
Guest: "I'd like to check in, please. I have a reservation under the name Smith."
Receptionist: "Welcome, Mr. Smith. Let me pull up your reservation. You're in room 408, a deluxe king with an ocean view. May I have a credit card for incidentals?"

## Latihan
1. Role-play: Check-in hotel — guest meminta early check-in, upgraded room.
2. Tulis 1 email konfirmasi booking hotel (50 kata).
3. Role-play: Restaurant server menangani complaint — makanan terlalu asin.
4. Baca 1 review hotel di TripAdvisor, identifikasi 5 frasa hospitality.
5. Buat tour itinerary 3 hari untuk turis asing di kotamu.
