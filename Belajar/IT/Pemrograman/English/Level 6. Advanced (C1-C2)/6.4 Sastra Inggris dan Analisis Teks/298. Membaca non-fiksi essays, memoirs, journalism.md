# 298. Membaca Non-Fiksi: Essays, Memoirs, Journalism

## Definisi & Target
Mengembangkan kemampuan membaca non-fiksi bahasa Inggris — esai, memoar, dan jurnalisme berkualitas. Target: mampu menganalisis argumen, gaya, dan struktur teks non-fiksi.

## Analogi
Seperti detektif yang memeriksa bukti — non-fiksi mengklaim kebenaran, dan tugas kita sebagai pembaca adalah mengevaluasi klaim tersebut secara kritis.

## Poin-Poin Penting
1. **Personal essay**: Montaigne, Orwell (*"Shooting an Elephant"*), Didion — reflective, subjective, narrative
2. **Memoir**: *Angela's Ashes* (McCourt), *The Glass Castle* (Walls), *Educated* (Westover) — personal history dengan literary craft
3. **Journalism**: feature article vs news report — narrative journalism (Tom Wolfe, Joan Didion)
4. **Longform journalism**: The New Yorker, The Atlantic — deep reporting, narrative arc
5. **Opinion/op-ed**: argumentative, persuasive — *"Why I Changed My Mind About..."*
6. **Literary nonfiction / creative nonfiction**: using literary techniques (scene, dialogue, imagery) for factual content

## Analisis
- **Orwell, "Politics and the English Language"**: argument that vague language enables political manipulation — contoh: *"elimination of unreliable elements"* vs *"killing people"*
- **Didion, "The White Album"**: fragmented form mencerminkan fragmented consciousness of 1960s — *"We tell ourselves stories in order to live."*
- **Feature journalism**: narrative lead, nut graf, alternating scenes and exposition

## Latihan
1. Baca satu esai Orwell dan analisis argumen utamanya.
2. Bedakan news article vs feature story tentang topik yang sama.
3. Analisis struktur personal essay: hook, narrative arc, reflection.
4. Bandingkan memoar dengan autobiography — apa bedanya?
5. Tulis satu halaman creative nonfiction tentang pengalaman pribadi.
