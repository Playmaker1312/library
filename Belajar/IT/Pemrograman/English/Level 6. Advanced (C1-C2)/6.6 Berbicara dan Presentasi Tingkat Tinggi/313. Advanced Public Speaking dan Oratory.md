# 313. Advanced Public Speaking dan Oratory

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai seni berbicara di depan umum tingkat lanjut — dari persiapan hingga delivery. Target: mampu menyampaikan pidato yang meyakinkan, terstruktur, dan berkesan dalam berbagai konteks.

## Analogi
Seperti konduktor orkestra — public speaker bukan sekadar menyampaikan kata-kata, tapi mengarahkan emosi, ritme, dan energi audiens.

## Poin-Poin Penting
1. **Speech structure**: 
   - Opening: hook (story, quote, question, statistic)
   - Body: 3 main points with transitions
   - Closing: circle back, call to action, memorable ending
2. **Rhetorical devices**: 
   - *Rule of three*: *"Education, opportunity, empowerment"*
   - *Anaphora*: repetition at beginning — *"We shall fight..."* (Churchill)
   - *Antithesis*: contrast — *"Ask not what your country can do for you..."*
3. **Delivery**: 
   - Eye contact: 3-5 seconds per person
   - Gestures: natural, purposeful, not distracting
   - Stance: balanced, open, grounded
4. **Voice**: volume, pace, pitch, pause — variasi untuk emphasis

## Langkah-Langkah
1. **Analyze audience**: siapa mereka? apa yang mereka butuhkan?
2. **Craft message**: satu inti pesan, 3 supporting points
3. **Structure speech**: opening → body → closing
4. **Write for speaking**: short sentences, repetition, oral style
5. **Rehearse**: out loud, record, time yourself, adjust

## Contoh
- **Opening hook**: *"In 1962, a young girl in Indonesia received a book that would change her life. That girl was my mother. And today, I want to talk about the power of literacy."*
- **Rule of three**: *"We need to invest in three things: education, infrastructure, and healthcare."*
- **Closing**: *"So next time you see a book, remember: it's not just paper and ink. It's a door."*

## Latihan
1. Tulis opening hook untuk pidato 3 menit tentang topik pilihan.
2. Rekam diri bicara 2 menit — analisis eye contact, gesture, voice.
3. Gunakan rule of three untuk membuat 3 argumen utama.
4. Latih anaphora: tulis 3 kalimat yang dimulai dengan kata yang sama.
5. Presentasi 5 menit di depan cermin — perhatikan bahasa tubuh.
