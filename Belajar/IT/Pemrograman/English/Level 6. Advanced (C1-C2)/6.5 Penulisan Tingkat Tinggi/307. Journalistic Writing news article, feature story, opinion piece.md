# 307. Journalistic Writing: News Article, Feature Story, Opinion Piece

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai tiga genre utama jurnalisme: berita langsung (hard news), feature story, dan opini. Target: mampu menulis artikel jurnalistik yang informatif, engaging, dan etis.

## Analogi
Seperti tiga alat berbeda di kotak jurnalis — palu (hard news) untuk fakta cepat, gergaji (feature) untuk ukiran mendalam, dan kuas (opini) untuk mewarnai perspektif.

## Poin-Poin Penting
1. **News article**:
   - Inverted pyramid: most important → least important
   - 5W+1H: Who, What, When, Where, Why, How
   - Lead: summary lead (direct) atau delayed lead (creative)
   - Objective tone: facts, quotes, attribution
2. **Feature story**:
   - Narrative structure: scene-based, character-driven
   - Nut graf: paragraph that states the story's point
   - Literary techniques: dialogue, description, pacing
   - Human interest: emotional connection
3. **Opinion piece / Op-Ed**:
   - Clear thesis: *"This is what I believe and why"*
   - Argument + evidence
   - Strong voice and perspective
   - Call to action or recommendation

## Struktur Penulisan
- **News lead**: *"A 5.2 magnitude earthquake struck West Java yesterday afternoon, killing at least 12 people and injuring hundreds, officials said."*
- **Feature opening**: *"The morning sun filters through the gaps in the bamboo walls. Inside, 12 children sit cross-legged on the dirt floor, waiting for their teacher."*
- **Op-Ed opening**: *"Indonesia's education system is failing its most vulnerable students — and we know exactly why."*

## Latihan
1. Tulis news lead untuk kejadian fiktif (5W+1H).
2. Ubah berita hard news menjadi feature story opening.
3. Tulis op-ed 300 kata tentang isu lokal yang kamu pedulikan.
4. Bedakan sumber langsung vs tidak langsung dalam kutipan berita.
5. Analisis etika jurnalistik: kapan identitas narasumber dilindungi?
