# 304. Creative Writing: Short Story

## Definisi & Target
Mengembangkan kemampuan menulis cerita pendek bahasa Inggris — dari ide hingga draft final. Target: mampu menulis short story yang utuh dengan struktur naratif yang kuat.

## Analogi
Seperti membuat film pendek — cerita pendek harus efisien, setiap kata berkontribusi pada efek keseluruhan. Tidak ada ruang untuk adegan yang tidak perlu.

## Poin-Poin Penting
1. **Elements of short story**:
   - *Plot*: exposition → rising action → climax → falling action → resolution
   - *Character*: protagonist, antagonist, round/flat, dynamic/static
   - *Setting*: time, place, atmosphere
   - *Point of view*: first person, third person limited, third person omniscient
   - *Theme*: central idea, what the story means
   - *Conflict*: internal vs external, man vs man/self/society/nature
2. **Show, don't tell**: *"He clenched his fists"* (show) vs *"He was angry"* (tell)
3. **Dialogue**: natural, reveals character, advances plot
4. **In medias res**: mulai di tengah aksi

## Struktur Penulisan
- **Opening**: hook pembaca dalam 3 kalimat pertama
- **Middle**: rising tension, complications
- **Ending**: surprising but inevitable — twist, epiphany, or resolution
- **Revision**: read aloud, cut unnecessary words, check pacing

## Contoh
- **Opening hook**: *"The day I decided to kill my husband was the same day I baked him a lemon pie."* — langsung menarik perhatian
- **Showing vs telling**: *"Her hands trembled as she poured the tea. A single drop landed on the white tablecloth, spreading like a small, dark flower."* (show) vs *"She was nervous."* (tell)

## Latihan
1. Tulis opening paragraph (5 kalimat) yang langsung hook pembaca.
2. Ubah "telling" menjadi "showing": *He was scared. She was rich. They were in love.*
3. Tulis dialog 2 karakter yang mengungkapkan konflik tanpa menyebutkannya.
4. Buat outline plot untuk cerita 1000 kata.
5. Tulis cerita pendek 500 kata berdasarkan prompt: *"The letter arrived on a Tuesday."*
