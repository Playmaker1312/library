# 302. Advanced Academic Writing: Research Paper

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai penulisan research paper akademik tingkat lanjut — dari pemilihan topik hingga publikasi. Target: mampu menulis research paper yang memenuhi standar jurnal akademik internasional.

## Analogi
Seperti membangun rumah — research question adalah fondasi, literature review adalah kerangka, methodology adalah alat, dan hasil adalah ruangan yang diisi.

## Poin-Poin Penting
1. **Structure IMRAD**: Introduction, Methods, Results, and Discussion
2. **Introduction**: 
   - Hook → background → gap → research question → significance
   - *"Despite extensive research on X, little is known about Y..."*
3. **Literature review**: sintesis, bukan summary — *"While Smith (2020) argues..., Jones (2021) challenges..."*
4. **Methodology**: reproducible, justified — *"A mixed-methods approach was employed..."*
5. **Results**: present findings objectively — tables, figures, statistics
6. **Discussion**: interpret results, connect to literature, limitations, future research
7. **Abstract**: 150-250 words — summary of entire paper

## Struktur Penulisan
- **Title**: clear, concise, includes key variables
- **Keywords**: 4-6 terms for indexing
- **Citations**: APA/MLA/Chicago — konsisten
- **Tone**: formal, impersonal, precise — *"It was found that..."* bukan *"I think that..."*

## Contoh
- Research question: *"To what extent does code-switching in bilingual classrooms affect L2 acquisition?"*
- Thesis statement: *"This study investigates the correlation between code-switching frequency and vocabulary retention among Indonesian EFL learners."*

## Latihan
1. Tulis research question untuk topik yang kamu minati.
2. Buat outline IMRAD untuk penelitian hipotetis.
3. Tulis satu paragraf literature review yang mensintesis 3 sumber.
4. Bedakan abstract vs introduction — tulis keduanya untuk topik yang sama.
5. Evaluasi struktur research paper dari jurnal bidangmu.
