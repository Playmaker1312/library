# 303. Advanced Academic Writing: Dissertation/Thesis Components

## Definisi & Target
Memahami komponen dan proses penulisan disertasi atau tesis — proyek akademik terbesar dalam pendidikan tinggi. Target: mampu merencanakan dan menulis tesis/disertasi yang sistematis.

## Analogi
Seperti maraton, bukan sprint — disertasi adalah proyek jangka panjang yang membutuhkan perencanaan, konsistensi, dan ketahanan mental.

## Poin-Poin Penting
1. **Thesis proposal**: 
   - Problem statement, research questions, theoretical framework, methodology
   - Timeline and feasibility
2. **Chapters**: 
   - Chapter 1: Introduction (background, problem, questions, significance)
   - Chapter 2: Literature Review (theoretical framework, related studies)
   - Chapter 3: Methodology (design, participants, instruments, analysis)
   - Chapter 4: Findings (data presentation, no interpretation yet)
   - Chapter 5: Discussion (interpretation, connection to literature)
   - Chapter 6: Conclusion (summary, implications, limitations, recommendations)
3. **Defense**: proposal defense, final defense — present, justify, respond
4. **Writing process**: 
   - *Shitty first draft* (Anne Lamott) — write first, perfect later
   - *Reverse outline* — check logical flow after writing

## Struktur Penulisan
- **Abstract**: 300-500 words
- **Table of contents**: automatic generation
- **List of figures/tables**: separate lists
- **Acknowledgments**: formal but personal
- **References/Bibliography**: complete, consistent style
- **Appendices**: instruments, raw data, transcripts

## Contoh
- **Thesis statement**: *"This dissertation argues that Indonesian EFL learners' pragmatic competence can be enhanced through explicit instruction of speech acts, particularly when combined with authentic materials and metapragmatic awareness tasks."*
- **Research gap**: *"While previous studies have examined pragmatic instruction in ESL contexts, limited research exists on its application in Indonesian EFL tertiary settings."*

## Latihan
1. Tulis problem statement untuk topik penelitianmu.
2. Buat outline 6 chapters untuk disertasi hipotetis.
3. Bedakan findings vs discussion — apa isi masing-masing?
4. Tulis satu paragraf theoretical framework.
5. Buat timeline realistis 12 bulan untuk penulisan tesis.
