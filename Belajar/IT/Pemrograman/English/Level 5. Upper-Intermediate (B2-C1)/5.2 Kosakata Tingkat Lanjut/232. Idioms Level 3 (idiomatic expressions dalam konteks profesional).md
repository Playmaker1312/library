# 232. Idioms Level 3 (Idiomatic Expressions dalam Konteks Profesional)

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai idioms tingkat lanjut yang sering digunakan dalam lingkungan profesional dan bisnis. Target: menggunakan idiom secara alami dalam meeting, presentasi, dan negosiasi.

## Analogi
Seperti bumbu rahasia dalam masakan — sedikit saja bisa mengubah keseluruhan rasa percakapan.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Idiom | Makna | Contoh |
|-------|-------|--------|
| To play hardball | Bersikap tegas/agresif | "In negotiations, you need to play hardball." |
| To go the extra mile | Berusaha lebih | "She always goes the extra mile for clients." |
| To see eye to eye | Setuju | "We don't always see eye to eye on strategy." |
| To be on the same page | Sepaham | "Let's make sure we're on the same page." |
| To cut corners | Mengambil jalan pintas | "Never cut corners on quality." |
| To get down to business | Mulai serius | "Let's get down to business." |

## Contoh
1. "We need to **think outside the box** to solve this supply chain issue."
2. "The CEO decided to **play hardball** with the union representatives."
3. "Our team **went the extra mile** to meet the deadline."

## Latihan
1. Gunakan "on the same page" dalam konteks meeting.
2. Buat dialog singkat menggunakan "get down to business".
3. Bedakan "cut corners" vs "save time".
4. Pilih idiom yang tepat: "To succeed, you must ___ (think outside the box/go through the motions)."
5. Terjemahkan "Kami harus sepaham dulu" ke idiom Inggris.
