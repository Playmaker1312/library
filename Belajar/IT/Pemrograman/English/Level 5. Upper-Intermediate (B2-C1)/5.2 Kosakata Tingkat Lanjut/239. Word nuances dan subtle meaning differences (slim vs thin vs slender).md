# 239. Word Nuances dan Subtle Meaning Differences (Slim vs Thin vs Slender)

## Definisi & Target
Memahami perbedaan makna halus antara sinonim yang tampaknya sama. Target: memilih kata yang tepat dengan konotasi yang sesuai.

## Analogi
Seperti memilih warna cat — hitam dan hitam legam sama-sama hitam tapi memberi kesan berbeda. Kata-kata punya "warna" emosi sendiri.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Kata | Nuansa | Contoh |
|------|--------|--------|
| Slim | Proporsional, positif | "She has a slim figure." |
| Thin | Netral/kurus, bisa negatif | "He looks thin after the illness." |
| Slender | Anggun, elegan | "The slender neck of the vase." |
| Skinny | Sangat kurus, informal | "You're too skinny — eat more!" |
| Lean | Berotot, sehat | "Lean muscle mass." |

Pasangan lain:
- **Cheap vs Inexpensive**: Cheap bisa berarti berkualitas rendah; inexpensive = harga terjangkau.
- **Famous vs Notorious**: Famous = terkenal (positif); Notorious = terkenal (negatif).
- **Persuade vs Convince**: Persuade = bujuk untuk bertindak; Convince = buat percaya.

## Contoh
1. "She bought an **inexpensive** dress, not a cheap one." (beda konotasi)
2. "He is **notorious** for his aggressive negotiation tactics." (negatif)
3. "I **convinced** him of the truth, but I couldn't **persuade** him to act."

## Latihan
1. Bedakan slim vs skinny dalam konteks kesehatan.
2. Pilih kata tepat: "The restaurant is ___ (famous/notorious) for its excellent service."
3. Buat kalimat dengan "persuade" dan "convince" yang benar.
4. Bedakan konotasi "thrifty" vs "stingy".
5. Isi: "She has a ___ (slim/thin) chance of winning." — mana yang lebih natural?
