# 253. Business Correspondence (Emails, Memos, Proposals)

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai korespondensi bisnis formal dan semi-formal. Target: mampu menulis email, memo, dan proposal bisnis yang profesional dan efektif.

## Analogi
Seperti seragam kantor — korespondensi bisnis adalah "pakaian" komunikasi Anda. Pilih yang tepat untuk setiap kesempatan.

## Poin-Poin Penting
- Email: subject jelas, opening, body, closing, signature
- Memo: internal, langsung ke inti, gunakan heading
- Proposal: problem → solution → benefit → budget
- Tone menyesuaikan: formal (client) vs semi-formal (coworker)
- Gunakan bullet points untuk memudahkan baca

## Struktur & Strategi
**Email formal:**
Subject: [Context] - [Purpose]
Dear [Title] [Name],
[Opening], [Body], [Call to action]
Best regards,
[Name]

**Proposal:**
1. Executive Summary
2. Problem Statement
3. Proposed Solution
4. Timeline
5. Budget
6. Expected Outcomes

## Contoh
"**Subject:** Q3 Marketing Strategy Proposal
Dear Ms. Johnson,
I am writing to propose a revised marketing strategy for Q3. **As discussed** in our meeting, the current approach has shown diminishing returns. **I have attached** a detailed proposal outlining three alternative approaches. **I would appreciate** your feedback by Friday.
Best regards,
Andi Pratama"

## Latihan
1. Tulis email formal meminta meeting dengan klien.
2. Buat memo internal tentang perubahan kebijakan WFH.
3. Tulis executive summary proposal 3 kalimat.
4. Bedakan tone email ke atasan vs rekan kerja.
5. Revisi email informal menjadi formal.
