# 258. Business Vocabulary (Marketing, HR, Supply Chain, etc)

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai kosakata khusus di berbagai departemen bisnis. Target: mampu berkomunikasi lintas fungsi dalam organisasi multinasional.

## Analogi
Seperti belajar bahasa daerah — setiap departemen punya "dialek" sendiri. Marketing bicara conversion, HR bicara retention, supply chain bicara logistics.

## Daftar & Penjelasan

| Department | Vocabulary | Definisi |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Marketing | ROI, Conversion rate, Bounce rate, KPI, A/B testing, Funnel | Metrik pemasaran |
| HR | Onboarding, Attrition, Performance review, Talent acquisition | SDM |
| Supply Chain | Logistics, Inventory, Procurement, Lead time, Just-in-time | Rantai pasok |
| Finance | Revenue, P&L, Cash flow, EBITDA, Amortization | Keuangan |
| Sales | Pipeline, Closing, Upselling, Cold calling, Prospect | Penjualan |

## Contoh
1. "Our **conversion rate** increased by 15% after the A/B test." (Marketing)
2. "The **attrition rate** has dropped since we improved the **onboarding** process." (HR)
3. "We need to reduce **lead time** in our **supply chain**." (Operations)

## Latihan
1. Buat kalimat dengan "ROI" dalam konteks marketing campaign.
2. Jelaskan "talent acquisition" dan "onboarding" dalam HR.
3. Gunakan "procurement" dalam kalimat supply chain.
4. Bedakan "revenue" dan "profit".
5. Buat 3 kalimat menggunakan vocabulary dari departemen berbeda.
