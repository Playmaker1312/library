# 230. Distancing Language dan Hedging (Grammar Perspective)

## Definisi & Target
Menggunakan distancing language dan hedging untuk menyatakan opini dengan hati-hati, menghindari klaim absolut. Target: berkomunikasi secara diplomatis dan akademik.

## Analogi
Seperti memasang rem sebelum bicara — memberi ruang untuk kemungkinan salah. Hedging adalah "rem" dalam bahasa.

## Pola & Struktur

| Teknik | Contoh |
|--------|--------|
| Modal verbs | might, may, could, would |
| Adverbs | possibly, arguably, seemingly, apparently |
| Verbs | suggests, indicates, appears, tends to |
| Qualifiers | somewhat, rather, to some extent, in a sense |
| Impersonal structures | It could be argued that... |

## Contoh
1. "It could be argued that the policy has been somewhat ineffective."
2. "The data appears to suggest a correlation, though further research is needed."
3. "This is arguably the most significant finding of the study."

## Latihan
1. Lunakkan "This theory is wrong" dengan hedging.
2. Tambahkan distancing language ke: "The experiment proves the hypothesis."
3. Buat kalimat dengan "It could be argued that..."
4. Bedakan "suggests" vs "proves" dalam konteks akademik.
5. Tulis opini tentang politik dengan 3 hedging devices berbeda.
