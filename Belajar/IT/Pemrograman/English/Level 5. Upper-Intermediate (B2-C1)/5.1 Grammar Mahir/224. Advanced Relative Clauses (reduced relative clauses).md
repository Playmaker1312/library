# 224. Advanced Relative Clauses (Reduced Relative Clauses)

## Definisi & Target
Memahami relative clauses tingkat lanjut termasuk reduced relative clauses (menghilangkan relative pronoun dan auxiliary). Target: membuat kalimat lebih ringkas dan variatif.

## Analogi
Seperti memotong kata-kata yang tidak perlu dalam SMS — makna tetap sama, lebih efisien.

## Pola & Struktur

| Full Form | Reduced Form |
|-----------|-------------|
| The man who is sitting there | The man sitting there |
| The book which was written by... | The book written by... |
| The students who are selected | The students selected |
| The car that is parked outside | The car parked outside |

Aturan reduksi:
- Active: hapus who/which + be, V → V-ing
- Passive: hapus who/which + be, V → V3

## Contoh
1. "The students selected for the program will receive scholarships." (Reduced passive)
2. "Anyone wishing to apply must submit by Friday." (Reduced active)
3. "The results obtained from the experiment were conclusive." (Reduced passive)

## Latihan
1. Reduksi: "The woman who is standing by the door is my professor."
2. Reduksi: "The data which was collected last month is missing."
3. Buat reduced clause dari "The experiment that was conducted in 2020 failed."
4. Identifikasi apakah reduced clause bisa digunakan di sini: "The man who came yesterday."
5. Ubah "The papers that are being reviewed are confidential" menjadi reduced.
