# 229. Advanced Passive (Impersonal Passive, Passive Reporting Structures)

## Definisi & Target
Menguasai bentuk pasif tingkat lanjut termasuk impersonal passive (It is said that...) dan passive reporting structures (is believed to be). Target: menulis secara objektif dan formal tanpa menyebutkan pelaku.

## Analogi
Seperti berita TV — fokus pada peristiwa, bukan siapa yang melaporkan.

## Pola & Struktur

| Struktur | Rumus | Contoh |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Impersonal passive | It + passive + that-clause | "It is believed that the virus mutates rapidly." |
| Personal passive | Subject + passive + to-infinitive | "The virus is believed to mutate rapidly." |
| Passive reporting | Subject + passive + have + V3 | "The team is said to have discovered a cure." |
| Causative passive | Have/get + object + V3 | "She had her paper published." |

## Contoh
1. "It is widely accepted that climate change is anthropogenic." (Impersonal)
2. "The CEO is reported to have resigned." (Personal passive)
3. "The manuscript is said to have been completed last year." (Perfect infinitive passive)

## Latihan
1. Ubah "People say that the company is failing" menjadi impersonal passive.
2. Ubah "They believe the project was successful" menjadi personal passive.
3. Buat causative passive dari "A professional edited my paper."
4. Bedakan "It is thought that..." vs "...is thought to be..."
5. Tulis kalimat passive reporting dengan "allege" tentang skandal.
