# 241. Academic Writing: Thesis Statement Development

## Definisi & Target
Mengembangkan thesis statement yang kuat, spesifik, dan argumentatif sebagai inti dari esai akademik. Target: menulis thesis statement yang jelas, debatable, dan terfokus.

## Analogi
Seperti kompas — thesis statement memberi arah pada seluruh tulisan. Tanpa kompas, pembaca (dan penulis) tersesat.

## Poin-Poin Penting
- Thesis statement adalah satu kalimat yang menyatakan argumen utama
- Harus debatable (bukan fakta)
- Spesifik, bukan umum
- Memberi roadmap untuk esai
- Biasanya di akhir paragraf pembuka

## Struktur & Strategi
```
[Topik] + [Argumen/Klaim] + [Alasan/Roadmap]
```

Contoh struktur:
- "Although [counterargument], this essay argues that [klaim] because [alasan 1, 2, 3]."

## Contoh
1. Weak: "Social media affects mental health."
   Strong: "While social media fosters connection, its excessive use contributes to anxiety and depression among teenagers due to social comparison, sleep disruption, and reduced face-to-face interaction."

2. Weak: "This essay discusses climate change."
   Strong: "Climate change requires immediate action, and governments should prioritize renewable energy subsidies over carbon taxes to achieve both environmental and economic goals."

## Latihan
1. Evaluasi: "Pollution is bad for the environment." — Strong atau weak? Perbaiki.
2. Buat thesis statement tentang pendidikan online.
3. Identifikasi masalah: "This paper talks about technology."
4. Kembangkan thesis statement 3-prong tentang kebijakan imigrasi.
5. Tulis thesis statement yang menyertakan counterargument.
