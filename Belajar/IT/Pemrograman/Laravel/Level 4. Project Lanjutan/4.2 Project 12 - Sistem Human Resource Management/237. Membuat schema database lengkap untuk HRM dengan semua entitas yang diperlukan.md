# 237. Membuat Schema Database Lengkap untuk HRM dengan Semua Entitas yang Diperlukan

## Definisi & Target
Merancang ERD dan schema database untuk HRM yang mencakup employees, departments, positions, attendances, leaves, payrolls, dan tabel pendukung lainnya.

## Komponen yang Terlibat
- Tabel `departments`: id, name, code, description, parent_id, is_active, created_at, updated_at
- Tabel `positions`: id, department_id, name, level, salary_min, salary_max, created_at, updated_at
- Tabel `employees`: id, user_id, position_id, department_id, employee_id, name, email, phone, address, birth_date, gender, photo, join_date, status, salary, created_at, updated_at
- Tabel `attendances`: id, employee_id, clock_in, clock_out, date, status, work_hours, overtime_hours, notes, created_at, updated_at
- Tabel `leaves`: id, employee_id, type, start_date, end_date, reason, status, approved_by, approved_at, created_at, updated_at
- Tabel `payrolls`: id, employee_id, period, basic_salary, allowances, deductions, total, status, paid_at, created_at, updated_at

## Perintah Artisan CLI
- `php artisan make:model Department -m`
- `php artisan make:model Position -m`
- `php artisan make:model Employee -m`
- `php artisan make:model Attendance -m`
- `php artisan make:model Leave -m`
- `php artisan make:model Payroll -m`

- Verifikasi struktur dan foreign key
