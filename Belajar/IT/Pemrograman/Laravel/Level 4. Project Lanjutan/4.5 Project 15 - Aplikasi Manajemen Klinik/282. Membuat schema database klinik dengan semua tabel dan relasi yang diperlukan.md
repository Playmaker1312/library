# 282. Membuat Schema Database Klinik dengan Semua Tabel dan Relasi yang Diperlukan

## Definisi & Target
Merancang ERD dan schema database untuk aplikasi klinik dengan tabel doctors, patients, appointments, medical_records, prescriptions, prescription_items, invoices, dan queue.

## Komponen yang Terlibat
- Tabel `doctors`: id, user_id, name, specialist, license_number, phone, schedule, is_active, created_at
- Tabel `patients`: id, user_id, name, nik, phone, address, birth_date, gender, blood_type, created_at
- Tabel `appointments`: id, doctor_id, patient_id, appointment_date, time_slot, status, complaint, created_at
- Tabel `medical_records`: id, patient_id, doctor_id, appointment_id, anamnesis, diagnosis, therapy, notes, created_at
- Tabel `prescriptions`: id, medical_record_id, notes, created_at
- Tabel `prescription_items`: id, prescription_id, drug_name, dosage, quantity, notes, created_at
- Tabel `invoices`: id, patient_id, appointment_id, consultation_fee, action_fee, drug_fee, total, status, created_at
- Tabel `queue`: id, appointment_id, queue_number, status, called_at, created_at

## Perintah Artisan CLI
- `php artisan make:model Doctor -m`
- `php artisan make:model Patient -m`
- `php artisan make:model Appointment -m`
- `php artisan make:model MedicalRecord -m`
- `php artisan make:model Prescription -m`
- `php artisan make:model Invoice -m`
- `php artisan make:model Queue -m`
