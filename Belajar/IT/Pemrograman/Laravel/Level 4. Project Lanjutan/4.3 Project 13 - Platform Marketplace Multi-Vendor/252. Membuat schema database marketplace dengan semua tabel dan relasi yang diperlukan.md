# 252. Membuat Schema Database Marketplace dengan Semua Tabel dan Relasi yang Diperlukan

## Definisi & Target
Merancang ERD dan schema database marketplace multi-vendor dengan tabel stores, products, orders, order_items, commissions, withdrawals, reviews, dan disputes.

## Komponen yang Terlibat
- Tabel `stores`: id, user_id, name, slug, logo, description, address, status, created_at, updated_at
- Tabel `products`: id, store_id, category_id, name, slug, description, price, stock, weight, images, status, created_at, updated_at
- Tabel `orders`: id, user_id, order_number, status, subtotal, shipping_cost, discount, total, payment_status, created_at, updated_at
- Tabel `order_items`: id, order_id, store_id, product_id, quantity, price, subtotal, status, created_at, updated_at
- Tabel `commissions`: id, order_item_id, store_id, amount, rate, status, created_at, updated_at
- Tabel `withdrawals`: id, store_id, amount, bank_account, status, created_at, updated_at
- Tabel `reviews`: id, product_id, user_id, rating, comment, created_at, updated_at
- Tabel `disputes`: id, order_id, user_id, reason, status, resolution, created_at, updated_at

## Perintah Artisan CLI
- `php artisan make:model Store -m`
- `php artisan make:model Order -m`
- `php artisan make:model OrderItem -m`
- `php artisan make:model Commission -m`
- `php artisan make:model Withdrawal -m`
- `php artisan make:model Review -m`
- `php artisan make:model Dispute -m`
